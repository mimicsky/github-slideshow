# Tacticus Teams – How to Play Them

_Built 2026-09-23 from your roster + ability kits in `wiki.md`. Written like Overwatch roles: **Tank** makes space, **DPS** gets kills, **Support** keeps people alive or buffs._

**Rules that apply to every team**
- **Get first blood.** Most of your carries snowball on kills (Haarken, Archimatos, Macer).
- **Adjacency is everything.** Most buffs and heals only hit *adjacent* allies. Move in pairs, not alone.
- **Don't feed a lone unit.** One unit walking ahead = focus-fired. Advance as a blob.
- **Machine of War:** Biovore, if the mode allows it. Use it on turn 1–2 to drop mines in the enemy's path. Mines body-block and eat enemy actions.
- **Which team goes where:** check the lock icon/alliance shown before each battle. My assumption: Fall of Cadia = Chaos, Indomitus = Imperial, Octarius + Saim-Hann = Xenos, and Mirror campaigns use a different alliance than the original. Confirm in-game.

## 1. Chaos – main team (Fall of Cadia, Chaos Onslaught, Arena default)
| Slot | Character | Role | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Haarken** | DPS (hyper-carry) | Flies in, gets kills, snowballs |
| 2 | **Angrax** | Tank / zone | Deep Strikes in, punishes anyone who walks away |
| 3 | **Maladus** | Off-tank | Armour-shred aura, won't die (Resilient) |
| 4 | **Archimatos** | Ranged DPS / summoner | Psychic damage ignores armour, spawns daemons |
| 5 | **Rotbone** | Support (healer) | Heals + revives adjacent Chaos |
| Bench | Corrodius | Swap for Maladus vs psyker enemies (they hurt themselves) |

**Game plan**
1. **Turn 1 – set up.** Angrax deploys from Deep Strike. Put him 2 hexes from the enemy's squishy units (he can't land adjacent). Now any enemy that walks past or away from him eats a free hit. Maladus and Rotbone walk forward **together**.
2. **Turn 1–2 – first blood.** Haarken flies over terrain to the **lowest-HP enemy** and kills it. Every kill = more damage, every *character* kill = +1 hit. This is the whole team's win condition.
3. **Turn 2 – add bodies.** Archimatos uses **Daemonic Incursion** to drop Bloodletters on enemies. Enemies waste attacks on summons (like OW tank space). Finish low targets with his **ranged** attack for a Blue Horror, or melee for a Bloodletter.
4. **Turn 2+ – grind.** Maladus stands in the middle. His aura lowers enemy armour and grows each turn, so everyone hits harder the longer he lives.
5. **Always:** Rotbone ends every turn **adjacent to Haarken and/or Angrax**. His heal only works on adjacent Chaos allies, and his passive can revive an adjacent fallen ally.

**Ability timing**
- **Haarken – Herald of the Apocalypse:** when two enemies stand **in a line** (target + the one behind).
- **Angrax – Bringer of Despair:** when 2+ enemies are next to him.
- **Rotbone – Revitalising Malignancy:** when 2+ adjacent allies are hurt. Don't waste it on one chip.
- **Maladus – Flail Swing:** on a low-HP enemy that's next to other enemies (the kill spreads corruption).

**Mistakes to avoid:** Haarken diving into 3+ enemies alone. Rotbone standing next to nobody.

### Battle 61 (only 3 slots, enemy 8,304)
**Haarken + Angrax + Rotbone.** Haarken is the win condition (12,795 alone beats the enemy total). Angrax holds a flank. Rotbone stays glued to Haarken to heal and revive him. Do not bring Archimatos or Maladus here. Their value comes from a 5-slot grind.

## 2. Imperial (Indomitus, Imperial Onslaught)
| Slot | Character | Role | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Azrael** | Tank / anchor | Lion Helm: nearby allies block more |
| 2 | **Lucien** | DPS (diver) | Your biggest Imperial damage, free shot after every attack |
| 3 | **Tigurius** | DPS (AoE) + support | Hits up to 6 enemies; allies take less psychic damage |
| 4 | **Certus** | DPS (sniper) | Range 3; big damage if he doesn't move |
| 5 | **Bellator** | Flex / summoner | Flies anywhere; summons Inceptors late |
| Swap | Incisus | Support | Bring him instead of Bellator if you keep losing units |

**Game plan**
1. **Turn 1 – form up.** Azrael goes front-center. Tigurius and Certus go 1–2 hexes behind him (inside the Lion Helm buff). Certus should get to a spot he can **stay in** with long sight lines.
2. **Turn 1–2 – poke.** Certus fires every turn **without moving**, and uses **Mortis Round** on the biggest threat. Tigurius opens with **Storm of Wrath** when 3+ enemies are grouped.
3. **Turn 2–3 – dive.** Lucien goes in once enemies are weakened. His passive gives a free bolter shot after every attack. Rapid Assault means his first hit of a turn is stronger, so move and attack in the same turn.
4. **Turn 3+ – summon.** Bellator uses **Death From Above**. It summons more Inceptors the later you use it, so **turn 3+ is the minimum**.
5. **Finisher:** Lucien's **Black Rage** = huge charge, but then **the AI controls him for the rest of the fight.** Only use it to secure a win or kill a key enemy.

**Mistakes to avoid:** moving Certus before shooting. Using Black Rage turn 1. Using Bellator's active turn 1.

## 3. Xenos (Octarius, Saim-Hann, Xenos Onslaught)
**New recommendation.** It's stronger than the 4-Ork team because of the Makhotep combo below.

| Slot | Character | Role | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Imospekh** | DPS (Overwatch) | 6 hits melee and ranged; punishes enemies walking in |
| 2 | **Gulgortz** | Tank / summoner | Waaagh!: summons Boyz, all Orks faster + stronger |
| 3 | **Makhotep** | Support (engine) | Gives adjacent summons an **extra action** |
| 4 | **Thutmose** | DPS (AoE, flyer) | Direct damage to several enemies |
| 5 | **Aleph-Null** | Support (repair + summons) | Scarabs + repairs your Mechanical units |

**Why this works:** Makhotep's active gives adjacent **non-character** allies (summons) an immediate extra action with bonus damage. Gulgortz makes Ork Boyz and Aleph-Null makes Scarabs, so he has plenty of summons to fire. Almost your whole team is **Mechanical** (all 4 Necrons + Gulgortz), so Aleph-Null's repairs hit everyone, and the Necrons regenerate every turn.

**Game plan**
1. **Turn 1 – summons.** Gulgortz uses **Waaagh!** to summon Boyz and charge. Aleph-Null uses **Scarab Hive** next to the Boyz.
2. **Turn 1–2 – combo.** Move Makhotep so he's adjacent to as many summons as possible, then use **Adaptive Strategy**. Every summon attacks again.
3. **Always – Overwatch.** Put Imospekh where enemies must walk and **end his turn without attacking.** He fires on anyone entering range, and his passive makes Overwatch hits stronger and lets it continue after kills.
4. **Flank.** Thutmose flies to the side and uses **Harbinger of Destruction** when 2+ enemies are in range. His passive zaps someone every time he moves.
5. **Sustain.** Aleph-Null stays next to your most damaged Mechanical unit.

**If the campaign forces Orks only:** Gulgortz, Snappawrecka, Snotflogga, Gibbascrapz. Gibbascrapz must stay adjacent to Snappawrecka, because every repair gives Snappawrecka's Dakka more hits. Snotflogga's Grots taunt, so throw them on the enemy's hardest hitter.

## 4. Tournament Arena (Common cap)
| Slot | Character | Role | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Re'vas** | Tank / DPS | 6 hits both ways, drones + Overwatch |
| 2 | **Aethana** | DPS (diver) + support | Move 5, line strike, buffs nearby allies |
| 3 | **Archimatos** | DPS / summoner | Psychic ignores armour, daemons |
| 4 | **Aleph-Null** | Summoner / sustain | Scarabs + repairs Re'vas (Mechanical) |
| 5 | **Sibyll** | Support + burst | Adjacent allies get Infiltrate + damage reduction; big psychic nuke |
| Backup | Godswyl | Use if Uncommons aren't allowed. Stun + armour shred |

**Why kit wins here:** at a rarity cap, everyone's stats are close. Summons, taunts and free actions decide fights. This team has **three summoners** (Re'vas, Archimatos, Aleph-Null). The enemy runs out of actions before you run out of bodies.

**Game plan**
1. **Turn 1 – wall.** Re'vas uses **Early Warning Override** to summon 2 drones and go into Overwatch. Aleph-Null goes next to him and uses **Scarab Hive**. The enemy now has 4 extra targets to chew through.
2. **Turn 1 – buff.** Sibyll stands adjacent to Archimatos and Aethana. Nightshroud gives them Infiltrate (no Overwatch triggers) and damage reduction.
3. **Turn 2 – dive.** Aethana uses **Swooping Hawk** through up to 3 enemies in a line. Keep allies near her for Path of Command.
4. **Turn 2 – nuke.** Sibyll uses **Psychic Maelstrom** on the highest-value target. Archimatos uses **Daemonic Incursion**.
5. Aleph-Null repairs Re'vas every turn.

## 5. Arena / PvP (normal)
**Default: the Chaos team (#1).** It's your strongest and most synergistic team. Haarken alone outclasses most things at your level.
- On defense, the AI plays your team. Chaos works well there because Angrax's passive and Rotbone's revives don't need smart play.
- If enemies are **psyker-heavy**, swap **Maladus → Corrodius** (enemy psykers hurt themselves).
- If Arena lets you mix alliances, **Imospekh** is the best non-Chaos add (Overwatch + 6 hits). Swap him in for Maladus. Rotbone won't heal him, though, because Imospekh isn't Chaos.

## 6. Raids (Celestine node raids)
Use the **Chaos team** unless the raid locks your alliance. Raids are pure damage checks: Haarken, Archimatos and Maladus (armour shred) matter most. Rotbone stays only if you're losing units.
