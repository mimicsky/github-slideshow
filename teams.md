# Tacticus Teams – How to Play Them

_Built 2026-09-23 from your roster + ability kits in `wiki.md`. Written like Overwatch roles: **Tank** makes space, **DPS** gets kills, **Support** keeps people alive or buffs._

**Rules that apply to every team**
- **Get first blood.** Most of your carries snowball on kills (Haarken, Archimatos, Macer).
- **Adjacency is everything.** Most buffs and heals only hit *adjacent* allies. Move in pairs, not alone.
- **Don't feed a lone unit.** One unit walking ahead = focus-fired. Advance as a blob.
- **Machine of War:** Biovore, if the mode allows it. Use it on turn 1–2 to drop mines in the enemy's path. Mines body-block and eat enemy actions.
- **Campaign locks (confirmed by Alex):** Indomitus = Imperial · Indomitus Mirror = **Necrons only** · Fall of Cadia = Chaos · Octarius = **Orks only** · Saim-Hann = **Aeldari only**.

## 1. Chaos – main team (Fall of Cadia, Chaos Onslaught → Xenos badges)
| Slot | Character | Role | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Haarken** | DPS (hyper-carry) | Flies in, gets kills, snowballs |
| 2 | **Angrax** | Tank / zone | Deep Strikes in, sticks to enemies; anyone who leaves his side gets hit |
| 3 | **Maladus** | Off-tank | Armour-shred aura, won't die (Resilient) |
| 4 | **Archimatos** | Ranged DPS / summoner | Psychic damage ignores armour, spawns daemons |
| 5 | **Rotbone** | Support (healer) | Heals + revives adjacent Chaos |
| Bench | Corrodius | Swap for Maladus vs psyker enemies (they hurt themselves) |

**Game plan**
1. **Turn 1 – set up.** Angrax deploys from Deep Strike. He can't land adjacent to enemies, so put him **1 hex away** from the enemy's ranged/squishy units. Maladus and Rotbone walk forward **together**.
2. **Turn 2 – stick.** Angrax walks **into contact** with those units. His passive only fires when an enemy that is **already adjacent moves away**. Now they're stuck: stay and trade melee with a Terminator, or leave and take a free power hit. Ranged units hate this because they want to back off to shoot. Walking past him does **not** trigger it.
3. **Turn 1–2 – first blood.** Haarken flies over terrain to the **lowest-HP enemy** and kills it. Every kill = more damage, every *character* kill = +1 hit. This is the whole team's win condition.
4. **Turn 2 – add bodies.** Archimatos uses **Daemonic Incursion** (range 2). It keeps summoning Bloodletters (**up to 6**) as long as the target is **alive, in range, and has free hexes around it**. So aim it at a **tanky enemy standing in the open**, not a low-HP one: killing the target stops the summons. Enemies waste attacks on summons (like OW tank space). Finish low targets with his **ranged** attack for a Blue Horror, or melee for a Bloodletter.
5. **Turn 2+ – grind.** Maladus stands in the middle. His aura lowers enemy armour and grows each turn, so everyone hits harder the longer he lives.
6. **Always:** Rotbone ends every turn **adjacent to Haarken and/or Angrax**. His heal only works on adjacent Chaos allies, and his passive can revive an adjacent fallen ally.

**Ability timing**
- **Haarken – Herald of the Apocalypse:** when two enemies stand **in a line** (target + the one behind).
- **Angrax – Bringer of Despair:** when 2+ enemies are next to him.
- **Rotbone – Revitalising Malignancy:** when 2+ adjacent allies are hurt. Don't waste it on one chip.
- **Maladus – Flail Swing:** on a low-HP enemy that's next to other enemies (the kill spreads corruption).

**Mistakes to avoid:** Haarken diving into 3+ enemies alone. Rotbone standing next to nobody.

### Battle 61 (only 3 slots, enemy 8,304)
**Haarken + Angrax + Rotbone.** Haarken is the win condition (12,795 alone beats the enemy total). Angrax holds a flank. Rotbone stays glued to Haarken to heal and revive him. Do not bring Archimatos or Maladus here. Their value comes from a 5-slot grind.

## 2. Imperial (Indomitus, Imperial Onslaught → **Chaos badges for Angrax**)
| Slot | Character | Role | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Azrael** | Tank / anchor | Lion Helm: nearby allies block more |
| 2 | **Lucien** | DPS (diver) | Your biggest Imperial damage, free shot after every attack |
| 3 | **Tigurius** | DPS (AoE) + support | Hits up to 6 enemies; allies take less psychic damage |
| 4 | **Certus** | DPS (sniper) | Range 3; big damage if he doesn't move |
| 5 | **Bellator** | Flex / summoner | Flies anywhere; summons Inceptors late |
| Swap | Incisus | Support | Bring him instead of Bellator if you keep losing units |

**Game plan**
1. **Turn 1 – form up.** Azrael goes front-center. Tigurius and Certus go 1–2 hexes behind him (inside the Lion Helm buff). Certus should get to a spot he can **stay in** with long sight lines.
2. **Turn 1–2 – poke.** Certus fires every turn **without moving**, and uses **Mortis Round** on the biggest threat. Tigurius opens with **Storm of Wrath** when 3+ enemies are grouped.
3. **Turn 2–3 – dive.** Lucien goes in once enemies are weakened. His passive gives a free bolter shot after every attack. Rapid Assault means his first hit of a turn is stronger, so move and attack in the same turn.
4. **Turn 3+ – summon.** Bellator uses **Death From Above**. It summons more Inceptors the later you use it, so **turn 3+ is the minimum**.
5. **Finisher:** Lucien's **Black Rage** = huge charge, but then **the AI controls him for the rest of the fight.** Only use it to secure a win or kill a key enemy.

**Mistakes to avoid:** moving Certus before shooting. Using Black Rage turn 1. Using Bellator's active turn 1.

## 3a. Necrons – Indomitus Mirror (Necrons only)
You own 4 Necrons, so this is a 4-unit team.

| Slot | Character | Role | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Imospekh** | DPS (Overwatch) | 6 hits melee and ranged; punishes anyone who walks in |
| 2 | **Thutmose** | DPS (AoE, flyer) | Direct damage to several enemies; zaps someone every time he moves |
| 3 | **Makhotep** | Support (engine) | Gives adjacent summons an extra action; +1 move to neighbours |
| 4 | **Aleph-Null** | Support (summons + repair) | Scarabs + repairs your Mechanical units |

**Why it works:** all 4 are Mechanical with Living Metal, so they **regenerate every turn** and Aleph-Null repairs all of them. The team wins long fights by out-sustaining the enemy.

**Game plan**
1. **Turn 1 – summons.** Aleph-Null uses **Scarab Hive** (2 swarms). Makhotep moves **next to the scarabs**.
2. **Turn 1–2 – combo.** Makhotep uses **Adaptive Strategy**, and each adjacent scarab gets an extra attack with bonus damage. Makhotep only boosts **non-character** allies, so the scarabs are what make him worth a slot.
3. **Always – Overwatch.** Put Imospekh on the path enemies must take and **end his turn without attacking.** He fires on anyone entering range, and his passive makes Overwatch hits stronger and lets it continue after kills.
4. **Flank.** Thutmose flies to the side and uses **Harbinger of Destruction** when 2+ enemies are in range.
5. **Sustain.** Aleph-Null ends each turn next to your most damaged unit.

**Upgrade note:** 🔥 **Anuphet (141/250) moves up.** He'd be your 5th Necron, and his active summons one Necron Warrior per round passed, which is more summons for Makhotep to fire. Reanimator (MoW, 10/40) also revives Mechanical allies.

## 3b. Orks – Octarius (Orks only)
| Slot | Character | Role | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Gulgortz** | Tank / leader | Waaagh!: summons Boyz, all Orks faster + stronger |
| 2 | **Snappawrecka** | DPS (ranged) | Normal attacks **ignore armour**; Dakka gains hits per repair |
| 3 | **Snotflogga** | Summoner / taunt | Grots that attack + taunt |
| 4 | **Gibbascrapz** | Support (mechanic) | Repairs walkers, armour aura, summons a Grot Tank |

**Game plan**
1. **Turn 1 – Waaagh!** Gulgortz summons Boyz, gives all Orks move + melee damage, and charges. Get Get Stuck In bonus hits by keeping Orks **next to each other**.
2. **Turn 1 – taunt.** Snotflogga uses **Get 'Em Runtz** on the enemy's hardest hitter. The Grots taunt it, so it wastes its turn on them.
3. **Every turn – repair.** Gibbascrapz stays **adjacent to Snappawrecka** and repairs him. Each repair adds hits to Snappawrecka's **Dakka Dakka Dakka**, so save that active until he's been repaired 2–3 times.
4. Gibbascrapz uses **Grot Tank** early for another body. His Kustom Force Field gives nearby allies armour, so he belongs in the middle of the pack.

## 3c. Aeldari – Saim-Hann (Aeldari only)
You own 3 Aeldari. Thin team, but the kits fit together.

| Slot | Character | Role | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Aethana** | DPS (diver) + support | Move 5, line strike through 3 enemies; buffs nearby allies |
| 2 | **Eldryon** | DPS (amplifier) | Doom: marked enemies take more damage, extra from Aeldari |
| 3 | **Calandis** | DPS (sniper) | Range 3, Overwatch, hit-and-run |

**Game plan**
1. **Turn 1 – Doom.** Eldryon's passive marks enemies at an exact range. Focus those enemies. Everyone hits them harder.
2. **Turn 1 – Executioner.** Eldryon uses **Executioner** early on the biggest threat. It grows **every round**, so earlier = more total damage.
3. **Poke.** Calandis uses **Fire and Reposition** to move, shoot, move back out of reach. Her Wireweave Net punishes the first melee attacker each enemy turn.
4. **Dive.** Aethana uses **Swooping Hawk** when 3 enemies line up, then stays near the other two for Path of Command.

**Upgrade note:** Calandis is 1/3 of this team, so her ascension (10 uncommon Xenos orbs) matters more now.

## 4. Tournament Arena (Common cap)
| Slot | Character | Role | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Re'vas** | Tank / DPS | 6 hits both ways, drones + Overwatch |
| 2 | **Aethana** | DPS (diver) + support | Move 5, line strike, buffs nearby allies |
| 3 | **Archimatos** | DPS / summoner | Psychic ignores armour, daemons |
| 4 | **Imospekh** | DPS (Overwatch) | 6 hits melee + ranged, Overwatch, regen. (Replaced Aleph-Null: Scarab Hive only fires as an attack on an enemy, low value.) |
| 5 | **Sibyll** | Support + burst | Adjacent allies get Infiltrate + damage reduction; big psychic nuke |
| Backup | Godswyl | Use if Uncommons aren't allowed. Stun + armour shred |

**Proven combo (2026-09-24):** Re'vas **Early Warning Override** + Imospekh **Overwatch** side by side = double Overwatch wall; enemies walking in get shredded by 12+ hits. **Vs Kariyan:** hit him at range (Parry only reduces melee), keep Re'vas (Big Target) away from him, don't feed him summons, overkill past his Resilient 1 HP.

**Counters seen (2026-09-24):**
- **Volk / Morvenn Vahl** (range 3) outrange your Overwatch (range 2) – they never walk in. Morvenn's missile blasts target + adjacent, punishing a clumped wall. → **Spread out** and **dive** them: Aethana (move 5, flying) onto Volk; Archimatos psychic at range 2.
- **Khârn** heals his momentum on kills (bonus attack + move after each kill, less psychic damage per melee kill). Bloodletters = free kills for him. → **No summons near Khârn**; focus him at range before his first kill; Godswyl stun if you bring him.

**Blind pick – you can't see the enemy before locking in.** So run one balanced team:
**Archimatos · Sibyll · Re'vas · Thutmose · Aethana**
- vs **walk-in melee:** Re'vas Overwatch + drones.
- vs **long range:** Aethana dives (move 5, flying), Thutmose flies too.
- vs **tanks:** Archimatos + Sibyll psychic and Thutmose direct damage all **ignore armour** (armour is subtracted per hit, so many small hits like Imospekh's fall off vs tanks).
- vs **Khârn/Kariyan:** hold Archimatos's summons, focus them at range.
- Imospekh = swap back in if you're losing mostly to melee rushes.

**Why kit wins here:** at a rarity cap, everyone's stats are close. Summons, taunts and free actions decide fights. This team has **three summoners** (Re'vas, Archimatos, Aleph-Null). The enemy runs out of actions before you run out of bodies.

**Game plan**
1. **Turn 1 – wall.** Re'vas uses **Early Warning Override** to summon 2 drones and go into Overwatch. Imospekh sets up on **Overwatch** covering the approach (end his turn without attacking).
2. **Turn 1 – buff.** Sibyll stands adjacent to Archimatos and Aethana. Nightshroud gives them Infiltrate (no Overwatch triggers) and damage reduction.
3. **Turn 2 – dive.** Aethana uses **Swooping Hawk** through up to 3 enemies in a line. Keep allies near her for Path of Command.
4. **Turn 2 – nuke.** Sibyll uses **Psychic Maelstrom** on the highest-value target. Archimatos uses **Daemonic Incursion**.
5. Imospekh stays on Overwatch until enemies are in range, then unloads 6 hits.

## 5. PvP (no faction lock – your top 5)
**Fact:** PvP and Arena are **not faction locked**. PvP uses your top 5.

| Slot | Character | Power | Role | Job |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Haarken** | 12,795 | DPS (hyper-carry) | Flies in, first kill, snowballs |
| 2 | **Lucien** | 6,420 | DPS (diver) | Second dive; free bolter shot after every attack |
| 3 | **Azrael** | 4,596 | Tank / anchor | Lion Helm: nearby allies block more |
| 4 | **Tigurius** | 4,533 | AoE + support | Hits up to 6 enemies; allies take less psychic damage |
| 5 | **Imospekh** | 2,839 | DPS (Overwatch) | Punishes anyone who walks in; regenerates |

**Weak spot:** no healer. Rotbone only heals Chaos, and Incisus is too low power to replace anyone here. Just win fast.

**Game plan**
1. **Turn 1 – hold.** Azrael front-center. Tigurius 1 hex behind him (inside Lion Helm). Imospekh on the path the enemy must take, **ending his turn without attacking** so he's on Overwatch.
2. **Turn 1–2 – first blood.** Haarken flies to the **lowest-HP enemy** and kills it. Every kill makes him stronger.
3. **Turn 2 – follow-up.** Lucien dives the next weakest target. Move + attack in the same turn for the Rapid Assault bonus.
4. **When 3+ enemies group:** Tigurius uses **Storm of Wrath**. Azrael uses **Dark Talon Strike** on a target with enemies behind it in a line.
5. **Finisher only:** Lucien's **Black Rage** (the AI controls him after).

## 6. Arena (level/rarity capped – any faction)
**How the cap works:** your characters get **bumped down** to the arena's cap. A capped Haarken is no stronger than anyone else's capped character, so **kit matters more than investment**. Characters already below the cap stay where they are.

**Rule:**
- **High arena tiers** (cap near your top units' level): use the **PvP team (#5)**.
- **Low arena tiers** (everyone's stats flattened): use the **kit team (#4)**: Re'vas, Aethana, Archimatos, Aleph-Null, Sibyll. Three summoners win flattened fights because the enemy runs out of actions first.
- **In between:** keep **Haarken** (Flying + snowball kit is great at any level) and **Imospekh** (6 hits both ways), and fill the rest from the kit team.

## 7. Raids (Celestine node raids)
No team needed. Raiding a 3★ campaign battle is an **instant sweep**: it just spends energy and rolls the drop. The team only matters the first time you clear the battle.
