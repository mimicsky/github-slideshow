# Restaurant Inventory

A living record of everything in stock — proteins, produce, pantry goods,
seasonings, sauces, dairy, and the bar. Kept up to date from your phone.
This is the source of truth used to suggest specials, new menu items, and
seasoning blends based only on what's actually on hand.

**How this gets used:**
1. You tell me what's in stock (add, remove, or update quantities) and I keep
   this file current.
2. When you want ideas for a special, new dish, or a seasoning blend, I read
   this file and suggest options built only from what's listed here.
3. Stock levels help flag what to use up soon vs. what's low and needs
   reordering.
