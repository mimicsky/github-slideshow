## Patterns
- **Machine of War missions** pay shards + badges + MoW parts for just *using* them. In fights where Biovore isn't needed (easy Imperial fights), rotate Galatian → Storm Speeder → Exorcist.
- **Mirror "Beat Battle 1"** steps: 3 missions each have one. Worth a try – Battle 1 should be easy if the campaign is open.
- **Rank missions (Bronze I–III)** are the long pole. Ranks = upgrade slots, so they come from farming upgrade items with energy.
- **Ability 15+/17+ missions for Orks, Aeldari and Necrons** all need **Xenos badges**, which come from the **Chaos Onslaught track**. Those three missions compete for the same badges.
