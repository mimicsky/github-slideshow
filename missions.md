## Patterns
- **Rank missions (Bronze I–III)** are the long pole. Ranks = upgrade slots, so they come from farming upgrade items with energy.
- **Ability 15+/17+ missions for Orks, Aeldari and Necrons** all need **Xenos badges**, which come from the **Chaos Onslaught track**. Those three missions compete for the same badges.
