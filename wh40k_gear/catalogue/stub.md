# Solid-shot and special guns (autoguns, shotguns, pistols, web, needle)

Item rules for this category. General rules (heat, hot-shot, drop-prone, going Down, ammo crates) are in DND_CODEX Part 8, **Gear rules**.

**Every gun in this file has [Precision Sacrifice](abilities.md#precision-sacrifice)** (give up shots from a string for +1 each on one shot, up to +3; 3-beat cooldown).

## Infantry autogun, 'Columnus' pattern

**Infantry autogun, 'Columnus' pattern** (common, 1.25 TV; Munitorum, black market,
Free Traders). Mid-length rifle, dark parkerised steel, folding wire stock with a
rubber butt-cap, short ribbed handguard with a top rail, curved 30-round magazine,
square-hooded front sight and flip-up rear, olive canvas sling. Select fire:
single (+1 on aimed shots past 30 m), burst (a 3-shot chain where a miss gives no
-1), or auto (a chain of up to 6, no hit bonuses). Autogun punch.

## Braced autogun, 'Graia' pattern

**Braced autogun, 'Graia' pattern** (uncommon, 2.5 TV; Munitorum, black market,
Free Traders). Long and heavy: hardwood stock with a deep cheek-rest, perforated
heat shroud over a thick barrel, folding bipod, 60-round box magazine, carry handle
as a sight base, brass ejection deflector, heat-blued barrel. Full auto only, chains
of up to 8. Braced on cover or its bipod it suppresses (the target takes -2 on its
shots; stacks with a heavy's suppression); from the hip, -2. Heat: each chain of 6+
adds 1; at 3 it must vent a beat; firing anyway at 3, d20 1-5 jams it (a beat to
clear). Autogun punch, but each 3 hits in one chain count as one solid hit against
carapace.

## Vigilante autogun, 'Kasr Warden' pattern

**Vigilante autogun, 'Kasr Warden' pattern** (player design, uncommon, 2 TV;
Munitorum, black market, Free Traders). Light L-shaped skeleton stock in bare steel,
slim urban-grey receiver, top rail over built-in iron sights, straight 20-round
magazine, railed handguard, vented barrel cover, threaded muzzle (thread-cap when
bare). Semi-auto: chains of up to 4 single shots, normal hit bonuses. Light
trigger: +1 on the first shot of every string, and it is drop-prone. Vented: never
builds heat, and the handguard stays cool to hold. Muzzle takes a silencer or heavy
suppressor, or a muzzle brake (kit.md, Muzzles). Autogun punch.

## Headhunter autogun, Agripaa pattern

**Headhunter autogun, Agripaa pattern** (Darktide; uncommon, 3 TV; Munitorum,

Autogun punch.

## Shredder autopistol, Ius pattern

**Shredder autopistol, Ius pattern** (Darktide; uncommon, 1.5 TV; Munitorum, black

Shot chains of up to 8 with no hit bonuses (misses still -1). Autopistol punch:

## Twin-barrel autopistol, 'Kasr Hydra' pattern

**Twin-barrel autopistol, 'Kasr Hydra' pattern** (player design, Ash on Ostrava day 45; uncommon, ~3 TV; black
market, Free Traders). Two stub barrels stacked under one slide, fed from a **40-round twin-column magazine**,
firing **twice as fast as an autopistol**, with an **ergonomic moulded grip**. Both barrels eject through **one
port**, which is its weak point.
- **Twice the rate:** strings of up to **16**, no hit bonuses in the string (misses still -1); **recoil 1.**
- **Stub rounds:** good on flesh; **through flak on a solid hit**; carapace stops them.
- **Ergonomic grip:** **+1 on the quick-draw.**
- **Runs hot:** **heat 1 per 8 shots** in a fight. At **3 heat** it must cool for a beat; firing anyway, d20 **1-5**
  the slide seizes and the grip scalds (the shooter drops a step, the pistol is out of the fight). Heat clears
  after cooling or when the fight ends.
- **One ejection port:** any shot die of **1-2** is a **jam** instead of the shot (a beat to clear).
- A magazine lasts about two and a half full strings; a fresh one takes half a beat.

## Machine pistol, 'Kasr Hornet' pattern

**Machine pistol, 'Kasr Hornet' pattern** (player design, uncommon, 2.5 TV; black
market, Free Traders). Fires autogun rounds from an extended 40-round magazine in a
purpose-made magwell. Moulded plastek grip with ergonomic grooves, side-ejecting
slide, a short barrel that takes no muzzle attachment. **Extremely fast:** shot
chains of up to 10, with no hit bonuses in the chain (misses still -1). Autogun
punch. **Hair trigger:** +1 on the first shot of every string (a single shot counts); drop-prone.
**Malfunctions (heat of continuous fire):** the jam risk lives on the shot dice,
with no extra rolls. Rounds are counted only while firing continuously, beat after
beat; stopping for one beat resets the count (reloading doesn't matter). A chain is
at most 10, so the risk starts when the shooter keeps firing into the next beat:
from the 13th continuous round on, any shot die of **1-4** is a malfunction instead
of the shot. A natural 1 on any shot is always a malfunction. Malfunction, d6: 1-2 jam (a beat to clear);
3 misfire (that chain ends, no hit); 4 double feed (two beats to clear); 5 runaway
(it fires the rest of the magazine uncontrolled, as a dropped dump, below); 6 a part
cracks (out of the fight until repaired). **Dropped:** on the drop-prone table's
"fires" result it dumps the whole magazine, spraying the area: roll the
where-it-goes d20 once for every 10 rounds left (at least once).

## Submachine gun, 'Kasr Locust' pattern

**Submachine gun, 'Kasr Locust' pattern** (player design, Ash on Ostrava day 45; uncommon, ~3 TV; Munitorum,
black market, Free Traders). The Hornet's bigger sister (named for a swarm): a compact stub SMG with a **30-round curved
magazine**, a **select-fire switch**, a front grip and a **folding stock**. Stub rounds: good on flesh,
**through flak on a solid hit**, carapace stops them.
- **Full auto:** strings of up to **8**, no hit bonuses in the string (misses still -1); **recoil 1.**
- **Burst:** strings of up to **4 bursts**; each burst that hits **lands as two rounds** (through flak on any
  hit, carapace on a solid hit); recoil 1.
- **Semi:** single aimed shots, **no recoil**, and **+1 with the stock out**.
- **Folding stock:** folded, it fits a holster and draws like a pistol (-1 quick-draw); out, it shoulders
  like a carbine.
- A magazine lasts about **three full strings**; a fresh one takes half a beat.

## Stamped submachine gun, 'Munitorum Mk I' pattern

**Stamped submachine gun, 'Munitorum Mk I' pattern** (player design, Ash on Ostrava day 45; common, ~1 TV;
Munitorum mass issue, black market). Made by the million in forge-world manufactories with only the parts it
needs: a stamped tube receiver, a **barrel that screws onto the front** (threaded at the muzzle), a
**30-round stub magazine** that feeds straight into the receiver, a **wire stock** that folds flat along it, and
a **skeletal grip** that looks like scaffolding with the panels left off. It looks like it would come apart in
your hands, and it does, on purpose.
- **Safe and auto, nothing else:** strings of up to **8**, no hit bonuses in the string (misses still -1);
  **recoil 1.** No aimed single shots (a single short pull is just a string of one).
- **Stub rounds:** good on flesh; **through flak on a solid hit**; carapace stops them.
- **Wire stock:** folded, it draws like a big pistol (-1 quick-draw) and takes **-1 past ~15 m**; out, it
  shoulders fine.
- **Threaded muzzle:** takes any muzzle attachment (a silencer, a flash hider).
- **Simple:** strips and cleans in **~15 minutes**; anyone can fix it, and **two broken ones make one working
  one.** A magazine lasts about three full strings; a fresh one takes half a beat.

## Silenced submachine gun, 'Kasr Whisper' pattern

**Silenced submachine gun, 'Kasr Whisper' pattern** (Ash on Ostrava day 45; uncommon, ~4 TV; black market, Free

## Riot SMG, Arbites pattern

**Riot SMG, Arbites pattern** (Ash on Ostrava day 45; rare, ~6 TV; Arbites issue, black market). A drum-fed SMG
for taking people alive: a **50-round drum of shock rounds** (or standard stub drums).
- Strings of up to **6**; **recoil 1.**
- **Shock rounds:** a hit **doesn't wound**; it forces a **CON save against 13, or the target is stunned and loses
  their next beat.** Flak doesn't help; **carapace gives +2 on the save**; power armour ignores them.
- With a standard drum it's an ordinary stub SMG (through flak on a solid hit).

## Rotary SMG, 'Hive Spinner' pattern

**Rotary SMG, 'Hive Spinner' pattern** (Ash on Ostrava day 45; uncommon, ~3 TV; black market). A ganger's mini
rotary: **three barrels that spin** on a little motor, fed from a **60-round drum**.
- **Spin-up:** the first beat it only spins (no shots); after that, while you keep it spinning, strings of up to
  **12**, no hit bonuses in the string; **recoil 1.**
- **Loud:** the whine carries across a hab block.
- Stub rounds: **through flak on a solid hit.** Natural 1: it jams (a beat to clear).

## Boarding SMG, Navy 'Gyre' pattern

**Boarding SMG, Navy 'Gyre' pattern** (Ash on Ostrava day 45; uncommon, ~5 TV; Navy stores, Free Traders). A void

## Needle SMG, Assassinorum pattern

**Needle SMG, Assassinorum pattern** (Ash on Ostrava day 45; legendary, ~40 TV; Assassinorum, by arrangement only).

## Bullpup SMG, 'Kasr Adder' pattern

**Bullpup SMG, 'Kasr Adder' pattern** (player design, Ash on Ostrava day 45; uncommon, ~4 TV; Munitorum, Free

## Heavy SMG, 'Orlock' pattern

**Heavy SMG, 'Orlock' pattern** (player design, Ash on Ostrava day 45; uncommon, ~3 TV; black market, Munitorum,
Free Traders). Named for Necromunda's House of Iron, whose gangers swear by guns like it. An old-world SMG: the

- Strings of up to **8**, no hit bonuses in the string (misses still -1); **recoil 1.**

- **Never jams:** like the Kasr Frontier, it ignores gun condition.
- **No attachment slots at all:** it is what it is.
- **Heavy and steady:** the wooden stock makes single aimed shots **+1** when it's shouldered.
- A magazine is about five strings; a fresh one takes half a beat.

## Auto-revolver, 'Hammerfall' pattern

**Auto-revolver, 'Hammerfall' pattern** (player design, uncommon; stub 2 TV, long
2.5 TV). Full-metal frame, built-in iron sights, an 8-round cylinder of autogun
rounds (autogun punch: through carapace only on a solid hit). Holding the trigger

## Phosphor blast pistol (Mechanicus)

**Phosphor blast pistol** (rare, ~14 TV; Mechanicus, Free Traders; player request, Ash on Ostrava day 45). A
heavy Skitarii sidearm built like a revolver: a **6-chamber cylinder of phosphor cells**, a vented barrel and
Mechanicus sigils on the frame. Each shot is a spitting white-green bolt of burning phosphor.
- **Strings of up to 3; recoil 1** (as a revolver).
- **Phosphor:** **through flak on any hit; through carapace on a solid hit.**
- **Lit up:** a hit leaves the target **glowing with phosphor** until the end of their next beat: **+1 to every

- **Burns:** a **solid hit sets them alight** (they spend their next beat putting it out, or drop a step).
- **Bright:** every shot lights up the shooter too; they can't stay hidden after firing.
- **Reload:** a fresh cylinder takes a beat. **Phosphor cylinders ~0.5 TV each** (Mechanicus).

## Lever-action stub rifle, 'Kasr Frontier' pattern

**Lever-action stub rifle, 'Kasr Frontier' pattern** (player design, Ash on Ostrava
session 9; uncommon, 2.5 TV, changed with the player, day 36 cleanup; Munitorum, black
market, Free Traders). A classic lever
gun with a **12-round tube**; **never jams**. **Single lever:** chains of up to 6,
normal stub-rifle shots. **Double lever:** work the lever twice to stack two rounds,
and the shot lands **as two rounds** (through flak on any hit, through carapace on a
solid hit); chains of up to 3.
