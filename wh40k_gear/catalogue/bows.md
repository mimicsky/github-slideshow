# Bows, quivers and arrows

Item rules for this category (the player's designs, Ash on Ostrava day 47). General rules (going Down, ammo crates) are
in DND_CODEX Part 8, **Gear rules**.

**How bows work:** **the arrow decides the punch; the bow throws it.** Bows are **silent** (a shot doesn't give the
archer away), have **no recoil**, and **STR 50+ drives the arrow one armour step further** (the archer's arm powers it,
as a thrown weapon: codex Part 8, Strength behind the blow). **Arrows are carried in a [quiver](#quiver)**; drawing from
it is free. **After a fight, each unbroken arrow is found on a d6 4-6.** Bows have **no attachment slots**; on the
[attachment sheet](attachments.md#the-rollable-list) only arrows roll.

## Recurve bow, 'Reedhawk' pattern

**Recurve bow, 'Reedhawk' pattern** (common, 0.5 TV, DM's price; feral-world traders, poachers' markets, black market).
A light, fast bow. **Strings of 2** (nock and draw fast). **-2 past ~40 m.** Silent, no recoil, STR 50+ counts (above).
Feral worlders, poachers and quiet hunters carry it.

## Compound bow, 'Kasr Talon' pattern

**Compound bow, 'Kasr Talon' pattern** (uncommon, 2 TV, DM's price; Free Traders, hunters' outfitters, black market).
Pulleys and cams: more power and accuracy, less speed. **Needs STR 30+ to draw** (the player, day 47): below that he
can't use it. **Strings of 1.** **Hits harder: the arrow goes one armour step further** (stacks with STR 50+). **+1 on
aimed shots**; **-2 past ~60 m.** Silent, no recoil, no attachment slots.

## Quiver

**Quiver** (common, 0.25 TV; leatherworkers, feral-world traders, Free Traders). Holds **20 arrows**, any mix of
types; drawing from it is free (it's what the bow's string draws from). Worn on the back or the hip. Without one, an
archer holds a few arrows in his bow hand: **3 at most**.
