# Bows, quivers and arrows

Item rules for this category (the player's designs, Ash on Ostrava day 47). General rules (going Down, ammo crates) are
in DND_CODEX Part 8, **Gear rules**.

**How bows work:** **the arrow decides the punch; the bow throws it.** Bows are **silent** (a shot doesn't give the
archer away), have **no recoil**, and **STR 50+ drives the arrow one armour step further** (the archer's arm powers it,
as a thrown weapon: codex Part 8, Strength behind the blow). **Arrows are carried in a [quiver](#quiver)**; drawing from
it is free. **After a fight, each unbroken arrow is found on a d6 4-6.** **Bows and crossbows have three slots: string, sight and tracker** ([Bow parts](#bow-parts-day-48); day 48: before
it they had none); arrows roll in the load slot ([attachment sheet](attachments.md#the-rollable-list)).

## Recurve bow, 'Reedhawk' pattern

**Recurve bow, 'Reedhawk' pattern** (common, 0.5 TV, DM's price; feral-world traders, poachers' markets, black market).
A light, fast bow. **Strings of 2** (nock and draw fast). **-2 past ~40 m.** Silent, no recoil, STR 50+ counts (above).
Feral worlders, poachers and quiet hunters carry it.

## Compound bow, 'Hunter Kestrel' pattern

**Compound bow, 'Hunter Kestrel' pattern** (uncommon, 2 TV, DM's price; Free Traders, hunters' outfitters, black market).
Pulleys and cams: more power and accuracy, less speed. **Needs STR 30+ to draw** (the player, day 47): below that he
can't use it. **Strings of 1.** **Hits harder: the arrow goes one armour step further** (stacks with STR 50+). **+1 on
aimed shots**; **-2 past ~60 m.** Silent, no recoil, no attachment slots.

## Stake crossbow, 'Condemnor' pattern

**Stake crossbow, 'Condemnor' pattern** (rare, 10 TV; Ecclesiarchy, Free Traders; the player's pick, Ash on Ostrava day 48, after Darktide's Sisters
Novitiate and their Condemnor stake thrower; in the lore the Condemnor is a witch-hunter's weapon whose stakes carry
psychic-disrupting cores). A compact crossbow firing blessed, silver-cored stakes. It has its own stakes, not arrows.
- **One stake a beat; re-spanning it is the short move.** **Silent,** no recoil. **6 stakes** in its quiver; picked up
  after a fight as arrows are (a d6 4-6 each).
- **Punch:** through flak and reinforced flak; **carapace only on a solid hit.**
- **Witch-killer:** **a hit on a psyker drops an extra step, and he can't use powers next beat.**
- **Pins them:** **a solid hit within ~5 m of a wall or the ground pins the target:** he can't move until he spends a
  beat pulling free.
- **Slots: string and sight only** (it fires its own stakes: no arrows, no tracker).
- **Who:** Sisters Novitiate (their kit list); rarely zealots (witch-hunters carry them).

## Crossbow, 'Hunter Windlass' pattern

**Crossbow, 'Hunter Windlass' pattern** (uncommon, 2.5 TV; hunters' outfitters, black market, Free Traders; the player's pick, Ash on Ostrava day
48; numbers the DM's). A stock with a steel prod across the front, a trigger and a re-spanning lever.
- **One bolt, then re-span with the lever as the short move:** it fires every beat. **Silent,** no recoil. It can be
  carried spanned and loaded, ready for the first shot.
- **Bolts are arrows:** the same types and the same table ([Arrows](#arrows)), in the same quiver. **The steel prod
  pushes a bolt one armour step further** (as the compound bow); STR 50+ doesn't add (the prod does the work, not the
  arm).
- **Steady:** the stock and trigger give **+1 on aimed shots**; **-2 past ~60 m.**
- **Slots as a bow:** string, sight and tracker ([Bow parts](#bow-parts-day-48)); bolts roll as arrows.
- **Who:** commoners (hunters), cultists, raiders; trackers; assassins and infiltrators (it's quiet).

## Bow parts (day 48)

Designed by the player, Ash on Ostrava day 48 (numbers the DM's). Bows and crossbows; one per slot.

- **Heavy bowstring** (regular, uncommon, 1 TV; string slot): **reaches farther: the -2 starts 20 m later** (a
  recurve past ~60 m, not ~40; a compound past ~80 m, not ~60; the Hunter Windlass past ~80 m). **A heavier draw: a
  bow needs 5 more STR** (a recurve STR 25+, a compound 35+). A crossbow's lever does the work: no STR need.
- **Bow sight** (regular, common, 0.5 TV; sight slot): pin sights on the riser: **+1 on aimed shots past ~20 m**
  (stacks with the compound's +1).
- **Arc tracker** (*custom, not traditional: no trader carries this*; a smith's build, parts ~4 TV; tracker slot). A
  small cogitator lens on the riser reads range, drop and wind and puts **a glowing aim mark in the lens**. **No -2 at
  long range, out to twice the bow's reach.** **It arcs a shot over cover** onto someone the archer can see past it:
  **their cover counts one level lighter.** **No beams:** the archer stays silent and unseen (unlike the launcher's
  [arc calculator](kit.md#arc-calculator-launcher-sight), which gives +3 on far shots and shows two beams in smoke).

## Quiver

**Quiver** (common, 0.25 TV; leatherworkers, feral-world traders, Free Traders). Holds **20 arrows**, any mix of
types; drawing from it is free (it's what the bow's string draws from). Worn on the back or the hip. Without one, an
archer holds a few arrows in his bow hand: **3 at most**.

## Arrows

(The player's designs, Ash on Ostrava day 47.) **Gets through:** a hit drops a step. **Dents:** the armour shrugs it,
but it wears the armour down (codex Part 8, Wear-down). **Can break:** a d6 1-3 snaps it (lost). **The bow and the arm
push an arrow further:** the compound bow and STR 50+ each move it **one armour step** (a steel arrow from a compound
bow gets through reinforced flak; with STR 50+ too, carapace). Too weak to matter still applies (codex Armour).

| Arrow | Gets through | Dents | Breaks | Rarity, price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Scrap arrow** (a hit is **half a step, 0.5**: day 48) | no armour | nothing: flak and up shrug it, **no wear-down** | **always**, on any armour it hits | common; **anyone makes 5 from scrap in a few minutes, no roll** |
| **Metal arrow** | no armour | flak, reinforced flak | **can break** on carapace and up | common, 0.25 TV for 10 |
| **Steel arrow** | **flak** | reinforced flak, carapace | **can break** on reinforced flak and up | uncommon, 0.5 TV for 10 |
| **Seeker arrow, Mechanicus pattern** (explosive; [below](#explosive-arrows-day-48)) | **flak and carapace** (heavier on a solid hit) | n/a: it bursts | **spent on use** | rare, **5 TV each** |
| **Scrap explosive arrow** (explosive; [below](#explosive-arrows-day-48)) | as a tin-can bomb | n/a: it bursts | **spent on use** | common; **anyone makes 2 from scrap and powder in a few minutes** |

### Explosive arrows (day 48)

Designed by the player, Ash on Ostrava day 48 (numbers the DM's).
- **Seeker arrow, Mechanicus pattern** (rare, 5 TV each; Mechanicus, Free Traders): a full plasteel shaft, a
  heat-seeking head and a small shaped charge: a missile on a bow. **It locks onto heat: +3 to hit anything warm**
  (people, engines); no bonus on cold things (servitors, daemons). **It turns in flight: light and half cover don't
  count.** **On a hit it bursts:** through flak and carapace (heavier armour on a solid hit); **the target drops 2
  steps**, and everyone within ~2 m dives (DEX) or drops half a step (0.5).
- **Scrap explosive arrow** (common; made, not bought): **a tin-can bomb lashed to a shaft.** Light the fuse before he
  nocks it (the beat's short move). **Heavy: -2 on the shot, and half the bow's reach.** Where it lands it goes off
  **as a tin-can bomb** (catalogue explosives.md: ~3 m, half a step, flak and heavier shrug it), **with its d6 for
  timing; an 'early' bursts in mid-air, halfway there.**
