

## Flanged mace (scrap)

**Flanged mace (scrap)** (common, 0.25 TV; black market; the player's pick from his picture, day 48). A rebar haft with flanges cut from a hatch plate and welded on. **A solid hit goes one armour step further** than a club would. **Scrap quirk: when a hit lands on carapace or heavier, a d6: on a 1 a flange tears off, and it's a plain club for the rest of the fight.** *Who carries it: cultist, pirate, raider.*

## Spiked club (scrap)

**Spiked club (scrap)** (common, 0.1 TV; black market; the player's pick from his picture, day 48). A table leg or a length of pipe driven full of rusty nails and bound with wire. **Against no armour, a hit drops an extra step.** **Rusty: anyone it hits makes a CON save vs 13 or the wound festers: sickened, -1 on all rolls until treated (an antitoxin or a medic's care)** (the player, day 48). **Scrap quirk: on a hit, a d6: on a 1 it sticks in them, and it takes a beat to wrench it free.** *Who carries it: commoner, cultist, raider.*

## Ball mace (scrap)

**Ball mace (scrap)** (common, 0.1 TV; black market; the player's pick from his picture, day 48). A pipe with a valve wheel, a bearing or a gym weight welded on the end. **A solid hit staggers: -1 on their next roll.** **It batters armour: a hit the armour shrugs still counts 2 toward wear-down** (the player, day 48). **Top-heavy: -1 to parry.** **Scrap quirk: on a solid hit, a d6: on a 1 the weld snaps and the head flies off (~d6 m); what's left is a pipe.** *Who carries it: commoner, cultist, pirate, raider.*
