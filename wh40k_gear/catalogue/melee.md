

## Crusher, two-handed mace

**Crusher, two-handed mace** (uncommon, 5 TV; Munitorum, black market, Ecclesiarchy; the player's pick, day 48). Darktide's Zealot weapon: a heavy two-handed mace built for crowds. **Two hands, STR 30+** (under it, -2 on swings). **One swing sweeps up to 3 people in reach in front of him, each rolled separately.** **Through flak, and through carapace on a solid hit; a solid hit knocks the target flat.** **Slow: -1 to strike first.** (On the Zealot's kit list too.) *Who carries it: cultist, pirate, traitor.*

## Studded jian (iron bar-club), 'Kasr Rod' pattern

**Studded jian (iron bar-club), 'Kasr Rod' pattern** (common, 0.75 TV; Munitorum, black market; the player's pick, day 48). A **jian (鐧)**: a blunt 'sword', a four-sided iron bar on a sword's guard and grip, here with rivet studs near the tip (the player's picture). One-handed. **The studs bite: a solid hit when he beats the number by 2+, not 3+.** **It takes sword hilts and grips** (basket hilt, wide hilt, tsuka grip). **Through flak; carapace shrugs it** unless the hit is solid. *Who carries it: commoner, pirate, raider, traitor.*

## Blacksmith's hammer

**Blacksmith's hammer** (common, 0.5 TV; Munitorum, black market; the player's pick, day 48). A forge hammer: every village smithy and cult workshop has one. One-handed but heavy: **-1 to parry.** **It dents plate: a hit the armour shrugs still counts 2 toward wear-down.** Through flak. *Who carries it: commoner, cultist.*

## Scrap thunder hammer

**Scrap thunder hammer** (uncommon, 6 TV; black market; the player's pick, day 48). A two-handed sledge with a jury-rigged power field, wired to **a station power cell** (the salvage part) strapped to the haft. **Two hands, STR 30+; slow: -1 to strike first.** It hits as a sledgehammer (through flak; carapace on a solid hit), and **on every hit, a d6: 4-6 the field fires: through carapace on any hit, and the target is knocked flat.** **10 field hits per cell.** **Scrap quirk: a natural 1 on a swing shorts it: the wielder takes half a step (0.5) and the field is dead for the fight.** *Who carries it: cultist, raider.*
