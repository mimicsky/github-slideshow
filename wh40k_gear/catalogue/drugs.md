# Drugs and servo-skulls

Item rules for this category. General rules (heat, hot-shot, drop-prone, going Down, ammo crates) are in DND_CODEX Part 8, **Gear rules**.

## Stimm

**Stimm** (0.5 TV): keeps you on your feet; ignore the slowing of Hurt while it
lasts. **Concentrated stimm** (rare, 2 TV): one step better while it lasts (Badly
hurt fights as Hurt) and stabilises someone Down. How long a dose lasts, chaining
doses and the crash: Codex, Gear rules (Doses).

## Antitoxin

**Antitoxin** (1 TV): purges early plague or poison. **Concentrated antitoxin**
(rare, 4 TV): purges deep infections too: **the patient makes a CON save at +5** (the medic only gives the dose).

## SCIHU stim (speed coma inducing heal-up)

**SCIHU stim** (player design, Ash on Ostrava session 10; rare, 4 TV; Mechanicus and
medicae suppliers, Free Traders). A heavy medicae injector: accelerant compounds, a flood
of nutrients and cultured blood cells, and a big dose of anaesthetic. **Main use for the
Light: knocking someone out** (a captive to take alive, a wounded friend who won't lie still);
the healing comes with it. **On a willing or helpless patient** it just works. **On an
unwilling target** you must get the needle in: a melee hit (win the exchange) on someone
grabbed, pinned or unaware. They then roll **d20 + CON, 15+ fights it off** (groggy instead:
-2 on everything for the fight); otherwise they **drop in d6 beats (1-3: next beat, 4-6: two
beats)** and can still act until then. Astartes, daemon-touched and heavily augmented targets
shrug it off. **One dose puts the patient into a coma: d6, 1-2
for 4 days, 3-4 for 3 days, 5-6 for 2 days.** They're helpless the whole time and need a
medic's watch (fluids, turning). **They wake one health step better**, and **lasting injuries
heal in half the time** (a healing eye socket, broken bones, surgery recovery: Varga's 1-2
weeks becomes 4-7 days). **Risk:** on a Down or dying patient the medic rolls d20 at the
injection: 1-3 the heart can't take it (they stay Down; the next turn decides). One course
per injury; a second dose on the same wound does nothing more.

## Combat drugs

**Combat drugs** (uncommon, 1.5 TV a dose; one at a time): **Quicksilver** (+2
Athletics and moving), **Deadeye** (+2 shooting), **Frenzon** (+2 melee, -2 to
block or parry while it lasts), **Slaught** (the world slows down: +2 to strike
first and to dodge, +1 STR), **Adrenaline** (player design; heightened senses:
+4 Perception, +1 shooting). Length, chaining and the crash: Codex,
Gear rules (Doses). **Backfire:** on taking a dose
roll a d20; on 1-3 it gives **-2** to the same thing instead. On 4-17 it
works and ends in a crash; **on 18-20 it works with no crash.**

**Concentrated Frenzon** (rare, 4 TV): +3 melee, -2 to block or parry. Backfire
only on 1-2. Same dose rules.

## Drugs and interrogation

**Anyone under a dose can't be interrogated properly.** What they give you depends
on the drug (the table below; plain stimms and Quicksilver/Deadeye/Adrenaline
leave them clear-headed). Wait for the dose to wear off, or work with what the
drug lets through.

| Drug | Under interrogation |
| --- | --- |
| Frenzon / concentrated Frenzon / Blood-stimm | Only screams and fights. Nothing until it wears off |
| Slaught / Onslaught / Spur | Talks too fast, jumps topic to topic; half of it is true, and they can't tell which half |
| Spook | Raves about the warp. Mixed in may be real visions, true things they shouldn't know |
| Obscura | Dream-talk, nonsense. Useless until it wears off |
| Kalma | Calm and clear, but can't be frightened: threats and fear do nothing, reasoning still works |
| Morphia | Drowsy, slow answers; pain does nothing to them |

## Street, Guard and cult drugs

Same dose rules as the combat drugs (Codex, Gear rules: Doses) unless said
otherwise. Player-approved, session 8.

| Drug | Price | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Spook** | rare, 6 TV, black market | Wakes latent psychic power: anyone gets one random power from the psyker chart (Codex) for the dose, with a Perils trigger of 1-6 |
| **Obscura** | common, 0.5 TV, black market | The hive narcotic: euphoria and dreams, addictive, illegal and everywhere. No fight bonus; a user is dazed (-2) for the dose. Trade value, and a lead into the underhive and the syndicates |
| **Kalma** | uncommon, 1 TV | Guard-issue nerve calmer: immune to fear, panic and wavering for the dose, but -1 to strike first |
| **Morphia** | uncommon, 1 TV | Field painkiller: fight as one health step better, but you can't feel how hurt you are (the DM tracks it secretly) |
| **Spur** | common, 0.5 TV, black market | Cheap ganger stimulant: +1 to everything, but you crash on the last turn instead of one turn after |
| **Onslaught** | uncommon, 1.5 TV, black market | Speed: two moves in one turn, but -2 shooting |
| **Glanding** | rare, 20 TV, needs an augmetic surgeon | An implanted gland, no doses: once a fight, trigger any one combat drug's effect for 2 turns |
| **Blood-stimm** | Khorne cults only | +3 melee; can't shoot or retreat; must attack the nearest body, friend or foe |

## Cult and heretic drugs

Enemy chem-doc kits only. **Crimes to carry**: the Inquisition burns them. Same
dose rules (Codex, Gear rules: Doses) unless said otherwise.

| Drug | Who carries it | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Rot-stimm** | Nurgle cultists | Works like a stimm, but the user ignores Hurt *and* Badly hurt. Anyone not marked by Nurgle who takes one makes a CON save or catches sickness |
| **The god's "medicine"** | Cultists | Heals one health step. Anyone outside the cult makes a WIS save or hears the god for a day |
| **Forbidden tonic** | Raiders | Heals one health step and gives +1 to all rolls while it lasts. The crash is straight to Down |
| **Heretic cure-all** | Pirates | Cures poison or sickness. d20: on 1-5 it's poison instead (drop a health step) |

## Servo-skull, gun

**Servo-skull, gun** (rare, 30 TV): hovering skull with a laspistol; one shot a
beat at +0, directed by bead. **Servo-skull, burner** (rare, 35 TV): a
micro-flamer, a few metres' reach, 4 bursts a fight. Fragile: one solid hit
drops either.
