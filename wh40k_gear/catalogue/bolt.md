# Bolt weapons

Item rules for this category. General rules (heat, hot-shot, drop-prone, going Down, ammo crates) are in DND_CODEX Part 8, **Gear rules**.

**Every gun in this file has [Precision Sacrifice](abilities.md#precision-sacrifice)** (give up shots from a string for +1 each on one shot, up to +3; 3-beat cooldown).


## Bolt rounds (special loads)

**Kraken bolt rounds** (rare, 20 TV for 10; Astartes-pattern, Mechanicus, Free Traders; rule written with the player,
Ash on Ostrava day 47). Long-range penetrator bolts. A normal bolt already gets through everything up to power
armour, so a Kraken's edge is reach and vehicles: **it gets through an APC's armour** (Chimeras, Tauroxes, armoured
haulers: [vehicles.md](../bestiary/vehicles.md); tanks still shrug it), and **+1 on aimed shots past ~30 m**. Fits
bolt pistols, boltguns and heavy bolters. A magazine holds one load at a time.

**Off-brand bolt rounds** (**common, 2.5 TV for 25 rounds** (the player's price; standard bolts are 8 TV for 10);
black market, hive gunsmiths, Free Traders; **common in the enemy roll**; the player's idea, day 47). Cheap,
badly-made bolts: **less pen:** through carapace, but **heavy carapace and ceramite-forged only on a solid hit**, and
**power armour shrugs them** (it can still be worn down by them); **less damage:** **a crit drops just one step** (no
extra). Fits any bolt weapon.

**Meltacore bolt rounds** (rare, 25 TV for 10, DM's price; Mechanicus, black market, Free Traders; **rare in the
enemy roll**; the player's idea, day 47: named meltacore, not hellfire, because hellfire means bio-acid on the shells,
the belt and the Vindicare's rounds). Super-hot cores: **they get through a tank's armour from any side** (a hit takes a
hull point: [vehicles.md](../bestiary/vehicles.md)). Too hot for the gun: **+1 heat a string; at 3 it vents a beat**
(Vigil's scale). **Heavier kick: +1 recoil** (each shot after the first in a string takes -1 more: a boltgun's 2
becomes 3). Fits any bolt weapon.

## Bolt-shotgun, 'Breacher' pattern

**Bolt-shotgun, 'Breacher' pattern** (player design, rare, 10 TV). A short,
single-shot breech-loader that fires one bolt round as a slug: hits like a
boltgun. **Recoil:** at most 2 shots a beat; the second shot only if the first
roll was 10 or more (raw), otherwise the beat is spent recovering. **Simple:**
no magazines, loose bolt rounds, fast to reload. **Boltgun, Locke Mk IIb
'Spearhead'** (rare, 26 TV) added to the bolt pool as the Darktide pattern.

## Astartes bolters

**Astartes bolters** (player designs). Space Marine guns, sized for a hand twice a
human's: the bolt carbine (55 TV), bolt rifle (60) and auto bolt rifle (65) are
**epic** (the exotic tier: natural-20 finds only); the heavy bolt rifle (90) and
Astartes heavy bolter (120) are **legendary** (a natural 20, then a second d20 of
18-20). Sold only by the black market and Free Traders. A human can fire one only
braced on cover or a mount, at -4, one shot a beat, and can't move that beat; with
no brace it can't be fired. Mostly trade value or a trophy, and dangerous to own:
the Astartes want their gear back. The Imperial Guard heavy bolter (rare, 40 TV,
crew-served) is a separate, human weapon.
