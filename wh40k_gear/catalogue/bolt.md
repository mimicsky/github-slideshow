# Bolt weapons

Item rules for this category. General rules (heat, hot-shot, drop-prone, going Down, ammo crates) are in DND_CODEX Part 8, **Gear rules**.

**Every gun in this file has [Precision Sacrifice](abilities.md#precision-sacrifice)** (give up shots from a string for +1 each on one shot, up to +3; 3-beat cooldown). **Bolt pistols and boltguns have [Trigger Finger](abilities.md#trigger-finger)** (+1 every shot, +2 shots, a miss -2, +1 recoil; day 47).


**Every bolter has a STR need (set by the player, Ash on Ostrava day 48).** **Bolt pistols 20 · the bolt-pipe 25 ·
the Sororitas bolt pistol 15 · the bolt carbine and the Sororitas boltgun 25 · boltguns, combi-bolters, the Condemnor boltgun and the 'Breacher' bolt-shotgun 30 · the storm bolter 30 with two hands, 40 one-handed · the Guard heavy
bolter none braced or on a tripod, 50 from the hip.** Astartes bolters as their entry says (braced only, -4). **Under
the need he can still fire it, badly: -2 on every shot and +1 recoil** (the kick throws him about; unlike a bow, which
he can't draw at all).

## Sororitas boltgun (Godwyn-De'az pattern)

**Sororitas boltgun** (rare, 30 TV; shrine sellers, Free Traders; entry written with the player, Ash on Ostrava day 48).
In the lore, the Sisters' **Godwyn-De'az** pattern: a smaller, human-sized take on the Space Marine boltgun, **built for
normal hands** and blessed by the Ecclesiarchy. Strings of 3, recoil 2, bolt punch. **Lighter to fire: STR 25** (other
boltguns 30). **Blessed: its bolts count as blessed weapons against daemons** (daemon flesh can't shrug them:
bestiary bosses.md). **A 20-round box.** **Who carries it:** Sisters Novitiate (rare: sworn Sisters carry them more),
zealots.

## Sororitas bolt pistol (Godwyn-De'az pattern)

**Sororitas bolt pistol** (rare, 16 TV; shrine sellers, Free Traders; entry written with the player, day 48). The
Godwyn-De'az pistol: strings of 3, recoil 2, bolt punch. **Lighter to fire: STR 15** (other bolt pistols 20). **Blessed,**
as the boltgun. **An 8-round magazine.** **Who carries it:** Sisters Novitiate, zealots, Ecclesiarchy-linked commissars
(rare).

## Heavy bolt rounds (.998)

**Heavy bolt rounds (.998)** (the player's ask, Ash on Ostrava day 48; numbers the DM's). **Standard bolt rounds are the
.75** (they fit any bolt weapon). **The .998 is the bigger heavy bolt and fits only heavy bolters** (the Guard heavy
bolter, the Astartes heavy bolter): **a hit drops two steps**, one more than a .75; armour as any bolt (through
everything up to semi power armour; Space Marine armour on a solid hit). **Sold as the heavy bolter belt** (10 TV).
**The special bolt loads come in .998 too** (Kraken, Vengeance and the rest), as special stub loads come in heavy stub.

## Bolt rounds (special loads)

**Kraken bolt rounds** (rare, 20 TV for 10; Astartes-pattern, Mechanicus, Free Traders; rule written with the player,
Ash on Ostrava day 47). Long-range penetrator bolts. A normal bolt already gets through everything up to power
armour, so a Kraken's edge is reach and vehicles: **it gets through an APC's armour** (Chimeras, Tauroxes, armoured
haulers: [vehicles.md](../bestiary/vehicles.md); tanks still shrug it), and **+1 on aimed shots past ~30 m**. Fits
bolt pistols, boltguns and heavy bolters. A magazine holds one load at a time.

**Off-brand bolt rounds** (**common, 2.5 TV for 25 rounds** (the player's price; standard bolts are 8 TV for 10);
black market, hive gunsmiths, Free Traders; **common in the enemy roll**; the player's idea, day 47). Cheap,
badly-made bolts: **less pen:** through carapace, but **heavy carapace and ceramite-forged only on a solid hit**, and
**power armour shrugs them** (it can still be worn down by them); **less damage:** **a crit drops just one step** (no
extra). Fits any bolt weapon.

**Meltacore bolt rounds** (rare, 25 TV for 10, DM's price; Mechanicus, black market, Free Traders; **rare in the
enemy roll**; the player's idea, day 47: named meltacore, not hellfire, because hellfire means bio-acid on the shells,
the belt and the Vindicare's rounds). Super-hot cores: **they get through a tank's armour from any side** (a hit takes a
hull point: [vehicles.md](../bestiary/vehicles.md)). Too hot for the gun: **+1 heat a string; at 3 it vents a beat**
(Vigil's scale). **Heavier kick: +1 recoil** (each shot after the first in a string takes -1 more: a boltgun's 2
becomes 3). Fits any bolt weapon.

**Storm bolt rounds** (uncommon, 10 TV for 10, DM's price; Munitorum, Free Traders; the player's idea, day 47). Each
bolt bursts into shrapnel by the target: **a near miss still hits** (missing by 1-3 clips him), but **every hit lands
like a frag, not a bolt:** through flak, through carapace only on a solid hit, heavier armour shrugs it (it still
wears it down; Too weak to matter applies, codex Armour). More hits, less punch: for crowds in flak. Fits any bolt
weapon.

**Vengeance bolt rounds** (rare, **30 TV for 5** (the player's price); Deathwatch-pattern, black market, Free Traders;
**rare in the enemy roll**; the player's idea, day 47: super armour-piercing). **Through Space Marine armour on any hit** (plain bolts need a solid hit against it: the 11 tiers, day 48; DM default). **Against power armour (either kind) a hit drops one
extra step** (2 in all); against lighter armour, a normal bolt. **Gets through an APC's armour** (as Kraken; tanks shrug
it). **Unstable: on a natural 1 the round cooks off in the chamber:** the bearer drops a step and the gun is out for
the fight. Fits any bolt weapon.

## Boltgun, Godwyn pattern

**Boltgun, Godwyn pattern** (rare, 25 TV; Munitorum, Free Traders; entry written with the player, Ash on Ostrava day 48). **The baseline
boltgun.** In the lore the Godwyn is the Space Marines' pattern; **ours is a human-sized copy** made for Guard officers
and the Commissariat (the player, day 48: kept human-sized), heavier than the Sisters' Godwyn-De'az.
- **Strings of 3, recoil 2,** [Trigger Finger](abilities.md#trigger-finger).
- **Bolt punch:** each hit a step, through everything up to semi power armour; Space Marine armour only on a solid hit.
- **STR 30.**
- **A 15-round box magazine** (a spare is 0.2 TV; codex Gear rules, Magazines). Takes any .75 bolt load.
- **Owned in Ash on Ostrava:** the stolen one, with 18 bolt rounds, kept back from the haul sale by Ollin (day 36; in
  the crate).

**Abilities:** [Trigger Finger](abilities.md#trigger-finger); [Precision Sacrifice](abilities.md#precision-sacrifice) (every gun).

## Storm bolter, 'Throne Armageddon' pattern

**Storm bolter, 'Throne Armageddon' pattern** (rare, 45 TV; Munitorum (Commissariat), Mechanicus, Free Traders; the player's pick, Ash on Ostrava day 48,
after Commissar Yarrick's storm bolter; **Throne** is the maker name for human bolt guns, day 48; numbers the DM's). Two
bolters built side by side as one gun, firing together.
- **Strings of 6** (twice a boltgun's). **Recoil 2.** [Trigger Finger](abilities.md#trigger-finger), as any boltgun.
- **A bolt's punch:** each hit a step, through everything up to semi power armour; Space Marine armour only on a solid
  hit (codex Part 8, Armour).
- **STR 30 with two hands; Yarrick's grip: STR 40+ fires it one-handed** (the other hand free: a sword, a klaw, a fist).
- **Heavy: -1 on swapping targets.**
- **A 30-round twin box** (15 a barrel; 5 strings); a spare box is 0.3 TV empty (codex Gear rules, Magazines); bolts
  cost extra.
- **Who carries it:** commanders, leaders and commissars (rare); renegade and fallen commissars.

**Abilities:** [Trigger Finger](abilities.md#trigger-finger); [Precision Sacrifice](abilities.md#precision-sacrifice) (every gun).

## Combi-bolter, 'Throne Twinfire' pattern

**Combi-bolter, 'Throne Twinfire' pattern** (rare; Munitorum, Mechanicus, Free Traders; drafted with the player, Ash on
Ostrava day 48: the lore's combi-weapons; numbers the DM's). **A boltgun with a special weapon under it.** As a
boltgun: strings of 3, recoil 2, bolt punch, **STR 30**, [Trigger Finger](abilities.md#trigger-finger).
**Firing the special is the beat's main thing** (in place of the bolter string); **it's refilled after the fight.**

| Variant | The special | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Combi-flamer** | **one flamer gout** (a ~10 m line; targets catch fire) | 35 TV |
| **Combi-melta** | **one meltagun shot** (two steps through any armour inside ~15 m; 1 hull point) | 50 TV |
| **Combi-plasma** | **two plasma shots** (plasma punch; plasma heat: fired hot, it can overheat) | 50 TV |

**Who carries it:** commissars, veterans, leaders and commanders (rare).

## Condemnor boltgun, 'Throne Inquest' pattern

**Condemnor boltgun, 'Throne Inquest' pattern** (rare, 55 TV; Inquisition, Ecclesiarchy, Free Traders; the lore's
witch-hunter's gun, drafted with the player, day 48). **A boltgun with a stake crossbow on top.** As a boltgun, **STR
30.** **One stake loaded: firing it is the main thing; loading the next is the short move.** **The stake is the
[Condemnor's](bows.md#stake-crossbow-condemnor-pattern):** a hit on a psyker drops an extra step and stops his powers
next beat; a solid hit near a wall pins him. **Who carries it:** zealots and leaders (witch-hunters; rare).

## Officer's bolt carbine, 'Throne Patrician' pattern

**Officer's bolt carbine, 'Throne Patrician' pattern** (rare, 18 TV; Munitorum, Free Traders; drafted with the player,
day 48). **A short, light boltgun** for officers and boarding crews: strings of 3, recoil 2, bolt punch. **Handier:
STR 25** (between a pistol and a boltgun); **+1 on quick-draws.** **Shorter: -2 past ~40 m; a 15-round magazine.**
**Who carries it:** leaders, commanders, breachers; pirate boarders (rare).

## Boltgun, Locke Mk IIb 'Spearhead'

**Boltgun, Locke Mk IIb 'Spearhead'** (rare, 26 TV; Munitorum, Free Traders; Darktide's pattern, in the bolt pool since session 10; rules
written with the player, Ash on Ostrava day 48, after Darktide's: it fires by how it's held). A boltgun: bolt punch,
**STR 30**, recoil 2.
- **From the hip: full auto: strings of up to 6,** no hit bonuses in the string (misses still -1).
- **Shouldered: semi-auto: strings of 3, +1 on aimed shots;** [Trigger Finger](abilities.md#trigger-finger) works in
  this mode.
- **Switching is free:** by how he holds it, every string.
- **A 20-round box magazine** (a spare is 0.3 TV; codex Gear rules, Magazines).
- **Who carries it:** veterans; traitor elites (rare).

## Bolt-shotgun, 'Breacher' pattern

**Bolt-shotgun, 'Breacher' pattern** (player design, rare, 10 TV). A short,
single-shot breech-loader that fires one bolt round as a slug: hits like a
boltgun. **Recoil:** at most 2 shots a beat; the second shot only if the first
roll was 10 or more (raw), otherwise the beat is spent recovering. **Simple:**
no magazines, loose bolt rounds, fast to reload.

## Astartes bolters

**Astartes bolters** (player designs). Space Marine guns, sized for a hand twice a
human's: the bolt carbine (55 TV), bolt rifle (60) and auto bolt rifle (65) are
**epic** (the exotic tier: natural-20 finds only); the heavy bolt rifle (90) and
Astartes heavy bolter (120) are **legendary** (a natural 20, then a second d20 of
18-20). Sold only by the black market and Free Traders. A human can fire one only
braced on cover or a mount, at -4, one shot a beat, and can't move that beat; with
no brace it can't be fired. **An Ogryn can fire one unbraced, at -2** (big enough to hold it, not built for him; settled
by the player, day 48): **he never cleans it, so it's Dirty after its first fight and jams on a natural 1-3** until
someone else cleans it; **no special loads** (he can't tell them apart); Strength behind the blow doesn't apply (a gun). Mostly trade value or a trophy, and dangerous to own:
the Astartes want their gear back. The Imperial Guard heavy bolter (rare, 40 TV,
crew-served) is a separate, human weapon: [heavy.md, Heavy bolter, Guard pattern](heavy.md#heavy-bolter-guard-pattern).
