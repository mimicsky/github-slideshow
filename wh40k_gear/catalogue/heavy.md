# Heavy and vehicle weapons

Item rules for this category. General rules (heat, hot-shot, drop-prone, going Down, ammo crates) are in DND_CODEX Part 8, **Gear rules**.


## Heavy stubber

**Heavy stubber** (common, ~4 TV; Munitorum, black market; belts ~1 TV; rules set by the player, Ash on
Ostrava session 10). A belt-fed heavy stub gun, carried by one strong trooper or set on a tripod.
**Strings of up to 8,** with no hit bonuses in the string (a miss still costs -1 on the next shot).
**Recoil 1 when braced** (on a wall, a crate, a bipod or a tripod), **2 on the move.** Gets through flak and
carapace; heavy carapace shrugs it at range (codex Part 8, Armour). **Suppression:** whoever it is firing
at takes **-2 on their shots** while it keeps firing, hit or miss (the same -2 as a heavy gunner's or
Morcant's specialty: it counts once).

## Heavy stubber belts, special

(player design, Ash on Ostrava day 35; any heavy stubber; Morcant's twin feed lets him carry one
special belt beside a standard one and switch with the lever.) A standard belt is common, ~1 TV.
- **Man-stopper belt** (uncommon, 2 TV; Munitorum, black market, Free Traders): expanding rounds.
  Against **flak or less**, a **solid hit drops one extra step**. They flatten on hard plate:
  **carapace and heavier shrug them** (worse than a standard belt against armour).
- **Inferno belt** (uncommon, 3 TV; Munitorum, Mechanicus, Free Traders): incendiary rounds, the
  Nocturne Ember's ignite: **a hit on a raw 17+ sets the target alight** (next beat they spend it
  putting the fire out or drop a step; anything flammable nearby can catch). Armour as a standard belt.
- **Tungsten-core belt** (rare, 6 TV; Munitorum, Mechanicus, Free Traders): armour-piercing.
  **Gets through heavy carapace at any range** (the standard belt's weakness); ceramite-forged and
  power armour still shrug it (wear-down applies).
- **Hellfire belt** (rare, 10 TV; Mechanicus, Free Traders): bio-acid rounds, the Hellfire rule:
  **a hit that gets through also forces a CON save against 15, or the target drops one more step.**
  Armour as a standard belt.

## Brute stubber, 'Kasr Deddog' pattern

**Brute stubber, 'Kasr Deddog' pattern** (player design, Ash on Ostrava day 44; rare, ~6 TV; Munitorum,
black market, Free Traders; or built by a smith from a heavy stubber as a schematic). Named for Nork
Deddog, the Ogryn who guarded Lord Castellan Creed of Cadia. A heavy stubber cut down into a rifle
shape for a big man's hands: **no stock, a short barrel**, a top carry handle and a forward grip; it
feeds **heavy stubber belts from a box mag through an LMG-style feed tray**.
- **Brutes only: STR 50+** (Ogryns; a human that strong). Anyone weaker can fire it **only braced, at -3**.
- **Strings of up to 8**, no hit bonuses in the string (a miss still costs -1 on the next shot), as the heavy stubber.
- **Recoil 1, standing or on the move:** a heavy stubber a brute can walk and fire (a heavy stubber is 2 on the move).
- **Short barrel: -2 on shots past ~30 m.**
- **No stock: no aimed bonuses and no optic slot.**
- **Hits as a heavy stubber:** gets through flak and carapace; heavy carapace shrugs it at range. **Suppression** as the heavy stubber (-2 on the target's shots while it fires; counts once).
- **Belts:** any heavy stubber belt, the special belts included.

## Twin-linked heavy stubber, 'Bullgryn' pattern

**Twin-linked heavy stubber, 'Bullgryn' pattern** (player design, Ash on Ostrava day 45; rare, ~12 TV;
Munitorum, black market, Free Traders). Named for the Ogryn shock troops. Two heavy stubber barrels side by
side firing twice as fast, fed from a **belt box** that sits underneath and feeds up into the guns. A
**skeleton chainsaw-style handgrip** at the back, a **horizontal handle** at the front, a **carry handle** on
top, and **no sight at all**: it's made to spray and pray.
- **Brutes only: STR 50+** (Ogryn-sized). Anyone weaker can only fire it from a mount, at -3.
- **Strings of up to 16** (twice a heavy stubber), no hit bonuses in the string, and **-1 on every shot** (no
  sight). **Recoil 1 standing, 2 on the move.**
- **Hits as a heavy stubber:** through flak and carapace; heavy carapace shrugs it at range.
- **Heavy suppression:** whoever it fires at takes **-3 on their shots** while it keeps firing, hit or miss
  (counts once; it replaces the heavy stubber's -2).
- **Belt box:** a box (two heavy stubber belts, ~2 TV) lasts **3 full strings**. **Seating a new box takes two
  beats** while the belt is aligned in the feed (**one beat** with a loader helping).

## Hellstrike launcher, 'Bone'ead' pattern (Ogryn)

**Hellstrike launcher, 'Bone'ead' pattern** (player design, Ash on Ostrava day 45; rare, ~30 TV; Munitorum,
Free Traders; **Hellstrike missiles ~8 TV each**). Named for the Ogryn sergeants who get the brain implant. A
shoulder tube the size of a man, built for an Ogryn, that fires **the Valkyrie's own Hellstrike missiles**.
- **Brutes only: STR 50+.** Anyone weaker can only fire it from a mount (a vehicle, a sandbagged tripod), at -3.
- **One missile, one shot;** reloading the tube takes a beat. No strings.
- **Hellstrike:** the target is hit **through power armour and light vehicles and drops two steps.** The
  blast catches everyone else within **~4 m**: a **dive (DEX) or drop a step.**
- **Arming distance ~10 m:** closer than that the warhead doesn't arm; it's a hard hit, one step, no blast.
- **Backblast:** anyone **~3 m behind the tube** drops a step. **Loud:** the whole area hears it.

## Man-portable multi-laser

**Man-portable multi-laser** (rare, 15 TV; Munitorum, Free Traders; rare loot; Ash on Ostrava, day 34, DM
default; the player may change it). Shot strings **up to 6**; hits like a **hellgun** (through carapace); fires
**only braced** (no recoil when braced); **a pack lasts 2 strings**. Morcant's (campaign sheet).

## Valkyrie bolt-rocket pods, pair

**Valkyrie bolt-rocket pods, pair** (rare, 35 TV; one of the best things a
Valkyrie can carry): 6 rockets. Each bursts over its target into a spray of
bolt rounds: everyone within ~6 m is hit through carapace. Fire 1-2 a pass.

## Las-cannon, medium tripod emplacement

**Las-cannon, medium tripod emplacement** (rare, 35 TV): smaller than the crew
lascannon, bigger than anything carried. Set up in a beat; **one heavy beam
every other beat** (it recharges). Through power armour and light vehicles;
needs a coupling or a power cell pack.

## Ship and vehicle autocannons on people

**Ship and vehicle autocannons on people** (a rule, not an item: the autocannon itself is in pools.json; Ash on
Ostrava, day 34, DM default; the player may change it). A hit **drops one extra step** (heavy explosive rounds).
A ship's point-defence turret firing at ground targets: **-2** (not built for it); turret-mounted guns have **no
recoil**.
