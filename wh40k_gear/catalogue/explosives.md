# Explosives, mines and grenades

Item rules for this category. General rules (heat, hot-shot, drop-prone, going Down, ammo crates) are in DND_CODEX Part 8, **Gear rules**.

## Plasma tripwire mine

**Plasma tripwire mine** (player design, rare, 10 TV). Clamped beside a door,
vent or crate; a thin trip-laser crosses the gap. Whoever breaks the beam sets
off the plasma cell inside: everyone in the doorway or within ~3 m is hit through
carapace, power armour on a solid hit. Setting it takes a beat (a natural 1 while
setting it sets it off). The beam can be spotted (Perception; easier in smoke or
dust).

## Air-frag 'blender' grenade

**Air-frag 'blender' grenade** (player design, uncommon, 2 TV). Twice the
shrapnel, a **2-second fuse** (too short to throw back). In the open it's a frag
grenade. **In a closed room it turns the room into a blender:** everyone inside
is hit, cover inside the room doesn't help, flak is shredded (carapace mostly
holds). **Ricochet:** anyone friendly in the doorway or within ~5 m of the
opening makes a DEX save or takes a fragment.

## Thrown demo satchel

**Thrown demo satchel** (player design, uncommon, 5 TV). A heavy charge with a
**mag-clamp** and a **paired detonator** already linked: no wiring, no fuse.
**Throw it** (about 10-15 m; a throw roll decides whether it clamps where you
wanted or lands nearby) or **set it by hand**, then blow it from cover with the
detonator (line of sight or ~100 m). One satchel does what two kraks do to a
door; anyone within ~4 m is hit hard. **Armed, it beeps:** heard within a couple
of metres, or further on a good Perception roll.

## Cult gas bottle

(uncommon, 1.5 TV; black market; player design.) A potion-shaped glass bottle with
a sealed plastek cap for filling, inside a plastek cage that runs up the bottle so
it survives a pack or a pocket. It only breaks when thrown hard: on impact the
glass shatters and the gas comes out in a cloud about 3 m across that hangs for 2
beats (wind or a vent moves it). A natural 1 on the throw shatters it at the
thrower's feet. Cultists make them; the gas is their god's:

| Cult | Gas | Anyone in the cloud |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plague (Nurgle) | Rot-spores | CON save or sickened: drops a health step at the end of the fight unless given antitoxin |
| Blood (Khorne) | Red rage-mist | WIS save or must attack the nearest person next beat, friend or foe |
| Excess (Slaanesh) | Sweet euphoric haze | WIS save or lose their next beat, lost in it |
| Change (Tzeentch) | Shimmering vision-smoke | WIS save or -3 on every shot and throw next beat (they see too many targets) |
| Undivided / generic | Tear gas mixed with thick smoke | Blocks sight like a smoke grenade. CON save or eyes stream and lungs burn: -2 on shots, throws and Perception while in it and for 1 beat after, and no aimed shots. A gas mask or rebreather stops the tear gas (not the smoke) |

A rebreather or gas mask gives advantage on the save; a sealed void-suit or
hazard suit is immune. Enemy cultists can carry them (extras and found-or-made).

## Psyker overload grenade

**Psyker overload grenade** (player design, Ash on Ostrava session 9; rare, 10 TV;
Mechanicus, Free Traders; the Ordo issues them). Prime and throw: a burst of psychic
noise, like a hive mind's shadow in the warp, over ~5 m. **Non-psykers feel a buzz and
nothing more.** **Every psyker in the area rolls** (a friendly psyker: d20 + CON bonus;
an enemy: a flat d20):
- **1-10: overloaded.** They drop to the ground fighting it off for **3 beats** (no
  actions), then come out of it **Badly hurt.**
- **11-17: a minor wound** (eyes and ears bleed): **-1 on all rolls until treated or
  rested** (no health step lost), and **no powers for 2 beats.**
- **18-20: they shrug it off.**

(The Culexus psyk-out grenade, in the Assassinorum file, is the epic version.)

## Web grenade (Arbites pattern)

**Web grenade** (player pick, Ash on Ostrava session 9; uncommon, 2 TV; Munitorum, Free
Traders, Arbites armouries). Bursts into sticky strands over **~3 m**. Everyone caught
is **webbed: can't move, -2 on all actions.** **Getting free:** a beat cutting loose
with a blade, or a **STR roll of 15+** to tear out; an ally can cut them free with their
beat. **Does no harm** (a capture tool). Dissolves after ~10 minutes, or at once with
Arbites solvent. **Ogryns** tear free automatically, but it costs them their beat.

## Vox-jammer grenade

**Vox-jammer grenade** (player pick, Ash on Ostrava session 9; rare, 5 TV; Mechanicus,
Free Traders). Sticks where it lands and fills **~30 m** with screaming static for **3
beats**. **All vox inside dies:** beads, vox-casters, data-links; nobody inside can call
out or listen in. Enemy commanders can only order by shouting (allies within earshot);
vox specialists can't call reinforcements; **Skitarii data-links break** (no "act
first"). **It kills the thrower's side's vox too:** orders by voice only. Someone can
find and smash it (a beat).

## Blessed incense grenade (Ecclesiarchy)

**Blessed incense grenade** (player pick, Ash on Ostrava session 9; rare, 4 TV;
Ecclesiarchy; a priest or the Light's chaplain can bless one). Thick, sweet holy smoke,
**~5 m, 3 beats**; blocks sight like smoke. **Daemons, blessed cultists and anything
warp-tainted inside: -2 on all rolls, and they burn (drop a step each beat they stay).**
**Allies inside: +2 against fear and the warp.** **Rogue psykers inside: +1 on their
Perils trigger.** Ordinary people just cough.

## Spec flash grenade

**Spec flash grenade** (player design, Ash on Ostrava session 10; rare, 3 TV; Munitorum
(Scion and boarding issue), Mechanicus, Free Traders). An **impact-fused** flash grenade:
it bursts on contact (can't be dodged or thrown back) into a central white flash and a
spray of **mini flares** that scatter and burn across a room (~8 m). **Everyone in the
room** is **blinded and deafened for 1 beat**: **-3 on shots**, **can't block or parry**
(house rule 5), and can't hear orders or vox. **Anyone looking straight at it when it
bursts** (facing the door or the thrower; the DM calls it, or d20 per enemy, 1-10 looking)
gets it **twice as bad: 2 beats, -6 on shots**. The flares keep burning a beat after: no
darkness in the room, and anyone in the doorway is lit up. **Counters:** flash-proof gear
(Commissar eye) ignores it; polarised visors and void-masks cut it one step (looking-at-it
becomes the normal effect; normal becomes -1 for 1 beat). **The thrower's side** must be
outside the room or looking away, or it takes them too. A **natural 1 on the throw** bursts
it at the thrower's feet.
