# Heretic and cult items

Item rules for this category. Things only heretics should own. Carrying them is a
crime; the Inquisition (and Kesh) will want every one.

## Chaos grimoire

(rare, 12 TV on the black market; player design.) A cultist's forbidden book:
hand-bound, stitched skin or cracked leather, pages in blood, chalk-ink or a
script that won't hold still. **Mostly the DM's call.** Most are a madman's
scribble. Some are real, and those are dangerous.

**Opening or reading it:** the DM rolls a d20 **in secret** and decides what it
means, using this as a guide:

| d20 | Roughly |
| --- | --- |
| 1-5 | **Something very bad.** A warp-whisper that won't leave (WIS save or the DM gives a lasting quirk or nightmare), a possession attempt, lights dying and frost, something on the other side noticing the reader, or a ward that bursts and burns |
| 6-12 | **Nothing.** Ravings, recipes, lists of names and filth |
| 13-17 | **A clue.** A cult location, a name, a date, a ritual's purpose, a link to another thread (Velk, Thask, the gardeners) |
| 18-20 | **Real knowledge, at a price.** A rite, a true name or a warding; using it costs something the DM chooses, now or later |

- A **purity seal** gives advantage on any save it forces, and burns away doing it.
- A **psyker** feels it before it's opened.
- **Destroying** one properly takes fire and a priest's rite (or a blessed flamer);
  tearing pages out doesn't count, and it may not want to burn.
- **Handing it to the Inquisition** is the safe choice and earns favour. Keeping one
  is heresy if anyone finds out.
- Enemy cult leaders and witches may carry one (found-or-made, rare).

### In a cultist's hands: the chant

A cultist with a **real** grimoire (the DM decides) can use it as a weapon. They
start **chanting** from it, and everyone nearby hears and sees it: the words are
wrong, the air thickens, lights flicker. The chant builds over beats, and the
**Light gets a chance to stop it** before it lands.

- **Breaking the chant:** wounding the chanter, knocking the book away, a flash
  or stun, or anything that makes them stop for a beat ends it. It starts over
  from nothing.
- **Each beat of chanting, the DM picks one effect** (or lets it build to the next):

| Beats chanted | What it can do |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | **A curse on one target in sight:** WIS save or -2 on everything next beat; or a lash of warp-force that hits like a lasgun and ignores flak |
| 2 | **Fear or fury:** everyone of the Light within ~10 m makes a WIS save or freezes for a beat; or every cultist nearby gets +1 next beat; or warp-fire on one target (hits like a flamer) |
| 3 | **The big one, DM's choice:** a small daemon or warp-thing torn through, a warp storm in the room (lights out, frost, everyone at -2), the god's gift on the chanter (healed, mutated, stronger), or something the cult's god would do |

- **Backlash:** each beat the chanter rolls a d20; on **1-3** the warp bites them
  instead (they drop a health step, and on a natural 1 something worse, DM's call).
  The book doesn't care who it eats.
- A **purity seal** gives advantage on saves against the chant, and burns away.
- A **psyker** in the Light can try to fight the chant (opposed WIS roll; win and
  it breaks).

## Chaos psyker staff

(rare, 25 TV on the black market; player design.) A cultist witch's focus: a
staff of black iron, bone or warped wood, wound with chains and charms, the
crown cut into the mark of their god. **About one cult witch in four carries one**
(the rest have a crude stick). It channels Chaos through the psyker who holds it.

**In a witch's hands:** each beat they use **one power**, and roll a d20 for
Perils: **1-3**, the warp bites them instead (they drop a health step; a natural 1
is worse, the DM's call).

| Power | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Warp-fire** | A gout of fire at one target: hits like a flamer and sets them alight (put it out next beat or drop a health step) |
| **Lightning** | Arcs to a target and one person beside them: ignores flak, and anyone hit takes -2 next beat |
| **Force blast** | Hits like a hellgun and throws the target off their feet: they're **knocked down** (in-hand weapons drop; drop-prone guns roll) |
| **Warp ward** | Blocks the first shot and the first melee hit at the witch this beat (a raw 17+ still gets through) |
| **Mind-lash** | One target in sight: WIS save or frozen for a beat; a purity seal gives advantage |

The DM may give a witch powers that fit their god (plague-rot, blood-boil,
changing flesh). Wounding, stunning or disarming the witch breaks a power
mid-beat. **As loot:** useless to anyone but a psyker. Anyone else who carries it
makes a WIS save each day or hears it; the Inquisition wants it destroyed.
