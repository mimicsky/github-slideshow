# Heretic and cult items

Item rules for this category. Things only heretics should own. Carrying them is a
crime; the Inquisition (and Kesh) will want every one.

## Chaos grimoire

(rare, 12 TV on the black market; player design.) A cultist's forbidden book:
hand-bound, stitched skin or cracked leather, pages in blood, chalk-ink or a
script that won't hold still. **Mostly the DM's call.** Most are a madman's
scribble. Some are real, and those are dangerous.

**Opening or reading it:** the DM rolls a d20 **in secret** and decides what it
means, using this as a guide:

| d20 | Roughly |
| --- | --- |
| 1-5 | **Something very bad.** A warp-whisper that won't leave (WIS save or the DM gives a lasting quirk or nightmare), a possession attempt, lights dying and frost, something on the other side noticing the reader, or a ward that bursts and burns |
| 6-12 | **Nothing.** Ravings, recipes, lists of names and filth |
| 13-17 | **A clue.** A cult location, a name, a date, a ritual's purpose, a link to another thread (Velk, Thask, the gardeners) |
| 18-20 | **Real knowledge, at a price.** A rite, a true name or a warding; using it costs something the DM chooses, now or later |

- A **purity seal** gives advantage on any save it forces, and burns away doing it.
- A **psyker** feels it before it's opened.
- **Destroying** one properly takes fire and a priest's rite (or a blessed flamer);
  tearing pages out doesn't count, and it may not want to burn.
- **Handing it to the Inquisition** is the safe choice and earns favour. Keeping one
  is heresy if anyone finds out.
- Enemy cult leaders and witches may carry one (found-or-made, rare).
