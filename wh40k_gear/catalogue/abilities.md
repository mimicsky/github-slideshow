# Gear abilities

Set by the player, Ash on Ostrava day 47. **Abilities come from what someone carries, not from their role or
class:** anyone holding that kind of gear can use it, friend or enemy.

**How this list works (the paper trail):**
- **Every ability is written once, here,** with **the items that have it** listed under it.
- **Every item that has an ability carries an "Abilities:" line in its own catalogue entry** (and on the
  campaign sheet, where someone owns one), linking back here. So when an item is rolled or looked up, its entry
  points here, and from here every item with the ability points back.
- These lines are for the DM and the files; they don't get read out in play.

**Who uses them (the player asked, day 47):** **everyone who carries the gear, on their own, when it makes sense:** the Light's NPCs and enemies alike, played by the DM like any other choice (Dace plants his shield when the fire gets heavy and Tamsin's in the open; a Bulwark plants as it walks up a street). **An order can also call for one** ('Dace, plant!'); an ordered ally answers and does it. Tamsin uses his only when the player says so.

## Cooldowns (every gear ability)

**Every gear ability has a 3-beat cooldown, per person** (set by the player, day 47): **used on beat 1, it's
ready again on beat 4.** (Two different abilities don't share a cooldown: a Heavy Attack on beat 1 doesn't stop a
Plant on beat 2.) Passive things that aren't abilities (Strength behind the blow) have none.

## Not gear, but connected

**Strength behind the blow (STR 50+)** (set by the player, day 47): a body ability, not gear, written in the codex
(Part 8, Armour). Anyone with STR 50+ drives their **melee or thrown** weapon one armour step further; **power weapons** instead
**drop two steps a hit.** Who has it: Morcant (STR 51); every Ogryn; the strongest brutes.

## Plant

**Shields.** The bearer sets the shield and plants himself for the beat. **It takes his move: he can't move while
planted.**
- **He counts as in full cover from the front.**
- **He can't aim.** He can only **blind fire (-4, no chain bonuses; DM default)**, or, **if his shield has a
  viewing slot, fire through the slot (-2; semi-accurate; DM default).**
- **One other person can crouch behind him and shoot round it, peeking out:** they count as in **half cover
  (+2)** and shoot normally.
- **Heavy weapons** (krak, melta, plasma, heavy weapons) treat a planted shield as **heavy cover (+4), not full**,
  unless the shield's entry says otherwise (DM default).
- Shots from the side or behind ignore it, as with any shield (codex Part 8, Shields).

## Dive

**Jump packs and lift packs** (set by the player, day 47). **A big burst of movement, from the ground or in the
air:** a long leap (~30 m) **into the enemy** (to land in melee), **out of trouble** (a hot escape or a jump
back), or **up to high ground.** It's the beat's move. **The main thing that beat is halved, rounded down**
(the player's nerf, day 47): a string of 3 becomes 1 shot (Tamsin's 4 becomes 2), a melee chain of 3 or 2 becomes
1 swing; a reload still takes the whole main thing.
- **It burns two bursts** of the pack's charge (a Kasrkin-pattern charge holds about six).
- **It's very fast: anyone shooting at the wearer that beat treats him as abnormally fast (+3 to the hidden
  number), and it STACKS with Airborne's -3 to shots at a flyer** (the player, day 47: for that beat he's like a
  missile in the air, diving and landing). **Once he's landed, he's normal again** from the next beat.
- The pack's other rules still apply: loud and bright (no stealth), and **a natural 1** on the landing: the field
  stutters and he drops one step.

**Items with it** (all in [kit.md](kit.md)):
- [Jump pack, Kasrkin pattern](kit.md#jump-pack-kasrkin-pattern)
- [Lift pack (heretek grav-chute)](kit.md#lift-pack-heretek-grav-chute)
- **Guard jump packs** (no entry of their own: the Kasrkin pattern's rules)
- **Owned in Ash on Ostrava:** Varga's lift pack; Isk's Guard jump pack (from IX, day 47); the stolen Guard jump
  pack from the customs house (spare; waiting on Ferro) (campaign sheet).

## Wide Slash

**Two-handed melee weapons** (and one-or-two-handed ones when swung two-handed) (set by the player, day 47). **One
swing of the chain, swung wide and flat.**
- **It's a normal swing against the first target.** **If it lands** (beats his block or parry), **it automatically
  hits one more enemy standing next to him** (within reach): the same blow, no defence for the second.
- **If the first target blocks** (1-2 higher): no hit, no penalty.
- **If the first target parries** (3+ higher): the initiative flips as normal, and **the swinger is over-committed:
  -2 on his parries and dodges through the next beat.**

## Heavy Attack

**Every melee weapon** (set by the player, day 47). The wielder winds up and drives in with his whole weight.
- **The defender gets +3** on his parry or dodge against it (he sees it coming).
- **There's no block:** the defender's total **3+ higher is a parry** (as normal: no damage, the initiative flips);
  **anything less, it lands** (a 1-2-higher 'block' counts as a hit).
- **When it lands, it goes one armour step further and drops the target one extra health step** (two steps).
- **It's one swing of the chain, and it ends the chain** (the wind-up costs the rest) (confirmed by the player).
- **It stacks with Strength behind the blow** (STR 50+: one more armour step; with a power weapon, one more
  health step: three steps a hit) (confirmed by the player).
- A raw 17+ still can't be parried or dodged, as always.

**Items with it:** **every melee weapon** in [melee.md](melee.md) (said once at the top of that file, not on each
entry), the Light's and the enemy's alike: Shameholder, Promise Holder, Kell's chain axe, Varo's sabre, the
Sisters in hand, a Crusher's maul.

## Precision Sacrifice

**Every gun that fires strings** (set by the player, day 47). **Give up shots from this beat's string for +1 each
on the one shot you do fire:** with Vigil's string of 4, give up 3 and fire one precise shot at **+3**. It's the
first shot of the string, so no recoil, no chain.
- **Capped at +3** (DM default), so a machine pistol's string of 10 doesn't turn into +9.
- Everything else still applies: range, cover, moving, run and gun, the gun's crit range.
- Cooldown: 3 beats, like every ability.

**Items with it:** **every gun** in [las.md](las.md), [stub.md](stub.md), [heavy.md](heavy.md), [bolt.md](bolt.md),
[plasma.md](plasma.md), [scrap.md](scrap.md) and the guns in [assassinorum.md](assassinorum.md) (said once at the top
of each file, not on each entry). Launchers that fire one round a beat (Six Bells) gain nothing from it.
