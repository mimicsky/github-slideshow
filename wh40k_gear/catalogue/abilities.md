# Gear abilities

Set by the player, Ash on Ostrava day 47. **Abilities come from what someone carries, not from their role or
class:** anyone holding that kind of gear can use it, friend or enemy.

**How this list works (the paper trail):**
- **Every ability is written once, here,** with **the items that have it** listed under it.
- **Every item that has an ability carries an "Abilities:" line in its own catalogue entry** (and on the
  campaign sheet, where someone owns one), linking back here. So when an item is rolled or looked up, its entry
  points here, and from here every item with the ability points back.
- These lines are for the DM and the files; they don't get read out in play.

## Plant

**Shields.** The bearer sets the shield and plants himself for the beat. **It takes his move: he can't move while
planted.**
- **He counts as in full cover from the front.**
- **He can't aim.** He can only **blind fire (-4, no chain bonuses; DM default)**, or, **if his shield has a
  viewing slot, fire through the slot (-2; semi-accurate; DM default).**
- **One other person can crouch behind him and shoot round it, peeking out:** they count as in **half cover
  (+2)** and shoot normally.
- **Heavy weapons** (krak, melta, plasma, heavy weapons) treat a planted shield as **heavy cover (+4), not full**,
  unless the shield's entry says otherwise (DM default).
- Shots from the side or behind ignore it, as with any shield (codex Part 8, Shields).
