# Gear abilities

Set by the player, Ash on Ostrava day 47. **Abilities come from what someone carries, not from their role or
class:** anyone holding that kind of gear can use it, friend or enemy.

**How this list works (the paper trail):**
- **Every ability is written once, here,** with **the items that have it** listed under it.
- **Every item that has an ability carries an "Abilities:" line in its own catalogue entry** (and on the
  campaign sheet, where someone owns one), linking back here. So when an item is rolled or looked up, its entry
  points here, and from here every item with the ability points back.
- These lines are for the DM and the files; they don't get read out in play.

**Who uses them (the player asked, day 47):** **everyone who carries the gear, on their own, when it makes sense:** the Light's NPCs and enemies alike, played by the DM like any other choice (Dace plants his shield when the fire gets heavy and Tamsin's in the open; a Bulwark plants as it walks up a street). **An order can also call for one** ('Dace, plant!'); an ordered ally answers and does it. Tamsin uses his only when the player says so.

## Cooldowns (every gear ability)

**Every gear ability has a 3-beat cooldown, per person** (set by the player, day 47): **used on beat 1, it's
ready again on beat 4.** (Two different abilities don't share a cooldown: a Heavy Attack on beat 1 doesn't stop a
Plant on beat 2.) Passive things that aren't abilities (Strength behind the blow) have none.

**One ability per beat, per person** (set by the player, day 47). Whatever he carries, he uses **one** gear ability
in a beat (a Plant and a Pinned Fire can't both happen in the same beat; a Field Burst can't ride a Heavy Attack).

## Not gear, but connected

**Strength behind the blow (STR 50+)** (set by the player, day 47): a body ability, not gear, written in the codex
(Part 8, Armour). Anyone with STR 50+ drives their **melee or thrown** weapon one armour step further; **power weapons** instead
**drop two steps a hit.** Who has it: Morcant (STR 51); every Ogryn; the strongest brutes.

## Plant

**Shields.** The bearer sets the shield and plants himself for the beat. **It takes his move: he can't move while
planted.**
- **He counts as in full cover from the front.**
- **He can't aim.** He can only **blind fire (-4, no chain bonuses; DM default)**, or, **if his shield has a
  viewing slot, fire through the slot (-2; semi-accurate; DM default).**
- **One other person can crouch behind him and shoot round it, peeking out:** they count as in **half cover
  (+2)** and shoot normally.
- **Heavy weapons** (krak, melta, plasma, heavy weapons) treat a planted shield as **heavy cover (+4), not full**,
  unless the shield's entry says otherwise (DM default).
- Shots from the side or behind ignore it, as with any shield (codex Part 8, Shields).

**Items with it** (all in [kit.md](kit.md)):
- [Boarding shield](kit.md#boarding-shield)
- [Arbitrator shield](kit.md#arbitrator-shield): its **pistol notch counts as a viewing slot** (slot fire, -2)
- [Storm shield](kit.md#storm-shield)
- [Compact power shield, 'Praesidium' pattern](kit.md#compact-power-shield-praesidium-pattern): **only when on**;
  it's see-through, so it **counts as a viewing slot** (slot fire, -2)
- [Slab shield (Ogryn)](kit.md#slab-shield-ogryn) (enemy only; the Ogryn's Bulwark plants it as it walks: roles.md)
- [Makeshift shield](kit.md#makeshift-shield)
- [Cogitator shield (scrap)](kit.md#cogitator-shield-scrap)
- [Forged shield](kit.md#forged-shield)
- [Improvised shield (a door)](kit.md#improvised-shield-a-door)
- **Mods:** [Shield viewing slot](kit.md#shield-viewing-slot-mod) (gives any planted shield slot fire, -2);
  [Spiked shield](kit.md#spiked-shield-mod) (no change to Plant)
- **Owned in Ash on Ostrava:** Dace's breaching shield (forged, viewing slot): slot fire, -2 (campaign sheet).

## Dive

**Jump packs and lift packs** (set by the player, day 47). **A big burst of movement, from the ground or in the
air:** a long leap (~30 m) **into the enemy** (to land in melee), **out of trouble** (a hot escape or a jump
back), or **up to high ground.** It's the beat's move. **The main thing that beat is halved, rounded down**
(the player's nerf, day 47): a string of 3 becomes 1 shot (Tamsin's 4 becomes 2), a melee chain of 3 or 2 becomes
1 swing; a reload still takes the whole main thing.
- **It burns two bursts** of the pack's charge (a Kasrkin-pattern charge holds about six).
- **It's very fast: anyone shooting at the wearer that beat treats him as abnormally fast (+3 to the hidden
  number), and it STACKS with Airborne's -3 to shots at a flyer** (the player, day 47: for that beat he's like a
  missile in the air, diving and landing). **Once he's landed, he's normal again** from the next beat.
- The pack's other rules still apply: loud and bright (no stealth), and **a natural 1** on the landing: the field
  stutters and he drops one step.

**Items with it** (all in [kit.md](kit.md)):
- [Jump pack, Kasrkin pattern](kit.md#jump-pack-kasrkin-pattern)
- [Lift pack (heretek grav-chute)](kit.md#lift-pack-heretek-grav-chute)
- **Guard jump packs** (no entry of their own: the Kasrkin pattern's rules)
- **Owned in Ash on Ostrava:** Varga's lift pack; Isk's Guard jump pack (from IX, day 47); the stolen Guard jump
  pack from the customs house (spare; waiting on Ferro) (campaign sheet).

## Wide Slash

**Two-handed melee weapons** (and one-or-two-handed ones when swung two-handed) (set by the player, day 47). **One
swing of the chain, swung wide and flat.**
- **It's a normal swing against the first target.** **If it lands** (beats his block or parry), **it automatically
  hits one more enemy standing next to him** (within reach): the same blow, no defence for the second.
- **If the first target blocks** (1-2 higher): no hit, no penalty.
- **If the first target parries** (3+ higher): the initiative flips as normal, and **the swinger is over-committed:
  -2 on his parries and dodges through the next beat.**

**Items with it** (in [melee.md](melee.md)):

## Heavy Attack

**Every melee weapon** (set by the player, day 47). The wielder winds up and drives in with his whole weight.
- **The defender gets +3** on his parry or dodge against it (he sees it coming).
- **There's no block:** the defender's total **3+ higher is a parry** (as normal: no damage, the initiative flips);
  **anything less, it lands** (a 1-2-higher 'block' counts as a hit).
- **When it lands, it goes one armour step further and drops the target one extra health step** (two steps).
- **It's one swing of the chain, and it ends the chain** (the wind-up costs the rest) (confirmed by the player).
- **It stacks with Strength behind the blow** (STR 50+: one more armour step; with a power weapon, one more
  health step: three steps a hit) (confirmed by the player).
- A raw 17+ still can't be parried or dodged, as always.

**Items with it:** **every melee weapon** in [melee.md](melee.md) (said once at the top of that file, not on each
entry), the Light's and the enemy's alike: Shameholder, Promise Holder, Kell's chain axe, Varo's sabre, the
Sisters in hand, a Crusher's maul.

## Precision Sacrifice

**Every gun that fires strings** (set by the player, day 47). **Give up shots from this beat's string for +1 each
on the one shot you do fire:** with Vigil's string of 4, give up 3 and fire one precise shot at **+3**. It's the
first shot of the string, so no recoil, no chain.
- **Capped at a flat +3** (confirmed by the player, day 47, over a 20%-of-the-string cap or a cap by gun class: simple and the same for everyone), so a machine pistol's string of 10 doesn't turn into +9.
- Everything else still applies: range, cover, moving, run and gun, the gun's crit range.
- Cooldown: 3 beats, like every ability.

**Items with it:** **every gun** in [las.md](las.md), [stub.md](stub.md), [heavy.md](heavy.md), [bolt.md](bolt.md),
[plasma.md](plasma.md), [scrap.md](scrap.md) and the guns in [assassinorum.md](assassinorum.md) (said once at the top
of each file, not on each entry). Launchers that fire one round a beat (Six Bells) gain nothing from it.

## Bayonet Charge

**Any gun with a fixed bayonet** (set by the player, day 47). The bayonet also makes the gun a melee weapon: no
swapping; in melee **a two-handed spear thrust, chains of 2, +1 to strike first** ([Bayonet](kit.md#bayonet)).
1. **Sprint into melee and thrust: +2 on the first stab.** On a hit, **the target is knocked back a step and loses
   his next swing.**
2. **Then one shot, point blank** (1-10 m: the easiest band). **One shot, not the string;** it can come before the
   thrust (on the way in) or **after it, into him.** (No Precision Sacrifice: there's no string to give up.)
- Cooldown: 3 beats, like every ability.

**Items with it:** [Bayonet](kit.md#bayonet) (any rifle with a lug, a combat knife fixed on one); [Underslung
chain-bayonet](kit.md#underslung-chain-bayonet) (it thrusts as a chain weapon). **Enemy kits** with a 'bayonet
(fixed)' have it. **Owned in Ash on Ostrava:** **Vigil** has a bayonet lug; **Tamsin's scorched-tip combat knife** (the Kasr Holn
knife) fixes on it when he wants it (fixing or unfixing takes the beat's move, DM default) (campaign sheet).

## Fan the Hammer

**Revolvers only** (set by the player, day 47). The off hand slaps the hammer back again and again.
- **Fire every round left in the cylinder in one string** (up to 6), instead of the normal string.
- **-2 on every shot** (on top of recoil), and **no hit bonuses** in the string (misses still -1).
- **Both hands:** nothing in the other hand that beat (no dual-wielding with it).
- **Then it's empty:** the next beat is a reload (a speedloader: half a beat).
- Cooldown: 3 beats, like every ability.

**Items with it:** **stub revolvers** and any revolver with a hammer (said once at the top of [stub.md](stub.md)); not
the [Hammerfall auto-revolver](stub.md#auto-revolver-hammerfall-pattern), which already hammers its own cylinder, the
['Trinity' pocket revolver](stub.md#pocket-revolver-trinity-pattern) (double-action only, the hammer shrouded), or Six Bells (a launcher). **Owned in Ash on Ostrava:** **Corvane's revolver** (campaign sheet).

## Saw Lock

**Chain weapons** (set by the player, day 47). The teeth bind the other weapon.
- **When a block happens with his chain weapon** (his swing is blocked, or he blocks), he can lock it.
- **STR contest** (d20 + STR each):
  - **He wins:** the enemy's weapon is ripped away and lands ~2 m off; the enemy is unarmed until he spends a beat
    getting it back.
  - **He loses:** his chain is **jammed**. He **can still block and parry** with it, and **can hit with it like a
    blunt weapon** (a club with teeth: no chain bite, the chain's own rules are off), but nothing it does as a chain
    weapon works until he **spends a beat clearing it** (DM default; the player: jammed, not useless).
- **It ends the chain** either way.
- **Not against power weapons:** the field chews the teeth.
- Cooldown: 3 beats, like every ability.

**Items with it:** **every chain weapon** in [melee.md](melee.md) (chainswords, chain axes, chain knives, eviscerators,
chain glaives: said once at the top of that file), and the [underslung chain-bayonet](kit.md#underslung-chain-bayonet).
Enemies' chain weapons alike. **Owned in Ash on Ostrava:** Shameholder, Kell's chain axe, Tey's chain knives, and every
other chain weapon on the campaign sheet.

## Pinned Fire

**Automatic weapons** (set by the player, day 47; **named Pinned Fire, not 'suppressing fire'**, because Tamsin calls
'suppressing fire' as an order all the time). Anyone's version of Morcant's tripod ARC (schematic 3).
- **Spend the whole string spraying an area** (~5 m wide: a doorway, a corridor, a group behind a wall).
- **Everyone in it takes -1 on their shots next beat**, and **anyone who breaks cover in it draws one free shot.**
- **No damage rolls** for the string itself; it **burns double ammo.**
- The tripod ARC stays the stronger one (-2, and it rolls).
- Cooldown: 3 beats, like every ability.

**Items with it:** **heavy stubbers, heavy bolters and multi-lasers** (said once at the top of [heavy.md](heavy.md));
**las-LMGs** (the Kasr Rain, the Las-box: top of [las.md](las.md)); **autoguns and SMGs that fire full auto**
(machine pistols, submachine guns, autoguns with an auto setting: top of [stub.md](stub.md)). Enemies' alike.
**Owned in Ash on Ostrava:** Morcant's heavy stubber and multi-laser; Tamsin's Stinger and Columnus (campaign sheet).

## Cook Off

**Timed grenades** (frags, kraks, smoke, gas and the like; not impact grenades, which already burst on landing) (set
by the player, day 47).
- **Pull the pin, count, then throw:** it **bursts the moment it lands: no diving clear and no throwing it back**
  (as impact frags).
- **+1 on the throw** (the player: cooking it takes time, and that time lines the throw up properly).
- **The risk: on a natural 1-2 it goes off in his hand:** he and anyone within ~2 m drop a step (a krak: as its own
  rule).
- Cooldown: 3 beats, like every ability.

**Items with it:** **every timed grenade** (said once at the top of [explosives.md](explosives.md); the codex's
standard frag, krak and smoke too). Enemies' alike. **Owned in Ash on Ostrava:** every frag, krak and smoke the Light
carry (Tamsin's 3 frags and smoke, Hollis's frags, Tavi's kraks; Six Bells' rounds are launched, not cooked).

## Throw

**Knives, hatchets and other light blades not made for throwing** (set by the player, day 47; real throwing knives
and the [Fan throw](melee.md) keep their own rules).
- **Throw the melee weapon** out to ~10 m: **d20 + DEX at -2** (it isn't balanced for it).
- **It hits like the blade does**, and **STR 50+ still drives it a step further** (thrown weapons count: codex
  Part 8, Strength behind the blow).
- **Then it's gone:** it lies where it landed until someone picks it up; he's empty-handed of it.
- Cooldown: 3 beats, like every ability.

**Items with it:** **every light blade** in [melee.md](melee.md) (said once at the top of that file): combat knives,
mono-knives, power knives, chain knives, shanks, hatchets and hand axes. Enemies' alike. **Owned in Ash on Ostrava:**
Tamsin's scorched-tip knife, Kell's Schola fighting knife, Varga's power knife, Brenner's mono-knife, Tey's chain knives
and every other knife on the campaign sheet.

## Field Burst

**Power weapons** (set by the player, day 47). He dumps the whole field into one swing.
- **If it lands, it drops one extra step**, and the flash **knocks the target back a step and costs him his next
  swing.**
- **It ends the chain** (and, like every ability, it's his one ability that beat: no Heavy Attack on the same swing).
- **Then the field is off for the next beat:** the weapon cuts like a plain blade (through flak only) until it comes
  back.
- Cooldown: 3 beats, like every ability.

**Items with it:** **every power weapon** in [melee.md](melee.md) (said once at the top of that file): power swords,
axes, mauls, knives, fists, thunder hammers, relic blades. Enemies' alike. **Owned in Ash on Ostrava:** Junior
Commissar Varo's power sword (Tamsin's off weapon), Corvane's power sword, Varga's and Nessa's power knives, and any
other power weapon on the campaign sheet.

## Trigger Finger

**Semi-automatic guns** (the player's design, day 47). He works the trigger as fast as the gun will cycle.
- **+1 on every shot of the string** (the hair trigger's buff, on every shot; it doesn't stack with a fitted hair
  trigger's first-shot +1: DM default).
- **+2 shots to the string** (Vigil's 4 becomes 6; a string of 3 becomes 5).
- **A miss costs -2 in the chain, not -1**, and **+1 recoil** (each shot after the first takes -1 more).
- Cooldown: 3 beats, and it's his one ability that beat.

**Items with it** (DM default, the player may change it): **guns that fire one round a pull and cycle themselves:**
every las gun fired single (pistols, rifles, carbines, hellguns, long-las), stub automatics, autopistols and autoguns
on single, semi-auto shotguns, bolt pistols and boltguns. **Not:** manual actions (lever, pump, bolt-action,
break-action), revolvers (they have [Fan the Hammer](#fan-the-hammer)), guns that only fire full auto (machine pistols,
SMGs, LMGs), plasma (heat), heavy weapons, launchers, flamers, melta. Said once at the top of [las.md](las.md),
[stub.md](stub.md) and [bolt.md](bolt.md). Enemies' alike. **Owned in Ash on Ostrava:** Tamsin's Vigil, Last Word and
the Columnus (on single), and every las gun the Light carry.

## Slam (push-pull)

**Pump shotguns** (the player's design, day 47). He slams the pump as fast as his arms go.
- **+1 shot to the string.**
- **-2 on every shot** (accuracy).
- **No recoil** that string.
- **But a natural 5 or below on any shot die jams the gun** (clearing takes a beat: codex Part 8, Gun condition and
  jams).
- Cooldown: 3 beats, and it's his one ability that beat.

**Items with it:** **every pump-action shotgun** (the Munitorum pump, combat pumps, the CY-6 and the like; said once at
the top of [stub.md](stub.md)); the [Lucius 'Ironclad'](stub.md#lucius-pattern-ironclad-shotgun) (its reinforced
action only jams on a natural 1-3); the ['Harrow' farmer's pump](stub.md#pump-shotgun-harrow-pattern). Not semi-autos (the Kasr Sweeper: [Trigger Finger](#trigger-finger)), break-actions or
drum guns. Enemies' alike. **Owned in Ash on Ostrava:** the pump shotguns in the crate (and Morcant's old combat pump).
