# Scrap guns

Handmade guns of the hive gangs, cultists and raiders (player request, Ash on Ostrava
session 9). General rules (gun condition and jams, drop-prone, going Down) are in
DND_CODEX Part 8, **Gear rules**. **Scrap guns are always at least Dirty** (they never
count as Clean). **Who carries them:** poor, basic groups of cultists,
raiders and pirates; elite teams and named special teams don't.

## Pipe pistol ("zip gun")

**Pipe pistol** (common, 0.25 TV; black market; anyone with a workbench builds one in
an hour). Water pipe, a spring and a nail for a firing pin, wrapped in tape. **One stub
round, loaded by hand: one shot, then a beat to reload.** Stub-pistol punch (good on
flesh, poor on flak). **-2 past ~10 m.** **Scrap quirk:** on a natural 1 it jams, and a
d6 of 1 means it **bursts in the shooter's hand** (they drop a health step). Carried by
poor cultists, gang kids and prisoners: the thing in the boot.

## Nailer (scrap scattergun)

**Nailer** (common, 0.5 TV; black market). A pipe barrel welded to a gas canister; the
muzzle is stuffed with nails, bolts, glass and gravel, and a spark fires it. **One
blast, then a beat to repack.** **Wide spread:** within ~5 m it hits **up to 2 people
standing together**, vicious on flesh; **useless against carapace, and flak stops most
of it. -3 past ~10 m.** **Scrap quirk:** on a natural 1 the canister leaks, and a d6 of
1 sets it alight on the shooter. **Ammo is junk, so the carrier always has it:** a
looted nailer-user **always carries a bag of scrap** (nails, bolts, gravel, a spare gas
canister), which counts as scrap salvage. Carried by gangers, sump scum and cultist
mobs that rush in close.

## Rivet gun (converted industrial riveter)

**Rivet gun** (uncommon, 1.5 TV; black market). A manufactorum rivet driver with the
safety ripped out, fed by a hose from a pressure tank on the back; punches hot steel
rivets. **Semi-auto, chains of up to 3, 20 rivets in a hopper.** **Within ~15 m it hits
like a heavy stub round:** through flak on any hit, through carapace on a solid hit;
**past ~15 m, -3 and a weak hit.** **Scrap quirk:** a hit on the carrier, on a d6 of 1,
cuts the hose or bursts the tank, and the gun is dead for the fight. **Loud and
hissing:** no stealth. Carried by ex-manufactorum workers, gang breachers and raider
heavies; everywhere on a hive world.

## Torch-thrower (scrap flamer)

**Torch-thrower** (common, 1 TV; black market). A welder's torch, a fuel line and a
promethium can strapped to the back: the underhive's flamer. **A short gout, ~5 m,
hitting everyone in a narrow line; targets catch fire** (they drop a step next beat
unless they spend it putting themselves out). **4 bursts a can.** **Scrap quirk:** a
hit on the carrier, on a d6 of **1-2, ruptures the can**: the carrier goes up, and
anyone within ~2 m. Carried by cultist purgers, sump gangs and anyone clearing a room
on the cheap.

## Sump harpoon (spear-gun)

**Sump harpoon** (uncommon, 1 TV; black market). A spring-and-cable launcher built
from a vehicle suspension coil, firing a heavy barbed steel spear on a chain; sump
hunters use it on the big things in the waste pools. **One spear, then a beat to
rewind and re-cock.** **Silent:** no flash, no bang. **It hurts:** a hit goes through
flak. **Only a hit that goes through the target pins them:** the shooter can yank them
a few metres (out of cover, off a ledge) or anchor them in place. **Getting free:**
the target or an ally **cuts the cable** (a beat; frees them, no extra harm); tearing
the spear out instead drops them a step. **Scrap quirk:** on a natural 1 the spring
fails and the spear **drops out and sticks in the deck at the shooter's feet**; they
spend the next beat in the open wrenching it free and reattaching it (attacks on them
that beat get +2). Carried by sump hunters, gang trappers and raiders taking hostages.

## Battery las ("car-battery lasgun")

**Battery las** (uncommon, 2 TV; black market). A salvaged lasgun with its pack ripped
out, cabled to a vehicle battery in a backpack: crude, and it hits far harder than it
should. **Hits like a hellgun; one shot a beat; 15 shots a battery.** **Heat:** each
shot adds 1; at 3 it vents for a beat. **Scrap quirk:** on a natural 1 the wiring arcs
and **shocks the shooter** (they drop a step and lose their next beat). Counts one step
cleaner for jams (las), but never better than Dirty. Carried by gang tech-heads,
cultist "prophets" and anyone who can't afford a real hellgun.

## Pipe-bomb mortar ("tube lobber")

**Pipe-bomb mortar** (common, 0.75 TV; bombs 0.1 TV each or homemade; black market). A
wide pipe on a bipod bent from a chair, with a spark charge in the bottom: drop in a
pipe-bomb and it arcs over cover. **Open ground only:** it needs open sky (outdoors,
open land); **anywhere with a ceiling it can't arc and won't work.** **One lob a beat,
over walls and cover, out to ~40 m; hits like a frag** on everyone within ~3 m of
where it lands. **Wild aim: -2;** a miss lands it d6 m off in a random direction.
**Scrap quirk:** on a natural 1 the bomb **stays in the tube, fizzing, for a beat**;
the crew dives clear or someone brave tips it out. **Crew:** two (aimer and loader);
alone, one lob every other beat. Carried by gang artillery, cultist siege mobs and
raiders hitting a fortified door.

## Bolt-pipe ("one-shot boomer")

**Bolt-pipe** (uncommon, 1 TV; bolt rounds rare and dear in the underhive; black
market). A thick pipe sized for **one scavenged bolt round**, with a door-hinge hammer.
**One shot; hits like a boltgun;** reloading takes a full beat, if there's another
round. **Brutal recoil: -2 on the shot**, and a shooter with STR under 20 drops it.
**Scrap quirk:** on a natural 1 the pipe **splits**: the shot goes wild and the shooter
drops a step from the shrapnel. Carried by gang bosses showing off and desperate
cultists saving it for "the one who matters".

## Pipe rifle

**Pipe rifle** (common, 0.5 TV; black market). A long water pipe on a wooden stock with a
door-latch bolt: the commonest gun in any hive gang or cult mob. **Bolt-action, 5
rounds, chains of up to 2;** stub-rifle punch (through flak on a solid hit). **Fine to
~30 m; -2 past that** (the barrel isn't true). **Scrap quirk:** on a natural 1 it **falls
apart** in the shooter's hands: out of the fight. Carried by every second grunt in a
gang or a cult.

## Rattler (stamped scrap SMG)

**Rattler** (common, 0.75 TV; black market). Folded sheet metal, a sewing-machine spring
and a stick magazine welded in at an angle: loud, ugly and fast; gang workshops crank
them out by the dozen. **Full-auto, 20-round stick magazine, chains of up to 5, no hit
bonuses** (misses still -1). Autopistol punch (good on flesh, poor on flak). **-1 on
every shot past ~15 m.** **Scrap quirk:** on a natural 1 it **runs away**: the spring
sticks and it dumps the whole magazine in a random direction; anyone near the shooter
ducks. Carried by gang runners, cult mobs and raider boarding grunts.

## Junk shotgun ("double-pipe")

**Junk shotgun** (common, 0.5 TV; black market). Two pipes side by side on a plank, each
with its own nail-striker, and a shared pull-string trigger. **Two barrels:** fire one,
or **both at once** (one shot that lands as two hits up close); then **a beat to reload
both.** Loose stub shot: **brutal within ~8 m**, good on flesh, flak stops most of it;
**-3 past ~15 m.** **Scrap quirk:** on a natural 1 **both barrels fire together** (even
if one was meant) and the kick knocks the shooter flat; they spend the next beat
getting up. Carried by door guards, gang enforcers and cultists holding a chokepoint.
