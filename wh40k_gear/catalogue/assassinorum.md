# Officio Assassinorum gear

Item rules for this category. General rules (heat, hot-shot, drop-prone, going Down, ammo crates) are in DND_CODEX Part 8, **Gear rules**.

The Imperium's temple assassins carry gear made for one kill and no witnesses.
**All of it is epic or legendary** (player-set): **epic** is the exotic tier
(natural-20 finds only); **legendary** is a natural 20, then a second d20 of
18-20. Sold only by the black market and Free Traders, if at all. **Dangerous to
own:** the Officio Assassinorum wants its gear back, and anyone holding it has
questions to answer, an Interrogator included. Already in the pool and not
repeated here: the power sword (Eversor) and the melta bomb.

## Vindicare Temple: Exitus rifle

**Exitus rifle** (legendary, 150 TV): a custom long-range kinetic sniper rifle with
micro-sensors that correct for heat, air pressure and gravity. **+3 on aimed
shots, and no penalty for range, wind or weather.** One shot a beat; each hits
like a boltgun. On a **raw 18+ aimed shot, a human target goes Down.** Takes
Exitus rounds (below) and its own standard rounds.

## Vindicare Temple: Exitus pistol

**Exitus pistol** (epic, 60 TV): the rifle's matched close-quarters sidearm.
Hits like a bolt pistol, **+1 shooting, and silent** (built-in silencer). Takes
Exitus rounds. **Self-destruct:** whoever finds one rolls a d20 the first time
they pick it up; on 1-5 it burns out in their hand and is useless.

## Vindicare Temple: Exitus rounds

**Exitus rounds** (epic, 10 TV each; Exitus rifle and pistol only). One round
replaces one shot:
- **Shield-breaker:** ignores shields, refractor and conversion fields, and warp
  wards; the field or ward overloads and is down for the beat.
- **Turbo-penetrator:** goes through carapace and through half or three-quarters
  cover as if it weren't there.
- **Hellfire:** bio-acid toxin. On a hit the target also makes a CON save or drops
  one more health step.

## Callidus Temple: Phase sword

**Phase sword** (legendary, 200 TV): a C'tan phase blade that slips through matter.
**Ignores armour, and can't be blocked by a shield** (a weapon parry still works).
Cuts through a door or a thin wall in a beat. Xenos-made: an Imperial who carries
it openly is a heretic in most eyes.

## Callidus Temple: Poison blades

**Poison blades** (epic, 50 TV): concealed blades coated with fast-acting toxin.
**Hidden on a search** unless the searcher wins a Perception check. Any hit also
forces a CON save or the target drops a health step.

## Callidus Temple: Neural shredder

**Neural shredder** (legendary, 180 TV): a short-range energy weapon that fries the
nervous system. A cone about 10 m long; **ignores armour.** Everyone in it makes
a CON save or **drops two health steps**. Does nothing to servitors, machines or
daemons.

## Eversor Temple: Executioner pistol

**Executioner pistol** (legendary, 150 TV): a bolt pistol and a neuro-toxin needler
in one. Each beat fire the **bolt** (hits like a bolt pistol), the **needle**
(silent; CON save or drop a health step), or **both at -2**.

## Eversor Temple: Neuro gauntlet

**Neuro gauntlet** (legendary, 160 TV): a close-combat shock gauntlet that injects
neurotoxins and burns the target from the inside. A hit **stuns for a beat** and
forces a CON save or the target **drops two health steps**.

## Culexus Temple: Animus speculum

**Animus speculum** (legendary, 250 TV): a head-mounted device that channels a
pariah's void-dread and fires it back. **Works only for a pariah (a blank, with no
soul in the warp).** For one: a bolt that hits psykers and daemons like a
lascannon, and no psyker within ~10 m can use a power. For anyone else it does
nothing; wearing it, make a WIS save each day or lose sleep to dread.

## Culexus Temple: Psyk-out grenades

**Psyk-out grenades** (epic, 20 TV each): anti-psychic grenades. A burst about 5 m
across: **psykers in it can't use powers for 2 turns**, powers already running or
charging there end (wards drop, charges break), and daemons in it take a hit.

## Adamus Temple: Needlespine blaster

**Needlespine blaster** (legendary, 140 TV): a precision needle weapon. **Silent, +2
shooting at range**, goes through flak and mesh, and a hit forces a CON save or
the target drops a health step.

## Adamus Temple: Nemesii blade

**Nemesii blade** (legendary, 150 TV): a precision duelling blade. **+2 to strike
first and to parry**, and through carapace on a raw 17+.

## Venenum Temple: Toxin injectors and poison globes

**Venenum toxin kit** (legendary, 120 TV): injectors and poison globes.
**Injector:** in melee, a hit delivers a dose: CON save or drop a health step.
**Poison globes:** thrown, slipped into food or water, or broken into the air; slow
and hard to trace. The target makes a CON save each hour or drops a health step,
until treated with concentrated antitoxin.
