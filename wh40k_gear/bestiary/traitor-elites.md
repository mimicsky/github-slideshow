# Traitor elite special teams

Fallen companies of the Imperium's most famous regiments: rolled as a traitor
elite special team (2d20 33+). The regiments are loyal; these companies broke.

## The Lost 88th Cadian (fallen company)

A Cadian company that broke when Cadia fell and decided the Emperor had failed
them. Professional, bitter, and very good. **Tamsin is Cadian; these are his own.**
- **Look:** Cadian green fatigues and carapace, kasr numbers scratched out, the
  white gate painted black.
- **Weapons:** Kantrael lasguns, hellguns, plasma guns, frags and kraks.
- **Tactics:** Cadian doctrine: fire teams, overwatch, flanking, combined fire. They
  know every Cadian trick, including the Light's.

## The Unburied, Death Korps of Krieg (fallen company)

A Krieg company that lost the war in its head and kept fighting it for someone
else. Krieg almost never fall; these are the exception, and it shows.
- **Look:** greatcoats, gas masks, spiked helmets, the death's-head; silent.
- **Weapons:** Lucius lasguns with bayonets, hot-shot volley guns, heavy flamers,
  gas, mortars.
- **Tactics:** attrition. They dig in, gas the ground, and advance through fire as if
  they can't die. They never surrender and never talk.

## The Sand-Ghosts, Tallarn (fallen company)

Desert raiders who turned, and kept every skill they had.
- **Look:** sand-coloured robes and headscarves over light carapace, goggles.
- **Weapons:** Mezoa long-las, lascarbines, meltaguns, curved knives.
- **Tactics:** mobile and patient: they snipe from range, hit with melta on the
  move, vanish and come back from another side. Never fight them where they choose.

## The Disowned, Vostroyan Firstborn (fallen company)

Firstborn sons of an old forge-world, disowned by their families when they turned.
Proud, ornate, and carrying the best family heirlooms.
- **Look:** red-and-gold coats, tall fur hats, huge moustaches, ornate brass armour.
- **Weapons:** heirloom Kantrael lasguns, boltguns, hellguns, sabres.
- **Tactics:** formal and stubborn: volley fire from good positions, officers leading
  from the front with bolt pistol and sabre. Kill the officer and they waver.

## The Green Hell deserters, Catachan (fallen company)

Catachan jungle fighters gone renegade: the deadliest light infantry the Guard
ever made, now working for whoever pays.
- **Look:** sleeveless fatigues, red bandanas, huge knives, muscles, face paint.
- **Weapons:** Kantrael lasguns, flamers, heavy stubbers, Catachan fang knives,
  booby traps.
- **Tactics:** ambush specialists: traps, tripwires, hidden positions, then a short
  violent fight with knives. In the dark or the jungle, assume they're already
  around you.
