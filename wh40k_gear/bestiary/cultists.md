# Cult special teams

Named cult forces, rolled as a cultist special team (2d20 31+). Each follows one
god; blessed members show that god's marks (catalogue, Blessed cultists).

## The Moebian 6th

**Nurgle.** Once a loyal Guard regiment, the Moebian 6th rotted from the inside:
their chaplains preached Grandfather's mercy to men dying of a plague the
Munitorum never cured. Now they spread it on purpose.
- **Look:** green-grey Guard fatigues gone black with damp, gas masks fused to faces,
  sores weeping through the cloth, flies everywhere.
- **Weapons:** rusted lasguns, Columnus autoguns, braced autoguns, stolen flamers,
  plague-gas bottles.
- **Tactics:** they don't charge; they hold and grind. Gas first, then slow,
  steady fire that pins you while their sick walk forward through your shots.
  They foul the air and the water before a fight.
- **Poxwalker swarms** (added by the player, Ash on Ostrava session 10): they drive swarms of
  poxwalkers ahead of them, and infuse themselves with the rot, yet still move like a real
  regiment. Whoever fights them rarely comes out well.
- **Poxwalkers (DM default, Ash on Ostrava session 10; the player may change it):** the plague-dead
  the 6th drive ahead of them, people rotted into a moaning swarm. **Slow:** about 5 m a beat, never
  sprint. **No armour, no guns:** claws, teeth, old tools. **One health state:** any hit drops one,
  but they're **disgustingly resilient**: a hit that isn't solid (and isn't a crit or fire) gets back
  up next beat on a d6 6 (roll the batch). **Mindless:** no break checks, never surrender. **In
  contact,** the swarm makes one attack a beat on each person it reaches (d20 +2): a hit drops a step
  unless armour stops claws (carapace does, like most blades; flak doesn't), and **a bite that lands
  carries the rot** (CON save vs 13, masks don't help, or sickened). A crowd stays narrative: no
  exchange per body. Usually driven by a herder or two of the 6th behind them.

## The Volscani Cataphracts

**Chaos Undivided.** A traitor regiment of heavy infantry who sold themselves to
the Ruinous Powers as a whole and kept their discipline.
- **Look:** dark iron-grey plate over flak, full helms with the eight-pointed star
  cut into the visor, red cloth strips tied to every weapon.
- **Weapons:** stolen hellguns, semi-auto shotguns, Lucius lasguns.
- **Tactics:** drilled soldiers: fire-and-move by squads, grenades before every
  door, a reserve kept back to hit a flank. They fight like Guard, just damned.

## The Blood Pact

**Khorne.** A warhost sworn in blood to a warlord; every trooper cut their palm and
pledged their life. Captured Blood Pact never talk: they bite their tongues out.
- **Look:** red-black fatigues, iron grotesque masks with snarling faces, scarred
  palms, trophies of Guard tags.
- **Weapons:** Kantrael lasguns, autoguns, pump shotguns, bayonets always fixed.
- **Tactics:** brutal and disciplined: they close fast under covering fire and
  finish it with bayonets. They never retreat; wounded men fight on.

## The Sons of Sek

**Khorne.** Fanatics raised under the Anarch Sek, bloodier and less disciplined than
the Blood Pact: the warhost's attack dogs.
- **Look:** crude red paint over stolen Guard gear, shaved heads branded with Sek's
  sigil, chains wound round the arms.
- **Weapons:** autoguns, Lucius lasguns, heavy stubbers, cleavers and chain axes.
- **Tactics:** waves. They come all at once, screaming, berserkers in front, and
  they don't care how many fall. Make them come through a doorway.

## The Prospero Spireguard

**Tzeentch.** Guards of a sorcerer's spire, drilled by visions rather than sergeants.
They know things they shouldn't.
- **Look:** blue-and-gold lacquered armour, high crested helms, feathers and
  turquoise beads, the number nine painted everywhere.
- **Weapons:** blue-lacquered lasguns, stolen hellguns, plasma pistols, often a
  witch with a staff.
- **Tactics:** they're where you're going before you get there. Ambushes, traps,
  feints; a squad shows itself so you chase it into a crossfire. Expect a psyker.

## The Adorned

**Slaanesh.** Once nobles' house guards, now the jewelled killers of a pleasure-cult.
Vain, cruel and very good.
- **Look:** silks over mesh armour, painted faces, gold piercings, perfume you smell
  before you see them, a tentacle-finger or two.
- **Weapons:** needle rifles, duelling laspistols, shotlas scatterguns, elegant
  blades.
- **Tactics:** precise and personal. They pick off officers and medics first, then
  duel the best fighter they can find. They show off, which can be used.

## The Cohort of the Enraptured

**Slaanesh.** The Adorned's frenzied cousins: dancers, screamers, drugged out of their
minds and in love with pain.
- **Look:** half-naked, ritually scarred, lilac paint, bells and chains, sweet purple
  mist around them.
- **Weapons:** shotlas scatterguns, lascarbines, hand flamers, whips and knives,
  stacks of combat drugs.
- **Tactics:** loud, fast and close: they rush in with drugs running and fight like
  they feel nothing. Lots of berserkers. Gas and flame make them worse, not better.
