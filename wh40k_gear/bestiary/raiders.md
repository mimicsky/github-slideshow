# Raider special teams

Named raider gangs, rolled as a raider special team (2d20 33+).

## Renegade Void-Raiders

Hit-and-run crews who strike stations and outposts, strip them and leave.
- **Look:** void-suits, mag boots, light flak, tinted visors.
- **Weapons:** lascarbines, semi-auto shotguns, heavy laspistols.
- **Tactics:** speed: in, grab, out. They cut power and air first, fight in the dark,
  and won't hold ground they don't need.

## Crangor's Buccaneers

The band of a famous raider-chief, Crangor, who pays well and hangs cowards.
- **Look:** gaudy stolen finery over armour, gold teeth, Crangor's crossed-axes
  badge.
- **Weapons:** Hammerfall stub revolvers, ripper guns, hand cannons, axes.
- **Tactics:** loud and greedy: they push hard at the start, and they fight harder
  near loot. A dead lieutenant often breaks the rest.

## The Fallen Maelstrom Wardens

Once guards of the Maelstrom's edge, they went over to the pirates they were set to
watch. They know every hiding place in the region.
- **Look:** worn grey Warden armour with the old badge scratched off, long coats,
  auspex rigs.
- **Weapons:** hellguns, stolen boltguns, Kasr Warden vigilante autoguns.
- **Tactics:** patient hunters: they track you, pick the ground and open with
  marksmen. Good at void and station fighting.

## Chaos Cultist Scavengers

Cult-raiders who strip wrecks and colonies for their masters, half pirate and half
cultist.
- **Look:** rags over scavenged armour, cult marks, chalk gates, scrap jewellery.
- **Weapons:** drum scrap autoguns, hive scatterguns, chain-fed autopistols, cult
  gas bottles.
- **Tactics:** swarm and loot: lots of them, poorly drilled, but they bring a witch or
  a grimoire more often than other raiders do.
