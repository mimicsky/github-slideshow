# Traitor soldier special teams

Named renegade Guard forces, rolled as a traitor special team (2d20 33+).

## The Vraksian Renegade Militia

The rebel militia of Vraks, the armoury world that turned. Huge numbers, cheap
kit, and more guns than they know what to do with.
- **Look:** red-brown fatigues, flak, gas masks, the rebel cardinal's sign painted
  over the aquila.
- **Weapons:** Kantrael lasguns, autoguns, heavy stubbers, mortars; the armoury
  world's leftovers.
- **Tactics:** trenches and numbers: dig in, layer fire, call the mortars, and let
  you walk into it. Out of their lines they're far weaker.

## The Renegade 14th Volpone 'Bluebloods'

A company of the proud, aristocratic Volpone regiment that followed a disgraced
colonel into treason. They still think they're better than you.
- **Look:** immaculate pale-blue and gold uniforms, polished carapace, officers'
  sabres, a sneer.
- **Weapons:** Accatran lasguns, hellguns, Mezoa long-las, officers' bolt pistols.
- **Tactics:** textbook: proper fire lines, long-range marksmen, a reserve. Arrogant:
  they underestimate a smaller force and over-commit.

## The Urdeshi Deserter Storm Troops

Storm troopers from the forge-world Urdesh who deserted to a traitor warlord, taking
their kit and their training.
- **Look:** grey-green storm-trooper carapace, red visor lenses, Mechanicus-pattern
  wargear and good boots.
- **Weapons:** compact hellguns, Kasr Duet twin-focus lasguns, semi-auto shotguns,
  hot-shot packs.
- **Tactics:** fast assault troops: grav-chute drops, breaching charges, room
  clearing in pairs. They hit hard and leave before you can bring the heavies.
