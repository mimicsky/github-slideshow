# Commoners (militia)

(player-set, Ash on Ostrava day 45.) Ordinary people who picked up whatever was to hand: farmers, dockers,
hab-workers, miners, grox-herders, a town's self-made militia. They can be **enemies** (a town turned by a
cult, or a mob the bought officials set on you), **allies** (a town defending itself), or **neither** (frightened
people with guns). Rolled with `../roll_enemy.py commoner COUNT [WEALTH]`.

- **Wealth:** **Poor** (most), **Steady**, or **Good** in rich places; **never past Good.** Roll it with
  `../roll_enemy.py wealth commoner` (1-50 Poor, 51-90 Steady, 91-100 Good).
- **Stats:** civilians: **STR 10-30, DEX 10-25.** Guns are kept **Dirty**.
- **No named special teams.** Their specialists are local: the headman, the lay healer, the hunter, the herder
  with his prod, the big farmhand, the preacher, the man with the farm hounds, the one who knows the old PDF
  armoury.
- **Look:** work clothes and padded jackets, a few old PDF flak vests, family charms, mud to the knee.
- **Weapons:** hunting rifles, pump and scrap shotguns, stub revolvers, pipe rifles, old militia autoguns; farm
  tools, machetes, scrap axes, grox-prods. A lucky few have a PDF lasgun or grandfather's laspistol.
- **Tactics:** they fight for **their own ground**: barricades, walls, the farmhouse, the chapel. They know every
  ditch and hedge, so on home ground they get the first shot. They don't advance into fire and they don't
  chase far.
- **Brittle:** not soldiers. **When a third of them are down, or their leader falls, roll a d20 for the group:
  1-10 they break** (run, or throw down their guns and surrender). A preacher in the group makes it **1-6**.
- **They can be talked down.** Persuasion, Intimidation or a show of the Inquisition's rosette works on them
  where it wouldn't on cultists: **+2** to the roll if they're already losing, **-2** if you've hurt one of their
  own. Many would rather be on the right side.
