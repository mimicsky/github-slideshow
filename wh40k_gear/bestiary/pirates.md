# Pirate special teams

Named void-pirate crews, rolled as a pirate special team (2d20 35+).

## Renegade Privateers

Letters of marque gone bad: a ship and crew that stopped hunting pirates and
became them.
- **Look:** faded Navy greatcoats, mismatched flak, boarding helms, captain's
  feathers.
- **Weapons:** semi-auto shotguns, Hammerfall long-barrel revolvers, lascarbines,
  cutlasses.
- **Tactics:** boarding experts: breach, flood the corridor, take the bridge. They'd
  rather capture a ship than destroy it.

## The Kanak Skull Takers (renegade)

A feral-world warband turned pirate, collecting skulls from every crew they take.
- **Look:** war paint, bone necklaces, skulls on belts and helmets, scraps of looted
  armour.
- **Weapons:** autoguns, pump shotguns, drum autoguns, big knives and axes.
- **Tactics:** ambush and terror: they hide in vents and dark holds, then charge
  screaming. They take heads afterward, which gives you a moment.

## The Savlar Chem-Dog deserters

Convict soldiers from a prison world, deserted with everything they could steal.
Expert looters, addicts of every chem there is.
- **Look:** patched stolen Guard gear, rebreathers always on, chem-inhalers,
  pockets bulging with loot.
- **Weapons:** patched lasguns, autoguns, sawn-offs, whatever they took last week.
- **Tactics:** dirty fighters: gas, chems, traps, shooting from behind the wounded.
  They loot mid-fight, and they run when it goes bad.

## Red Corsair reavers

The human crews that serve Huron Blackheart's Red Corsairs, the renegade Space
Marines. Not Astartes themselves, but trained, armed and terrified of their masters.
- **Look:** red-and-black armour with the clawed-hand sigil, tattooed faces, chains.
- **Weapons:** stolen boltguns, semi-auto shotguns, hellguns.
- **Tactics:** disciplined raiders: fast, organised, no mercy. If they're here, a real
  Red Corsair may not be far off.
