# Vehicles

How a vehicle becomes a real thing in a fight (set by the player, Ash on Ostrava day 47). Vehicles are
**assets**, not roles: they're added to a force like beasts or walkers, come with their own crew, and only
heavy hits hurt them.

## When they show up

Rolled by the DM when the force is set (hidden), by the **mission type** (codex Part 8, Enemy numbers and
tactics), unless the story already puts one there (a truck in a yard, a patrol buggy):

| Mission type | Vehicles (d6) |
| --- | --- |
| **Stealth or small** | none, unless the story puts one there |
| **Kill-team mission** | **6:** one light |
| **Regular battle** | **4-5:** one light; **6:** an APC |
| **Whole-Light fight** | **1-2:** one light; **3-4:** an APC and one light; **5-6:** an APC and a **tank** |

**Wealth changes what they are:**
- **Poor** (cultists, raiders): technicals (an armed truck), buggies, scrap walkers; their "APC" is an
  armoured hauler, their "tank" a scrap-plated crawler with a heavy weapon welded on.
- **Middle** (traitor Guard, pirates): Sentinels, Tauroxes, Chimeras, the odd Leman Russ.
- **Rich** (traitor elites): Taurox Primes, Chimeras, Leman Russ, and in a whole-Light fight **air** (a
  gunship).

A vehicle comes with its crew (a driver and gunners); **an APC carries a squad of up to 10, who count in the
force's numbers.**

| **APC** (Chimera, Taurox, armoured hauler) | **5** | **Heavy:** only kraks, melta, plasma, autocannons and lascannons, **Kraken bolts** (catalogue bolt.md, day 47), demo charges, melta bombs, the ship's turret, the Valk's rockets. **Shots at a vehicle get +2** (a big target; a Sentinel +1).

## Hurting it

- A hit that gets through its armour takes **1 hull point**. A **solid hit or a crit** also rolls the **damage
  d6:** **1-2** crew shaken (-2 on all its shots next beat); **3** a weapon destroyed; **4** immobilised;
  **5** a crewman down (that gun stops until someone takes his place); **6** on fire (+1 hull point now and
  another each beat until put out).
- **An Astartes melta canister** in a melta gun: a hit takes **2 hull points** (catalogue plasma.md, day 47).
- **A Mechanicus lascannon pack:** a hit takes **2 hull points** (catalogue heavy.md, day 47).
- **Autocannon penetrator shells** get through a **tank's side or rear** (catalogue heavy.md, day 47).
- **Meltacore bolts** get through a **tank's** armour from any side; **Kraken and vengeance bolts** get through an APC's
  (catalogue bolt.md, day 47).
- **Charges by hand** (in contact): **a melta bomb clamped on: 3 hull points, through any armour. A demo
  satchel or a breaching charge set on it: 2. A krak through a hatch or into the rear: 2.**
- **Open crews** (technicals, buggies, a Sentinel's open cab, a gunner up in a hatch) can be shot directly
  (+1, a small target): kill the gunner and that gun stops.
- **0 hull: wrecked.** A d6 6 and it **explodes** (everyone within ~5 m drops a step; each crewman inside
  rolls a d6, 1-3 dead). **A wreck is heavy cover (+4).**

## What it does

- **Moves and fires all its weapons every beat** (its crew's DEX; each weapon's recoil and string); a battle
  cannon fires once a beat.
- **Rams and runs over:** anyone in its path makes a **DEX dodge against the driver's roll**; a fail and
  they're knocked flat and drop a step (a tank: two).
- **An APC drops its squad** out of the back in one beat, then pulls back to cover them with its guns.
- **The enemy's commander uses it like a chess piece** (codex: the enemy plays smart): to cut off a retreat,
  to carry a squad round a flank, to stand off at range where small arms can't hurt it.
