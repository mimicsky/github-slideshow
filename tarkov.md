# How quests are shaped

Four different things get called "a two-map quest," and they plan very differently.

### 1. Chains — Part 1, Part 2, Part 3…

You can't start Part 2 until Part 1 is handed in. Parts often sit on different maps and
each part has its own rules. Examples: Chemical, Punisher, Huntsman Path, Gunsmith.

### 2. One quest, pieces on several maps

Like `Informed Means Armed` — a camera on Woods, a camera on Interchange. **You can do
these across separate raids, weeks apart.** No pressure, it's just trip planning.

### 3. Transit quests — two maps, one raid

You take the transit *instead of extracting*, so you can't split it across two sessions.
How it actually works:

- Play the first map as long as you want. Transit with a few minutes left.
- **You arrive with a fresh timer.** The second map is a full-length raid.
- **You can send normal items to your stash at the transit** — guns, armor, junk, hideout
  parts. Anything that takes up inventory space.
- **You CANNOT send quest items.** Those stay on you, and you still have to extract to
  keep them.

**The real cost of a transit is that you arrive with used gear.** Ammo is spent, meds are
used, grenades are gone, and after a long first leg your **food and water are empty**. You
can't restock from your hideout between legs.

> **So on any transit run: pack extra food and water, extra meds, and more ammo than the
> first map needs.** That's the whole reason transits are annoying, not the timer.

### 4. "Do it in one raid" quests

Some need everything done in a single raid and you must extract alive or it resets —
`I Need More Power` is the bad one. Others you can just mark and leave, or spread over
several raids, and dying doesn't matter.

**It varies per quest and there's no pattern.** Read the in-game text each time. Don't
trust me to guess which is which.

### Two more things that bite

- **Found-in-raid.** Handover items must be found in raid. You can't buy your way out.
  This is why the GPU problem below is real.
- **Mutually exclusive quests.** Some are a choice — finishing one kills the other. This
  already cost you `One Less Loose End`, which failed when you finished
  `A Healthy Alternative`. **Before finishing anything that reads like picking a side,
  check what it locks out.**

# Keep or sell

Higher rule wins.

1. **Quest item → KEEP.** Found-in-raid status can't be bought back. The money never
   covers the setback.
2. **Hideout material for something you're building → KEEP.**
3. **Barter input → KEEP** only if the barter beats buying it.
4. **Otherwise, money per slot decides.** A 1×1 worth 90k beats a 4×4 worth 200k.
5. **Where you sell matters** — trader for thin margins, flea when it covers the fee and
   the wait.
6. **Rare and unsure → keep one, sell the rest.**

**Never sell — active quest items:** Bulbex cable cutter · AK-12 · all WARTECH gear ·
every Booze drink type · every key.

**Standing sell:** spare low-tier armor past one · attachments that don't fit the AK-74M,
AKS-74U or Glock · ammo you don't run · bulky stuff under 10k a slot with no quest
attached.

**Mid-raid, just type the item name and a `?`.** You'll get GRAB, IF SPACE or SKIP and one
line of why. I don't have live flea prices, so if it's genuinely borderline on price I'll
say "your call" rather than invent a number. Anything on the quest list is always GRAB.
