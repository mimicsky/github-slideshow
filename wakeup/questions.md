## Section 3 — Reflection (not verifiable, but unfakeable asleep)

Works from day one with zero seeded facts. He must write 2+ real sentences.
A one-word answer or something generic does NOT pass — push back and re-ask.

- What's the first thing today you can't do from bed?
- Name one thing you're putting off. Why that one?
- What would make today count? Be specific, not "be productive."
- Who are you talking to today, and about what?
- What did you dream about? If nothing, what was the last thing on your mind last night?
- What's one thing you did yesterday you'd do differently?
- What are you actually worried about right now?
- Say something you've never told me before.

## Judging

- Two or more real sentences that answer the actual question = PASS.
- One word, "idk", "nothing", copy-paste, or dodging = FAIL. Ask a different one.
- A recall question from Section 1 or 2 answered wrong = FAIL. Ask two more.
- Do not accept an answer that could have been typed with eyes closed.
