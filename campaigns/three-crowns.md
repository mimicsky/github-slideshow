## The world

A medieval world with no single ruler. Three powers are grinding toward
domination and none of them can finish the job.

| Faction | Shorthand | What they are |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Concord | Knights | Armored cavalry under one crown and one faith. Centralized, disciplined, expanding outward from the heartland. Holds the most land and the fewest friends. |
| The Jarldoms | Vikings | Clans of the northern reach. No central authority, no capital to besiege. Wealth comes off the water. **The player's people.** |
| The Dominion | Samurai | Came across the eastern sea two generations ago and never left. Rigid hierarchy, superior steel, patient. Takes ground and keeps it. |

None of the three can beat the other two at once. Every war is really about
which two are briefly not fighting each other.

### Features

- **Rage** — bonus action, 3 per long rest, lasts 1 minute. While raging: +2
  damage on Strength melee attacks, resistance to bludgeoning, piercing and
  slashing damage, advantage on Strength checks and Strength saves. Cannot cast
  or concentrate on spells. Ends early if a turn passes with no attack made and
  no damage taken.
- **Unarmored Defense** — AC is 10 + DEX + CON while wearing no armor. A shield
  is still allowed.
- **Reckless Attack** — declare before the first attack of the turn. Advantage
  on all Strength melee attacks this turn; every attack against Hallvard has
  advantage until their next turn.
- **Danger Sense** — advantage on Dexterity saves against effects they can see.
- **Frenzy** (Berserker) — while raging, take a bonus-action melee weapon attack
  each turn. When the rage ends, gain one level of exhaustion. Competes with the
  off-hand attack for the same bonus action.

## Threads I'm tracking

DM-side. Open loops I owe a payoff on, and things in motion the player doesn't
know about yet.

- **What's in the bronze tube**, and why a forty-year Jarldoms man was carrying
  a sealed Concord dispatch hidden against his skin.
- **Who killed Sigvard.** Narrow clean thrust through the back. Not the rocks,
  not the sea. Whoever it was, it happened on or near the ship.
- **The Dominion bead** the boy tore off something as he died. All three
  factions are now present in one shipwreck.
- **The drag furrow.** Something heavy came out of the water and went inland.
- **The nine missing crew.** Some should be alive.
- **The smoke.** Steady, straight, windless.
- **The Dominion is collecting sealed bronze tubes** out of a burned Concord
  waystation. Hallvard is carrying one of those tubes. They do not know that yet.
- **Ketil is alive.** One of the nine accounted for.
- **The officer got away with the tubes.** He does not know Hallvard is carrying
  a seventh one taken off Sigvard's body. That is the campaign's main lever.
- **Eight crew were marched east up the road before dawn**, roped together.
- **Sigvard was running a courier job for the Concord**, not a raid. He told
  Ketil they would be paid on delivery.
- **The Dominion took this waystation two days ago and waited.** They knew the
  ship was coming.
- **A loose Dominion horse** is standing at the far corner of the yard, spooked
  but catchable.

## Session log

### Session 1 — 2026-09-11

**Opening:** shipwreck on a hostile shore at dawn.

**What happened:**

- Woke on the shingle beach below the headland. *Sea-Wolf* wrecked, Sigvard and
  a boy dead on the sand, nine crew unaccounted for.
- Searched the bodies. Took Sigvard's seax, waterskin and silver, and a sealed
  Concord dispatch tube he was wearing hidden under his tunic. Sigvard had been
  killed by a narrow thrust through the back, not by the sea.
- Took a snapped Dominion bead out of the dead boy's fist.
- Moved inland toward a column of smoke rather than following the drag furrow.
- Found a burned Concord waystation held by four Dominion soldiers, with Ketil
  bound and kneeling in the yard and a row of bronze tubes laid out on a cloak.
- Blew the stealth approach. Was spotted, refused to surrender, and held still
  while two soldiers swept the brush.
- Ambushed the sweepers and killed all three soldiers in the open ground. Took
  a bad forearm cut and a thigh wound doing it.
- The officer never engaged. He bundled the tubes, mounted, and rode east.
- A thrown axe dropped the last soldier short of the second horse, but the horse
  broke loose and the chance to pursue was gone.
- **Mid-session the table switched to narrative mode.** See house rule 4.

**What changed on the sheet:**

- Health: fine to badly hurt, then hurt after field dressing.
- Gained: seax, waterskin, sling, 11 sp, sealed bronze tube, Dominion bead.
- Three Dominion soldiers killed. One officer escaped.

**Unresolved:**

- Eight crew marched east. Where.
- What is inside the tube Hallvard is carrying.
- Whether to tell Ketil about the tube.
- The drag furrow on the beach, never followed.
- The loose horse.
