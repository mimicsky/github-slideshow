# DM thinking list (DM only)

**Not for the player** (the player, Ash on Ostrava day 48). He asked for this list and chose not to read it, so the
tricks come as surprises and he learns to counter them in play. The public rule is codex Part 8, Enemy numbers and
tactics, 3 (They play for their lives). Each faction's own flavour is its **Tactics** line in the bestiary; each role's
habits are in `wh40k_gear/bestiary/roles.md`. This list is how to think, beat by beat, on top of those.

**When to run it:**
- **Before every fight:** section 1, the enemy's plan. Write it in one line in the beat notes.
- **At the top of every beat:** section 2, the enemy commander's look. Change the plan when the Light changes the fight.
- **Before every NPC's roll, enemy or ally:** section 3, the eight questions. Allies also get section 4.
- **Every fight, at the end:** section 5, a quick honesty check.

## 1. Before the fight: the enemy's plan

- **What do they want?** Hold the ground, kill the commander, get the cargo or the prisoner out, buy time, escape.
- **What do they know?** Only what they've seen, heard or been told: sentries, vox traffic, noise, word of the Light's
  last fight. Never the DM's knowledge.
- **Where's the fallback?** A force that had time to prepare has a second position and a way out.
- **What's held back?** A reserve, a heavy weapon, a beast, a boss, a vox call. Decide when it comes in.
- **Where's the trap?** Ambushes cover the obvious route. Choke points get a grenade or a heavy weapon on them. Doors
  get rigged if they had the time.

## 2. Every beat: the enemy commander

- **Answer what the Light just did.** A flank: shift a team to face it. They dig in: grenades and smoke. A sniper: smoke
  the lane, move, counter-snipe. A push: fall back into a crossfire.
- **Fire and move.** One team pins (Pinned Fire, steady strings) while another moves. Never everyone moving at once.
- **Rotate.** A team losing its lane breaks contact (smoke, back out of sight) and comes back from somewhere else: a
  flank, above, behind. The Light should lose track of it.
- **Bait.** One man shows himself to draw fire into a crossfire. A man who looks dead may not be (cultists, fanatics).
- **Focus fire, don't spread it.** Targets, in order of chance: the one in the open, the one reloading or venting, the
  hurt one, the medic, the vox, the heavy gunner, Tamsin.
- **Flush them out.** Grenades, gas, flamers and mortars go into the cover the Light is using. Heavy weapons shoot
  through light cover.
- **Lights.** Kill the lights when their side knows the dark; flares and lumens when the Light hides in it.
- **Call for help** as soon as the fight turns (codex, Calling for help).
- **When it's lost:** fall back in order, surrender or run, by type. Fanatics fight on; conscripts and hired guns break;
  elites withdraw by squads, covering each other.

## 3. Every NPC, every beat: eight questions (enemies and allies)

1. **Am I in the open?** Get to cover first (the short move: 5 m plus Handling). End the beat in cover unless charging
   or forced out.
2. **Which weapon this beat?**
   - The one that reaches: a shotgun inside ~15 m if he has one; the scoped gun at range.
   - Against armour he can't get through: the weapon that can, or shoot the gaps.
   - **Moving a long way:** the weapon in hand with the best Handling, for the extra metres on the short move or run and
     gun.
   - **Charging with a blade and a pistol:** run and gun with the pistol, then the blade when he gets there. Nobody runs
     in empty-handed, or blade-only, past a gun he could be shooting on the way.
3. **Can I shoot without showing myself?** Against a better shooter: peek and duck (open for 2 shots, then gone), or
   blind fire. Reload, vent and clear jams in full cover.
4. **Who do I hit?** The section 2 targets. A melee fighter goes for the one who can't parry well: a gunner, the hurt.
5. **Can I get an angle?** Move to where the cover doesn't block (the side), to high ground, or through a door nobody
   is watching.
6. **What do I carry that fits?** Every gear ability (`wh40k_gear/catalogue/abilities.md`: Plant, Pinned Fire, Slam,
   Trigger Finger, Precision Sacrifice, Cook Off, Heavy Attack, Saw Lock, Field Burst, Spin-cock and the rest) and every
   grenade, stimm, smoke and charge. Use it when the moment fits. Track the cooldowns.
7. **Is my buddy in trouble?** Drag a downed friend into cover, patch him, cover a reloader, hold the door they're
   coming through.
8. **Am I losing?** Hurt and outgunned: pull back, call it in, surrender or run, by type.

## 4. Allies (the Light and friendly forces)

- **They think for themselves every beat**, by section 3, their role, their specialty, their traits and their standing
  orders (the sheet's Full kit rows: Kell's plasma pistol on armour, Morcant's Doorknocker inside ~15 m). Tamsin
  doesn't have to order cover, reloads, patches, target calls or weapon swaps.
- **His orders win, and his calls stay his.** Never decide for him: pulling the whole team out, prisoners, changing the
  mission. They shout it to him and wait for his word.
- **They talk:** contacts ("three, left, moving!"), reloads ("dry, reloading!"), grenades, who's hurt.
- **They guard the medic and Tamsin**, and don't bunch up where one grenade gets two of them.
- **Their mistakes are the ones their traits give them** (Jumpy, Reckless and so on), never plain stupid ones.

## 5. Keep it honest

- **The Light's shots face the real numbers:** cover (+1 / +2 / +4), moving targets, darkness, smoke, peek and duck
  (only 2 shots at them). An enemy who uses cover well is hard to hit; that's the point.
- **Mistakes fit the type.** Poor cultists bunch up, rush and forget cover; drilled troops don't. A conscript can
  freeze; a veteran never does.
- **Never soften, never pile on.** They play to win with what they have and what they know. The Light earns every kill.
- **Don't telegraph.** The player sees and hears what Tamsin does, not the plan.
- **Vary it.** When he counters a trick, they adapt; the same trick doesn't come twice in a row against the same answer.
