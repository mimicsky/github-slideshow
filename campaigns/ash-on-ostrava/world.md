## Ustrennos Station

- **Ustrennos Station (set session 9):** the remnant fleet's anchorage station, four stacked rings on a central spine, each ring a few kilometres around; ~40,000 people (fleet crews, Navy, Munitorum, dockers, families). Ring one: security, brig, chapel. Ring two: berths (2-9 is the Light's), galleys, fleet stores, medicae. Ring three: dockmaster's office, yards. Ring four: crew bunks, fuel office, cold stores, relay rooms. **Shops (restock every 2 days, set by the player; next fresh rolls warp day 18; Voss's day 16 stock holds through day 17):** Voss's fleet stores (M, huge), the ring-four dock chandlers (B, large), the fleet yards' Mechanicus forge-shrine on ring three (A, medium), the ring-one chapel's sellers (E, small; Selk's vestry is burned, a new priest runs it), and Free Trader stalls only when a trader is docked (d20 each day, 15+).
- **Master of Stores Hadra Voss** (Ustrennos fleet stores, ring two; a broad grey woman): restocks every 2 days (`wh40k_gear/roll.py M huge`); no credit; buys Guard/Navy issue in trade (hellguns 3 TV, a sound carapace set 5, Navy shotguns 2); told by the Commodore to 'accommodate' the Light. Bought from her in session 9: sheaths (Tamsin's Shameholder scabbard on his back, Kell's axe hip hanger, Corvane's power-sword scabbard), 3 belt pouches (Corvane, Sallow, Nessa), the revolver-drum grenade launcher (Hollis won it 5-2 over Tavi), 2 pistol holsters and 3 plasteel (day 16). **Corvane's wishlist:** a combat shotgun, a Navy cutlass, a quick-draw holster for the revolver.

## The Light: who they are (bios)

- **Sergeant Kell** (storm trooper sergeant, 41, ex-Tempestus Scion, nine years
  with the Ordos). Tamsin's "brother"; their fist-bump handshake. Hellgun
  **Officer** (named by Tamsin): bracket straightened, Ottrick's tube stock,
  Dace's fore-grip stamped K. Schola fighting knife. **Tessaly's plasma pistol** (given by Tamsin, warp day 1 to the Rest) with its 4-shot cell and a full spare cell. Ordered Tamsin: "Bring them
  back. All of you." Won the chain axe at rock-paper-scissors. **Named Most Valuable Frontline Warrior (MVFW) by Tamsin, day 34.**
  **Personality (set with the player, day 34):** a lifetime soldier (Schola-raised from boyhood, ex-Tempestus Scion, nine years with the Ordos). Calm, steady, professional: the Light's anchor. Brotherly with Tamsin: says so plainly when an order is bad, then respects his authority and carries out the call. **In battle he's the one people rally to:** he sets the standard and the Light follow and respect him (his +1 to the others). Modest about himself, protective of everyone else. Dry, deadpan humour; fights a grin rather than laugh.
  **How he talks:** calls Tamsin "boss". Short, clipped, often low or quiet. Facts, then the next step ("So that's the floor. Where do you want everyone?"). Disagrees once, then follows ("...Any worse than it is, boss?"). Backs orders with "You heard him." Dry one-liners ("Celebrated too early. Again." / "I just stood there and got shot."). Teaches with a nudge ("Do that again, boy. But start with the third one."). Flat and calm on the bead even when it's going wrong ("We're blown. Light, going in.").
- **Varga** (storm trooper). Left-handed, dry, protective. Hellgun **Mercy**
  (last bearer died dragging a man out of crossfire on Hesk; his red cloth is on
  the stock) with Tamsin's tap-button refit and Ferro's gun-link port (day 30). Carries Corvane's laspistol and a
  sharpened Krieg trench shovel. Teaching Dace to parry. **Named the Light's Most Valuable Shooter (MVSH) by Tamsin, day 34** (the walking bombs and the psyker at Crane Yard 6).

  rings "sound, unbroken, and afraid"; first blood from his own scarred palm.

  old collar. Riveted a shotgun-steel brace inside the shield (warp day 5 to Ostrava). **Practice swords:** three plain, blunt, straight duelling swords (no weighting to match any real weapon), grox-hide grips. Two done warp day 1 to the Rest, the third tomorrow. Plain, nothing special, as Tamsin asked. ## Season Two: how it plays (set by the player, end of session 7)

- **Premise:** Kesh sends Interrogator Rook and the Light to **eradicate heretic
  presence** from worlds in her sector. More fighting, conquest and loot; less
  intrigue. Missions in the spirit of Darktide's: not easy, mostly killing
  heretics and taking their stuff.
- **The ship:** *The Bright Light* (the old *Ember of Tyrok*), ~100 m courier. **Berths (set session 9):** the bunk room has **16 bunks** (13 in use: everyone but Tamsin, Ferro-Ashe who sleeps in the workshop, and Dace in medicae), Tamsin's cabin, **2 spare cabins** (now **Kell's** and **Varga's**, from day 14; bunk room 11 of 16 in use), 2 cells (empty).
  **Armament (rolled day 33, d20 6):** **one dorsal point-defence turret**, a twin autocannon on the spine, slaved to the bridge augur and fired from the bridge (good against small craft, fighters and torpedoes; useless against a real hull).
  **Grey hull with white stripes.** Refit (session 8): **a little faster**; a
  **side hangar for the Valkyrie** (replaced the planned drop-pod bay); **two containers** in the hold (the Light's
  container and a second); a **medium-sized shooting range**. **Upper deck** is
  the original layout (bridge, briefing room, cabins, bunk room, galley, armoury,
  medicae, shrine nook, cargo office, hold). **New small lower deck:** **Dace's
  forge**, **2 cells**, and the **new tech-priest's workshop** with a **plasma
  cell charging station**, plus **las charging stations**. Upper deck changes:
  the old forge spot is now the **sparring mat**, the old short range lane is the
  **stair down**, the hold holds **both containers** (port) and the **medium
  range, 3 lanes** (starboard, along the hold), and the aft hold plus the
  old brig is the **Valkyrie hangar** (big side door, port, aft; stores beside it). Pictures: `maps/bright-light.png`
  (both decks side by side) and `maps/bright-light-exterior.png`.
  **The Valkyrie** (session 8): taken from Drachen's *Gate Unbroken* with Kesh's
  leave, painted **mostly white with a grey underside and grey wings**. Kept in the
  **side hangar**, which replaced the drop-pod bay: **no drop pods aboard.** Flown by Mira Dhal and Teo Brack. White on a vehicle: easy to spot against dark ground or sky, good
  in snow (same idea as the white-armour ruling).
  Crew: the Light (see the companions and new crew tables). No Ottrick,
  Hespa-Vor or Haldis aboard.
- **The mission terminal** (codex Part 8): the player describes the mission as if
  briefing the Light; the DM answers with the difficulty and any tweaks, then the
  mission is played.
