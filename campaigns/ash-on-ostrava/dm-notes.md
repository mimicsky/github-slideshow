## How the campaign runs (always)

- **It runs until Tamsin dies** (the player's rule, session 8; never bring it up at the table). No planned ending. If he dies it's final: the book closes as his memorial and the player starts a new character.
- **The longer he lives, the harder it gets**, through more enemies sized to the job and to the Light (most fights 10-30; a kill team sent after the Light is about its size, 6-12; a ship or compound at the top end; small fights still happen), smarter tactics (flanking, ambushes, focusing the wounded, falling back to better ground) and nastier ground. **Never** moved DCs, inflated stats or per-book roll bonuses, and never a rigged death: the dice stand both ways. Each enemy rolls its own STR and DEX. The Light keeps pace through gear.
- **Enemies use their roles together, the way the player would:** a bomb zealot under smoke and covering fire, snipers on high ground, spotters marking for them. Type still shows: cultists are reckless and sometimes just charge; pirates and traitors are cold and careful.
- The player is a collector: put interesting things in the world; invented gear is fine if it fits. In the field, carry what's plausible.
- The player's d20s run hot (about 14.5 average in sessions 4-5). The dice stand; shot chains were the player's own answer.
