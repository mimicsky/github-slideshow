## Start checklist (every new chat, and after every context compaction)

Read these in order. Then open the first reply with one line ticking them off, e.g.
`Checklist: README ✓ sheet ✓ DM notes ✓ codex Always ✓ voices ✓ last scene ✓`.
Don't say what's in the DM notes.

1. **This README**, all of it.
2. **The campaign sheet** ([`ash-on-ostrava-campaign-sheet.md`](ash-on-ostrava-campaign-sheet.md)): "Where we stopped", "Current state", "What the Light knows about Thask", "Full kit", Tamsin ("The character"), the Light (roster, roles, specialties, stats). It's about 260 lines, all current state.
3. **The DM notes** ([`dm-notes.md`](dm-notes.md)), **down to the STOP line only**, read silently, never told to the player: how the campaign runs, the live mission's secrets, nemeses, pre-rolls. Below STOP, open an entry only when its trigger comes up.
4. **The codex** ([`../../DND_CODEX.md`](../../DND_CODEX.md)): **only the sections marked "Always" in the map at its top** (about 450 lines: the play preferences, house rules 1-6, cover, the roll checklist, shots and hits, breaking and Down, describing the world, armour and weapons). Open the rest when the map's trigger comes up.
5. **The Light's voices** ([`world.md`](world.md), "The Light: who they are"): every personality and voice entry, **before writing anyone's speech.** This is a story game as much as an action game: each person talks and acts like themselves (set by the player, day 34).
6. **The end of the last chapter** in [`book-season-2.md`](book-season-2.md) (search for the last `## Chapter`; read its final scene), plus the sheet's day log for anything played since.
7. **A fight on?** Do the **Fight checklist** (below) before the first die.
8. **Nothing else up front.** The gear catalogue's index, the bestiary's index and its roles file are opened when an item or a fight comes up ("Where things are" below).

Then pick up exactly where we stopped.

**A fight is on (or starting)?** Before the first die, do the **Fight checklist** below, all of it.

## Fight checklist: START HERE

**Just cleared context? Resuming mid-fight? Reading this from a new entry? A fight starting?**
Start at step 1 and go through every step, in order, before rolling a single die. Don't skip a
step because it "probably doesn't apply": open it and check. (Set by the player day 34, after
two fights ran with bonuses missed.) On the first beat after a resume, open the reply with
`Fight checklist ✓ 1-10`.

**1. Where are we?** Open the campaign sheet ([`ash-on-ostrava-campaign-sheet.md`](ash-on-ostrava-campaign-sheet.md)):
"Where we stopped" (line ~9) and the last lines of "Day log". Then [`dm-notes.md`](dm-notes.md), "Live now":
the live fight's entry, down to its last **"After beat N"** line. Write down: who is up, where, at what
health, the distances, who is surprised, what the player declared. **Positions or distances not set yet? Set them
and save them before rolling**, so they don't drift between beats.

**2. How the player likes to play.** Codex Part 6, **"How the player likes to play"**: all of it, every fight.
In fights the ones that bite: everyone acts every beat with their own roll; stop at the first decision point;
don't act, think or feel for Tamsin; **leftover shots go into another enemy automatically unless he says hold**
(the one already hurt that it can finish, else the biggest threat; swap -1); full tags; narrate each roll line;
the still-up board.

**3. The enemy.** In the fight's entry in `dm-notes.md`: each enemy's weapon, armour (after any step-up),
role, gun condition (Dirty or Neglected guns jam), **traits** (codex house rule 9; roll them with the group if they aren't set). **Anything unclear (an armour step-up, a weapon's reach): rule it
once and write the ruling into the roster** before rolling. Then:
- [`../../wh40k_gear/bestiary/README.md`](../../wh40k_gear/bestiary/README.md), top paragraph: a special team's bonuses
  (+5 STR/DEX levels, +1 other rolls, armour one step better). Then the team's own entry (look, tactics: play them that way).
- [`../../wh40k_gear/bestiary/roles.md`](../../wh40k_gear/bestiary/roles.md): every enemy role in the fight (leader, duelist, jumper, herder...).
- Codex house rule 4, "Some roles and gear add more": extra health stages, stimms, blessings.
- **Who never surrenders:** cultist duelists, the Moebian 6th, poxwalkers (mindless); enforcers stop breaks.

**4. The Light: each person in the fight.** In the campaign sheet:
- **"Full kit"** (line ~207): their row. **Every** weapon, **every** attachment, eye, field, armour piece,
  grenade and ammo count. Read the whole row, not just the gun's name.
- **The stats table** (under "How the Light rolls", line ~340): stat bonus = +1 per full 10. **Allies add no
  proficiency** (d20 + stat + specialty + role or gear). **Tamsin** adds proficiency +4 ("The character").
- **The Specialty table** (line ~291): +2, only on its kind of roll.
- **The Roles table** (line ~272), then each role in `roles.md` (Commander: Tamsin gives **two orders a beat to
  specialists**, +2 and act first; Jumper; Medic; Duelist; Bodyguard; Heavy gunner...).
- **"Tamsin's standing modifiers"** and **"The Light's standing modifiers"** (line ~308), and **each person's traits** (codex house rule 9).
- Who is **present**: only the people in **this** team or fight act or take orders (check the team list in the fight's
  entry; e.g. Kell and Hollis at the cannery aren't at Crane Yard 6). **An order to someone not there is void:** say so in
  one line. Kell's +1 to Tamsin only when Kell is there. Each person's health and conditions (Hurt, sickened, stimm turns).

**5. Each weapon's and attachment's own rules.** [The catalogue index](../../wh40k_gear/catalogue/README.md), then
the entry for each gun, melee weapon, attachment and eye in play (`las.md`, `heavy.md`, `stub.md`, `melee.md`;
`kit.md` for eyes and "Pistol and gun attachments"): string limits, recoil, what armour it gets through, quirks.
**A bonus not written anywhere** (a named gun's part with no catalogue entry): rule it once and **write the number into
that person's Full kit row**, so it's never worked out again.
Then codex Part 8, **Gear rules**: hot-shot, swapping, recoil, slots (**a gun's optic and an eye's aim bonus
stack**), airborne, gun condition and jams, pack sizes (hot-shot 30, las 45), refractor
field, doses, drop-prone guns, going Down drops weapons.

**6. Write each shooter's bonus line before rolling.** For everyone acting this beat:
`Varga (Light-bringer, rifle/jumper), Mercy: +4 DEX +2 aimed rifle +3 eye +2 reflex sight (inside ~30 m) +2 order +2 surprise -1 airborne = +14`.
Then roll.

**7. Resolve the beat in this order** (codex Part 6, A beat):
1. **Surprise:** a group caught unaware can't act or defend this beat; attacks on it get +2.
2. **Orders first:** the ordered actions (+2) resolve before anyone else's.
3. **Tamsin** (the player's dice) and **everyone else in the Light**: everyone acts every beat, each with an open roll.
4. **The enemies** (unless surprised), each with their own roll, played by their role and team tactics.
- **Every roll:** codex Part 6, **Roll checklist**, all 11 lines, each bonus written by name (stat, specialty, order,
  surprise, gear and eyes, chain, swap, recoil, airborne, health, cover, darkness).

**8. Every hit and every swing.**
- **Shots:** codex Part 6, **Shots and hits** (hidden number 10 / 15 / 20 + **Cover**), solid hit (beat it by 3+),
  crits (a natural 20; Tamsin 19-20; Vigil 17-20 with heat). **Write each hidden number in the beat's notes.**
- **Armour:** codex Part 8, **Armour** (shrugs or gets through; part-armour; crits always get through, but only one
  step against armour that would have shrugged it; wear-down: every 3 shrugged hits drop a step). Refractor field rolls.
- **Melee:** codex house rule 5, **the exchange** (opposed rolls, block 1-2 higher, parry 3+, a raw 17+ can't be stopped; **resolve a chain swing by swing: a parry ENDS the chain** (no more swings that beat), a block lets it carry on;
- **Knife bouts on the mat** (codex Part 6, Matches and contests): **d20 + DEX each, no specialties** (Tamsin adds his proficiency: +11); **vital (2 hits) only on a raw 17+ or a win by 5+, otherwise minor (1)**: no location rolls; first to 2 hits takes the round, first to 5 rounds the bout. (Added day 36 after Kell's bout was first run at +6 STR and specialty.)
  **chains**: light 3, medium and heavy 2, only a parry ends it; which stat; two weapons; shields).
- Health steps, extra stages, Down (enemies d6 after the fight; the Light don't die from Down).

**9. After the beat, then the reply.**
- **Count, for each person, in their Full kit row:** ammo (packs: hot-shot 30, las 45), Vigil's heat (vent at 3), every
  grenade, charge, stimm and antitoxin used (Sallow's Bag too), shots since cleaning (~30 = Dirty; Stinger's bolt jams past
  25 straight rounds), wear-down (shrugged hits, every 3 = a step), stimm turns and crashes.
- **Break check** (codex Part 6, Breaking and Down): when half are Down or the leader falls. **The player wants the Mart's
  factions to keep fighting:** no easy folding; when they don't surrender they fall back to better ground and counterattack.
- **Save:** `dm-notes.md` (the beat's line with every roll, then "After beat N"), the sheet ("Where we stopped",
  Full kit counts, the day log and reps), then commit and push.
- **Reply:** the math lines (full ally tags, enemy role tags, every bonus by name, each roll narrated in a few words),
  then "(saved)", then the scene (second person, detailed, no game terms, nothing done or felt for Tamsin), then the
  **still-up board**. Stop at the first decision point. **A new enemy role, rule or term the player hasn't met:** one line
  out of character saying what it is. Day log lines go in **time order**.

**10. When the fight ends.**
- **Down enemies:** d6 each (1-3 dead, 4-6 alive; +2 for limb-shot-only Downs). Codex Part 6, Breaking and Down.
- **Loot every body, the full count:** every gun, blade, armour piece, grenade and charge, coin, and anything odd (codex
  Part 8, Loot: the two rare rolls). The player wants the whole haul listed, not a short pick. Loot lines go on the sheet.
- **Reps** into the day log (kills, hits, orders, successful rolls). **Full kit and the crate** updated.
- **Witnesses** for nemeses (codex Part 8, Nemeses; `dm-notes.md`).
- **Prisoners taken:** for each one, at once, in `dm-notes.md` (hidden): **Will % and breaking point** (codex house rule 8) and their **traits** (house rule 9). Missed once (the 6th's three, day 36): don't skip it.
- **Who holds what changed** (a faction lost or took a place)? **Update the sector map** and say so.

## Every reply (the things that keep slipping)

- **Voices (set by the player, day 34): it's a story game as much as an action game.** **More detail is always better** in scenes and in these entries. Before writing anyone's lines, check their personality and voice entry in [`world.md`](world.md) ("The Light: who they are") and write them that way. **Keep those entries current in the same save:** a new character gets an entry; a new trait, want, fear, relationship, title, or a piece of kit that matters to them goes into their entry. "Newer, still developing" characters: try new things; keep what gets a reaction or sticks.
- **Never roll Tamsin's dice.** Ask "Roll me a d20" and wait. NPC dice and undecided facts are rolled openly with a real RNG. The DM rolls will checks for the player.
- **Show the math** on every roll: (die + stat + other bonuses = total). DCs stay hidden; contests show both totals.
- **Name + role on every mention**, dialogue lines included: `Kell (Light-bringer sergeant, assault): "line"` (**speech always inside quotation marks**, the player, day 36).
- **Enemies get their role tag** in fights, every mention: `Coat guy (gunslinger)`, `Flamer man (flamer)`, `Knife man (grunt)`, `Old Wick (Tallow Boys boss, leader)`.
- **Full tag on every ally mention**, math lines included: `Tavi (Light-bringer, demolitions)`, never bare `Tavi`. Enemies by bestiary class (`assassin`, `grunt`, `psyker`, `leader`, `beast handler`). **Detail the scene** (sights, sounds, smells, what each person does).
- **In a fight: the Fight checklist (above) before the first die, and the codex Roll checklist on every roll,** with every bonus shown by name (stat, specialty, order, surprise, gear and eyes, chain, swap, recoil, airborne, health, cover).
- **'Still up' board at the end of every fight beat:** every enemy standing (tag, health, where, weapon), the Down count, the Light's hurt and key states.
- **Narrate each roll line:** a few words of action with the math (`Kell puts one round into Knife crank man: 16+7 = 23, it rips through him, badly hurt`).
- **Second person** for Tamsin. **Don't act, think or feel for him.** Stop at the first new decision point.
- **No game terms in the fiction.** NPCs never mention rolls, dice or numbers.
- **Save before the scene**: the note is just "(saved)", and the scene is the last thing in the reply. Commit and push after anything important.
- **Full kit:** when gear is used, swapped, found or handed out, update the sheet's Full kit table and the crate count in the same save.
- **Don't read pools, hidden notes or nemeses out to the player.**
- **Questioning a captive or trying to win someone over = the Will system (codex house rule 8), never a plain check against a hidden number.** Before the first question, look up (or set) their Will and breaking point in `dm-notes.md`. **Each push is one session (~an hour) = one will check**, rolled openly **by the DM for the player** (Intimidation, Persuasion or a pairing, by the approach), Will lost by the table; hard methods +5% and their risk roll. They answer truthfully only once broken, and only what they know. Say "(will check)" on the roll line, and save the new Will (hidden) to `dm-notes.md`. **Tamsin adds +1 on every will check (the provost's manual).**

## End of every in-game day

1. Show the **tally sheet** (Tamsin's stats before and after, then the Light's).
2. **Write that day as book chapters** in `book-season-2.md` (codex Part 7, The book).
3. Delete that day's scene notes from the day log, and update "Where we stopped".
4. Commit and push.

On a long day, add a one-line note to the day log after each big scene, so nothing is lost if the context is cut.

## End of a session

Award XP (the sheet's XP table), check the book is up to date with every day played, update "Where we stopped", commit and push.

## Start of a mission

- Roll the faction's wealth: `python3 wh40k_gear/roll_enemy.py wealth TYPE` (once per mission or world).
- Check the DM notes' witnesses and nemeses: bring one back if the story fits (codex Part 8, Nemeses; the DM's call, no automatic rolls).
- Enemies: `python3 wh40k_gear/roll_enemy.py TYPE COUNT WEALTH [GOD]`. The types are cultist, raider, pirate, traitor and traitor_elite.
- Any other roll (saves, break checks, loot d20s, NPCs): `python3 wh40k_gear/dice.py d20` (or `3d20`, `d6`...). A rare item: `python3 wh40k_gear/dice.py rare weapons` (rare roll 1, gear in use) or `rare any` (rare roll 2, stashed).
- Shops: `python3 wh40k_gear/roll.py SELLER SIZE` (restock every 2 days). Sellers: **M** Munitorum or fleet stores, **B** black market or chandlers, **A** Mechanicus shrine, **F** Free Trader or Void Exchange, **E** Ecclesiarchy. Sizes: small (5-7), medium (9-12), large (14-18), huge (20-25).
