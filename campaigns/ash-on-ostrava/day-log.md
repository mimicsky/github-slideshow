# Day log: Ash on Ostrava

Day log for Ash on Ostrava. One bullet per timestamp. At the day's end the day goes into the book and this file is cleared to the safety-net line; see README.

Safety-net notes, one line per big scene, deleted once the day's chapter is written (README, End of every in-game day).

- **Days 38-44 are all in the book** (Chapter 120: day 38; Chapters 121-123: days 39-41; Chapters 124-126: day 42; Chapters 127-128: day 43; Chapter 129: day 44, to ~0600 day 45). Their tallies are done.

## Day 45

- ~0600: Dawn on the bridge after a quiet night (warp day 4, the last full day). Tamsin: 'It's too quiet... it's plotting.' Mira: a day and a half left, then real space.
- ~0605: Tamsin: 'Come in, Ferro... what's going on?' Ferro: tonight the same watch; but tomorrow, translation: the field must cycle down for a few seconds as the ship drops out, the door's weakest moment; if it means to try, it will try then. He wants Tamsin at the node at translation, and the Light kitted.
- ~0607: Tamsin: 'I'll be there... with the knife and my chainsword... it won't get anything but death.' **Translation plan: Tamsin and Ferro at the node at ~1200 day 46; the Light kitted and ready.** Ferro to tell Kell.
- ~0610: Tamsin to Ferro: 'I will' (sleep). Slept ~0612-1200 (d20 13: quiet).
- 1200: Tamsin woke (slept ~0612-1200). Lunch on; the Light on their plan; Ferro asleep after his watch; Varga waiting in the galley with a slate: her off-weapon drawing for Dace and Ferro.
- ~1205: Varga's off-weapon sketch: a chest drop rig of six frags on ripcords; a drum autopistol on a lanyard to the jump harness. Tamsin: 'Not much of an idea person, are we? Pull frags and have shards all round your feet coming down?' Liked the lanyard autopistol (drop it, it hangs, easy pick-up). Varga: she pulls at height, so a fused frag bursts before she lands; or impact frags (Ottrick's conversion), which burst on the ground before she gets there.
- ~1207: Tamsin: 'Want to clear the ground, those do the trick. Just make sure you get above the targets and drop them.' **Approved: schematic 19, Varga's drop rig (six impact frags on ripcords) and lanyard autopistol; 19th in the build order.**
- ~1210: Lunch (d4 3; d13 11, 12, 9): Nessa, Sorel and Sallow. Sallow: rot country; rebreathers and gas masks for everyone going down (Dannel, Joren and Tamsin's crew short of them). Nessa: who's on the small team that goes in first as assessors?
- ~1212: Tamsin: Joren and Dannel stay on the ship, they aren't fighters. To Nessa: 'Do I need to tell you? Same team that went and got the watchers on the Rest.' **The assessor team: Tamsin, Nessa, Sorel, Tey** (the hostel-roof team). Sallow: then the masks go to whoever goes down, and the ship keeps the rest.
- ~1214: Tamsin: he has his own rebreather, as most of the team do; Nessa, Sorel and Tey the ones without. Sallow, from her list: Nessa has a rebreather; Tey and Sorel carry gas masks (the same job). (Fixed to the record.) The four going down are covered; the spares go to whoever follows in the assault. Tamsin to Nessa: she and Sorel blended in well on the Rest.
- ~1216: Tamsin: Tey's still young, an aspiring ego, still scared inside. Sallow: he jumps forward, so nobody sees it. Nessa: brave because he's scared. **Sorel takes Tey this afternoon to teach him to be nobody in a room (a clerk's stillness); Nessa teaches him to look bored.** Tamsin: 'Fine by me.'
- ~1225-1800: Tamsin on the range (~5.5 h, DEX), every gun in turn. Afternoon d20 7: quiet. Through the hold: Sorel drilling Tey in stillness by the containers; Nessa's 'look bored' lessons; Ferro up at ~1400 and back on his lasgun drills two lanes down. The trace flat all afternoon.
- ~1810: Supper. Kell: anything to tell them before they drop out? Tamsin: only that his Light-bringers are on point and ready; to Hollis, sarcastic: 'and learn some patience.' Laughter; Hollis held up his bandaged hand in surrender. Kell passed the word: on point at noon.
- ~1815: Tamsin: he's almost finished the wishlist, everything but their schematics; Dace, where are we? Dace: medal done; Valk panel not (needs the returned hull-plate manual); Ferro's plasma pistol a third (bench lost to the nose gun and the node); Sallow's carbine just started; most of the list after Gallowmere. Morcant: his drums and compensator are still on the wishlist.
- ~1817: Tamsin: shift priorities to the schematics and the hull and so on; he'll have Kell change Dace's schedule (**Dace: forge and bench all 6 h from day 46**). 'I said almost, didn't I?' Dace: he'll try the panel slow and cold, an inch at a time.
- ~1822: Tamsin: 'Corv. You haven't mentioned your cutlass. Still want it, or is the power sword better?' Corvane: the power sword is the better blade and he'll fight with it; but the cutlass was his on the Steadfast Ninth, and it hangs on Ottrick's wall on the Lantern; he still means to earn it back, to carry beside the sword, not instead of it.
- ~1824: Tamsin: it's on Ottrick's wall; Ottrick's a deal-maker; but Tamsin sold it to him (it paid a debt, season one), so he'll buy it back. **Owed: buy Corvane's cutlass back from Ottrick when they next meet the Lantern.** Corvane stood and bowed from the bench, the old officer's way.
- ~1826: Tamsin to Tey, Corvane and Sorel: they don't have to keep calling him Interrogator. Tey: he already says boss. Corvane: habit of the Ninth; he'll try 'boss' (it came out stiff). Sorel: forty-seven years of the Guard; he'll keep 'Interrogator' on duty, 'boss' at table, if that suits.
- ~1832: Tamsin to Varga: anything to say about her eye? Does she miss her old one? Varga (the left eye lost to shrapnel at the Orne fight; the Mechanicus shooting eye Tamsin bought her, day 29): it's better, which is the annoying part; it doesn't blink, doesn't water, sees the wind; she misses crying out of both sides, she said flatly, and colours being warm. Then: 'Thank you for it. I never said.'
- ~1845: Tamsin to his cabin; full kit (flak, carapace, refractor, Last Word, Stinger, Vigil unsealed, the knife, Shameholder; his plague rebreather on the belt).
- ~1900: Tamsin went to find the Light: most in the hold, kit laid out on tarps by the containers for Kell's check (masks, packs, blades); Sallow handing out the spare masks; Dace at the forge corner with Varga's slate; Ferro fitting the impact-frag conversion.
- ~1905-0600: Tamsin on the bridge all night with Mira, in full kit. Watches changed every two hours, all quiet; the trace flat. ~0600 day 46 the day lamps.
