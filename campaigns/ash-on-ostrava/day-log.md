## Day 43

- ~0110 (warp day 2 to Gallowmere): **the night d20 4: a Geller flicker; something brushed through.** Lights to ember-red, the hum gone, frost on the bulkheads; a whisper in a loved one's voice for each sleeper. Frost over Maren's page of stars; a child's voice at Tamsin's ear: 'Are you coming back?' **Tamsin's save: natural 20 (adv 8/20) + 4 + 1 = 25**: steady; the voice is a mask, not Maren's. Morcant shouting aft; Ferro chanting at the Geller node.
- ~0111: Tamsin grabbed Shameholder and Vigil, roared the chainsword to life, ran to the bunk room (Kell and Varga out of their cabins). The bunk room red, frost on the racks: Morcant on his bunk shouting at a corner, Tavi curled up, Dannel on his knees ('Mam?'), Brenner rigid, Sorel praying; Hollis, Isk, Corvane, Nessa, Tey, Dace trying to calm them.
- ~0111: **'BIG GUY, SNAP OUT OF IT! I'M HERE!' Intimidation 16 + 6 = 22: Morcant back** (his old sergeant in the corner; 'it's not him').
- ~0112: **'DANNEL, IT'S NOT REAL! STAY HERE WITH ME!' Intimidation 14 + 6 = 20: Dannel back** (his mam calling from the garden; 'she's dead, I know she's dead').
- ~0113: **The field back** (Ferro: 'Field restored... the node is blessed and holding. Apologies, Interrogator.'). Frost to water; the whispers gone. Still shaken till they've slept (-1 on all rolls): Tavi, Brenner, Sorel, Sallow, Mira, Teo, Ferro, Ollin.
- ~0114: Tamsin, Shameholder still running, shouted at the thing beyond the hull: 'DEAD! ... they're DEAD!' Nothing answered. Morcant joined in. Isk, low: it's gone, don't give it your voice; they feed on being spoken to. Sorel: in the Guard they never answered them. Kell, in the hatch: 'Boss. The sword.'
- ~0115: Blood running from Tamsin's nose (the player's: the fight inside with it). 'He knows... he wants to stop us getting to them... this really is it. But they can't stop us.' Kell saw the blood: 'Sallow!' Sallow in from the corridor with her bag (shaken herself). Sorel: then we're on the right road. Shameholder still running.
- ~0116: Tamsin shut Shameholder off and hung it at his belt: 'I dare it to touch my team again...' Sallow packed his nose (stopped in a minute). Kell: he'll sit up with the lights on in the corridor till morning; anyone who can't sleep sits with him.
- ~0117: Tamsin: 'No, all of you sleep... I'll handle it myself.' Kell held his eyes a moment, then: 'Yes, boss.' Lights down in the bunk room; the Light back into their bunks. Tamsin takes the night watch himself (Night owl).
- ~0118-0340: Tamsin on watch on the deck outside the bunk room (Night owl). Quiet. ~0340 (d20 1; d6 4): Ferro came forward from the Geller node with oil to the elbows, asking to speak low.
- ~0342: Tamsin: 'Something wants to stop us... what?' Ferro: he can't say what; the sensors show pressure, not shape; a Navigator or a psyker could tell, and the ship has neither. The trace grew stronger for a moment at ~0114 (when Tamsin shouted at it), then faded back. Ferro can harden the node with a rite (a few hours), and a purity seal on the node would help the crew; he asks leave.
- ~0344: Tamsin handed Ferro Vigil: 'I dare them come and face us... I'll rip them piece to piece... I can feel Shame in my hands right now... it's not happy either.' Ferro took Vigil carefully; asked whether he meant Vigil's purity seal for the node (Vigil would lose it), or Vigil itself in the rite.
- ~0346: 'Take the seal. I need Vigil in case we get boarded by daemons; it happened once on my old ship: daemons came in from the warp and attacked the ship.' Showed Ferro his knife: it stopped that breach, and the scorch is there to remind him. **Vigil's purity seal to Ferro for the node (Vigil unsealed); Vigil back to Tamsin.** Ferro to start the warding rite now (several hours).
- ~0350: Communing with Shameholder: WIS 11 + 4 = 15: **he feels it**: hunger, and the old revulsion it showed at the plague things, and a pull aft, toward the quarter where Ferro said the field was pressed.
- ~0352: Tamsin to Shameholder: 'I hear you... don't worry... you'll cut through the first one. That's a promise to you: I'll sate both our rages at the end of this fight.' The grip warmed once more under his hand, then went still. **(Owed: Shameholder the first cut on Gallowmere.)**
- ~0353-0600: Tamsin kept watch to morning, wide awake (Night owl: no penalty). ~0610 Ferro came forward grey with exhaustion: the rite failed at the last verse (natural 1): the trace spiked, a warding coupling cracked; patched. The seal is on the node. He can try again once he's slept.
- ~0612: Tamsin to Ferro: 'Go get some sleep. I'll hold here... nothing gets in this ship without meeting me.' Ferro to his bunk (sleeps till ~1300; then the rite again). The shaken all slept: cleared. ~0615 the Light out of the bunk room, finding Tamsin still on the deck with Vigil across his knees.
- ~0617: Tamsin: 'No, it's scared of me.' Got up: 'And I'm still pissed.' Kell: 'Then eat. Pissed on an empty stomach's no use to anyone.' Ollin's recaf on in the galley. Kell asked, low, what Ferro was chanting about all night.
- ~0618: Tamsin to Kell: 'They are following us... he is. They want to stop us... I dare them.' Kell took it level: then nobody sleeps soft till they drop out; he'd set a watch tonight, two and two, at the node and outside the bunk room, armed; asked whether the Light hear it.
