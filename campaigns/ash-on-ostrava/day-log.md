## Day 49

- ~0030: Wheels up from Fourteen; the ship held high and dark over the Varn. ~0130 Mira dropped Isk on the west bank; he was in the old chapel tower by ~0145: the landing in shadow, two guards with a lamp at the cart doors.
- ~0150: Tey and Sorel reached the barge landing; a stone went off the quay wall under Sorel's boot. The garden man on the landing challenged, 'Thorn.' Tey answered, 'Bloom.' Half-believed: 'Who sent you? Halloran said nobody comes by water tonight.' He came down the steps with a lamp, a weigh-guard behind him.
- ~0203: Brenner and Hollis were stopped at the cart doors; Brenner's 'tithe count, for Reeve Varrow's office' didn't wash ('Varrow's men don't walk the Row at two in the morning... Clear off before I fetch the Row's boys'). They walked on toward the yard end; one guard went inside the office door.
- ~0206: Brenner's auspex count: thirty-three to thirty-five warm bodies on the Row, about nineteen in the weigh-house (ten on the ground floor, one in the office, four upstairs, two under the floor, two at the doors), four in the yard, about twelve asleep in the cart sheds, two at the landing. Kell asked: go early, or hold for 0230?
- ~0207: Tamsin: "Go go go, move in take them out, it's time to go loud." BEAT 1: Tey put the lamp man on the landing down with five shots from both hellpistols and Hurt the weigh-guard behind him; Sorel dropped the weigh-guard. Isk, from the tower, dropped the mortar gunner on the water-gate winch-house roof (a crit). Brenner jammed the Row's vox. Hollis put a Six Bells frag on the cart doors and Grazed the door guard, who sprayed back; one stub round off Hollis's plate. The office guard came out behind a riot shield; the other ran inside to wake the floor. The Valk two minutes out.
- ~0208: Tamsin: "Isk take out that mortar before it causes trouble." "Hollis bells on guard." "Mira push it will you." BEAT 2: Isk hit the loader twice (Hurt). Hollis's last drum round went wide; Six Bells empty. Brenner's shots missed the shield. The door guard and the shield man missed; a round from the cart doors' gap came off Brenner's plate, and the second gunner there jammed. A veteran upstairs fired down from a river window; both rounds came off Tey's plate, and Tey hit him twice as he ducked (Hurt). The vault barred the iron door. Someone in the yard loosed hounds up the lane. Upstairs a voice roared for Halloran to get to the jars. The ship came down over the river; the Valk a beat from the roof.
- ~0209: Tamsin: "Tavi blow it fast." "Isk take out the mortar before it gets bad then, help the others." BEAT 3: the roof team dropped onto the weigh-house roof; Tavi's charge blew the hatch in clean. Isk dropped the mortar loader. Hollis's frag caught the three hounds and Varga finished all three from the roof edge. Kell, Mira's nose gun and Teo's turret hit four men coming out of the cart sheds. Brenner put the door guard to Badly hurt and took a crit from his SMG (Grazed). Rounds came off Kell's and Varga's plates. A jumper lit a scrap pack and went up Silo 3. Inside, men came up the stair toward the hatch; a voice roared 'HOLD THE STAIR'; someone dropped into the vault.
- ~0209: Tamsin fired four down through the blown hatch: the chainsword man under it (the Row's commander) went down in three, a crit among them; the fourth hit the pack man on Silo 3's gantry. (Fixed: Tamsin gives two orders a beat to specialists, so Isk and Hollis in beat 2 and Tavi and Isk in beat 3 all had +2; the loader was down a shot sooner, and the far sheds man is Hurt.)
- ~0210: Tamsin: "EVERYONE SPREAD OUT, GET THEM." "OUTSIDE TEAM SECURE THE PERIMETER." "KELL ON THE SHIELD." "MORCANT LET IT FLY." BEAT 4: Kell shot down past the shield; Morcant's Doorknocker wrecked the burner and dropped him and Hurt the next man. Tamsin jumped down the hatch and dropped the shield man and the axe man and hit the hammer man. Varga dropped the pack man off the gantry (a crit). Tavi dropped in and put a frag down the stairwell. Isk, Mira and Teo dropped two in the yard. Hollis and Brenner hit the two mutants charging up the lane. Tamsin's refractor stopped three stub rounds and his plate took three more; the door guard rolled two crits through Brenner's plate: Brenner Badly hurt and bleeding.
- ~0211: Tamsin: "BRENNER GET IN FULL COVER AND STOP FIGHTING YOU'RE DONE." "ISK COVER BRENNER." "VARGA ROTATE TO BRENNER." "ANYONE CLOSE TO BRENNER AND HOLLIS ROTATE OVER." Then: "VARGA AFTER YOU GET DOWN TEND TO HIM." "SALLOW WHERE ARE YOU?" BEAT 5: Brenner crawled into a loading-door recess; Isk couldn't cross the river (a jump-pack burst is only 10-15 m) and dropped a yard man from the tower instead. Tamsin, from a doorway, dropped the hammer man and the spike man (a crit). Kell dropped the window veteran. Morcant hauled Sallow up to the roof. Varga dropped off the roof on her pack and stopped Brenner's bleeding. Hollis, Mira and Teo dropped one mutant and wounded the other and the demo man; Hollis parried the second mutant. The demo man's mining charge went wide into a warehouse wall. A crit got through Hollis's plate (Grazed). Half the garden down: they threw down their guns, and Halloran's hired guards with them. Not the demo man; not the vault, barred and blind; not Corlis.
- ~0211 (ruling): standard stub can't touch carapace now, crits included. Brenner was never hit through: the SMG rounds flattened on his plate and knocked him flat. He's Fine, just winded, and waiting in the recess.
- (Correction: the stub line stops at heavy carapace, not carapace. Brenner's wounds stand: 2.0, bleeding stopped by Varga.)
- ~0212: Tamsin: "Head tap the ones who won't surrender, that will show the others we mean business." "GET THE SURRENDERED ONES ON THE GROUND AND IN CUFFS, THE OTHERS DIE." Varga dropped the demo man and put the second round through his head; his charge fell inert. The surrendered went on their faces; Hollis cuffed the mutant, Tey the yard. Tamsin went down through the weighing floor to the office, and the trapdoor behind the big scales started to lift: the vault crew coming up, with the crate.
- ~0212 (ruling): mutants never surrender. The second mutant hadn't gone down after all (they're tougher than I'd counted); Teo's turret finished it in the lane. The other came at Hollis: Varga's shot and Hollis's hand flamer left it burning, and Hollis slipped its grab.
- ~0213: Tamsin: "TAVI ON ME SHOOT WITH ME." "SALLOW PATCH UP BRENNER DON'T LET HIM DOWN." "Kell status report? How is the situation over there." The vault crew never saw them: Tavi and Tamsin killed the brute half out of the trapdoor (Vigil crit; it has to vent now). The burning mutant fell and Hollis finished it. Sallow ran the lane for Brenner. Kell: the yard locked down, both mutants dead. Two left in the vault: a man on the ladder under the brute's body, and one at the bottom with the lamp.
- ~0214: Tamsin: "Clear and search, we need these bakers now." "Kell search around." "Morcant can you make space up there? If not hold position." Morcant kicked in the end room upstairs; Kell found CORLIS in the wardrobe, unarmed, and bound him. Sallow patched Brenner to Hurt in the lane. The cleaver man on the ladder gave up. The last one in the vault went for the iron door and couldn't shift the bar; Sorel heard it.
- ~0215: Tamsin: "Someone rotate to Sorel." "DOWN ON THE GROUND." "Tavi cuff em." Tey ran for the grain stairs; Tavi cuffed the cleaver man. Tamsin went down into the vault: the cooler humming, empty slots in the jar racks. At the far end the last man got the bar up and opened the iron door, straight into Sorel's hellgun.
- ~0215: Tamsin: "ON THE GROUND NOW." The man in the fur collar set his lamp down and went flat on the stone: "I weigh grain." Sorel cuffed him. THE FIGHT WAS OVER: 22 down, 19 prisoners, both bakers taken alive (Corlis upstairs, Halloran at the iron door). Brenner Hurt, Hollis Grazed, the rest whole.
- ~0216: Tamsin, standing over the man in the fur collar: "Where are the jars, where did they go!!?" "Here! They're all here! The crate, your man's got it, in the office. The rest are on the racks. Forty. Count them, forty. I count everything." Sixteen frosted jars still stood in the cold room's racks; Tavi counted twenty-four in the crate, all sealed.
- ~0217: "We are Inquisition. I will rip it out of you. Who and where were you taking them?" The word Inquisition broke him. Halloran: the skiff under the landing, along the quay to the water-gate, in by the flour chute to the cold-box in the cellar under the third oven hall, to Master Baker Hemmet, who keeps it for the eve and takes whatever the Silo sends.
- ~0218: Tamsin: "Grab the other one." Then to Halloran: "The other bakers' names, and anyone else important. Names." Halloran gave them all: Hemmet (the only baker in it); Corlis (the Spire man, the word off the *Hesk Tender*); Under-Secretary Quint (the passes, the words in the address); the widow Hanne Sallis of Sallis & Daughters (the purse); Dray the lighterman (brings the jars down from the Tender; it comes again at the Lamps for two crates and a passenger); the Brother at Kettle Ford; Fenn at Varn Quay. Kell brought Corlis down.
- ~0220: The downed were checked. Fifteen of Halloran's and the garden's men were dead, the commander and the window veteran among them. Four lived, Down: the sniper from the landing, the mortar gunner, the jump-pack man on the gantry and a yard man. All three farm hounds lived. Twenty-three prisoners.
- ~0220-0320: Tamsin on the vox: "Everyone search the area, find everything, no matter the time." The team took the weigh-house apart in an hour and found it all: the forty jars and the cooler, Halloran's ledger, Corlis's satchel of sealed letters, the Silo's pay chest (3,000 Thrones), a phosphex canister, a crate of AP rounds, the garden's guns and blades, an auspex that sees through walls, a command vox, a scrap jump pack, two mining charges. Tamsin went to Corlis.
- ~0222: Tamsin to Corlis: "Who hired the militia?" "Halloran. Silo livery, the widow's coin. Nobody pays the garden. It grows." Then: "I know who you are and what you've done... the Inquisition tried to get you at the Mart and you escaped... to do what? Come here and flee and cower?" Corlis: he'd hidden in a wardrobe and never claimed to be brave; he came to carry the word, and it's delivered.
- ~0223: Tamsin: "And your satchel. What will I find in that?" Corlis, his eyes going to it for the first time: "Letters you can't read. A speech you've already heard. And a token for a boat you'll never catch."
- ~0224: Tamsin: "A speech that won't get its word out... The Inquisition is here to stop that. We've already halted your plan. Now how did you know to reinforce your position?" Corlis: Quint missed his hour on day 47, a man who never missed one, and the port cells burned at seven bells; so he had Halloran bring the garden in off the Row.
- ~0225: Tamsin: "Never enough. You can't win this war. Your governor will never get that speech out, those vox masts will not speak throughout Gallow. And my team already has an antidote; having jars on jars confiscated makes it easy." Corlis: "If you had a cure, you wouldn't be asking me questions." Then, softly: "Forty jars is one city's bread, Interrogator. Gallow has more than one city."
