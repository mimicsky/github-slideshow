Four hot-shot bolts at six metres: the Major jerked, staggered, and went down on his back in the dust of his own yard, his helmet lenses cracked; the fourth swung onto the plasma-gun guard and burned him half off his feet (a crit). In the same heartbeat the whole Light came up out of the hauler: Kell's Officer through the cab window put the priest down in three shots (a natural 20); Nessa came over the tailgate with her power knife and Kreel went down under it before his chainsword cleared its hook; Sorel's hellgun dropped the second guard; Morcant's Doorknocker emptied the man with the shovel off his feet; Corvane shot one and cut another; and Isk, from the bed, put two bolts into the stubber crew on the hall roof and one of them went over the parapet. Six of the Sixth down in the first breath of the fight, the Major among them. Then the alarms started, and the dormitory shutters began to bang open.
- ~1111 FOURTEEN, BEAT 2: 'WE ARE THE LIGHT!!!' 'THE LIGHT!' roared back through the stolen masks. 'CORV, TAKE OUT THE MACHETE!' Corvane: 'The machete's mine!' His revolver found a gap in the Machete man's plate and the power sword finished him. 'ISK, TAKE OUT THAT GUNNER!' Isk: 'Gunner. On it.' Patience hammered the hall roof and the last stubber gunner slumped badly hurt behind his gun. Tamsin slung Vigil, ripped Shameholder free and drew Stinger, and went for the plasma-gun guard: one swing tore through the gun's housing and into him and he went down; Tamsin dashed in on the next man and Shameholder took him wide across the ribs through his plate, then came back round over the shoulder and down through the collar, ripping in. Kell's Officer and Sorel's hellgun cut down two more round the hauler; Nessa and the last man of the yard party traded blows, knife against chain knife, and neither landed. The Bright Light came over the edge of the plateau with her engines roaring, and Teo's turret chewed the doorway of the dormitories as the first men stumbled out of them. The wall pairs and the gate fired at the yard and hit nothing.
