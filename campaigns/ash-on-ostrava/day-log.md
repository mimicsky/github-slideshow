# Day log: Ash on Ostrava

Day log for Ash on Ostrava. One bullet per timestamp. At the day's end the day goes into the book and this file is cleared to the safety-net line; see README.

Safety-net notes, one line per big scene, deleted once the day's chapter is written (README, End of every in-game day).

## Day 42

- ~0900: Tamsin woke (slept ~2040-0900: full rest; aboard at berth 38, Saint Ivaine's Rest). **The night quiet (d20 11).** Everyone Fine. A note pushed under his door in Aldwyn's square hand: the Lady will brief him at 1100; Oculine will see his two converts at 1000 in the residence; the girl slept through. Galley: Ollin's recaf on.
- ~0905: Tamsin pocketed Aldwyn's note and went to the galley. Ollin at the stove (bricks, recaf). **At the table (2d8 4, 5): Isk and Morcant**, the same pair as day 38's breakfast; plus Dannel and Joren in clean coveralls (scrubbed for the seer, both too nervous to eat much) and Pell between them with a brick. Morcant: 'Boss! Heard you blew up a whole ship!' Isk, quietly over his recaf: 'Heard you shot a prophet in the leg.' (Word went round the ship last night.)
- ~0907: Tamsin to Dannel and Joren: 'You both ready?' Dannel, swallowing: '...Yes, sir.' Joren: ready as he'll ever be, Interrogator; he's never been looked at by a seer before; what does she do? Morcant, cheerfully, mouth full: she looks right through you and tells you what you had for breakfast. Isk: 'She doesn't.' Pell, worried, between them. ~50 minutes to the seeing (1000, the residence; ~20 minutes' walk).
- ~0908-0935: 'Alright, eat up, then we go.' Dannel and Joren forced down a brick each; Ollin slipped them each a second mug of recaf. ~0935: ready at the ramp. Pell at Dannel's elbow with her slate under her arm: can she come too, to see Maren?
- ~0936: Tamsin to Pell: not with them, but she can get one of the guys to take her to Maren. **Hollis** (on the ramp): 'Here, lass. I'll walk you over.' Tamsin, Dannel and Joren through the station to the residence (~0955). Aldwyn took them up to a small room with one lamp: Oculine on her stool. Kesh standing in the doorway behind them, watching.
- ~1000: **Oculine's seeing (rolled openly, d20 each; 1-3 = something of the garden still in them; Dannel -2, raised in the garden): Dannel 13-2 = 11: clean. Joren 13: clean.** Oculine held each one's hands a long minute, blindfolded face close. Dannel: 'There was a garden in this one once. It's ash now. He grieves, that's all; let him.' Joren: 'A docker who loves his mother and owes nobody anything any more. Nothing else in him.' Kesh, from the door: she'll sign their names. Dannel shaking with relief; Joren let out a breath he'd held since the galley.
- ~1005: Kesh: 'Eleven o'clock, Interrogator. My room. You've the better part of an hour.' Tamsin: 'Dannel, Joren, come here.' They came (Dannel wiping his face; Joren's hand on his shoulder).
- ~1006: Tamsin between them, quietly: 'Head high. Stand at attention.' Stepped back: they've proven they're brave, adjusted to their lives and atoned for their past; he made them both members of the Light: **Light Hands** (a new rank: the Light's ship crew, not frontline; as offered day 37).
- ~1007-1100: the hour waited out at the residence. Dannel and Joren went to tell Pell (with Maren, in Kesh's house). **~1040 (d20 3, d6 5): Aldwyn hurried upstairs with a sealed slate from the Rest's choir; Kesh's door shut.**
- ~1100: Kesh's briefing (her mood d20 2: foul).
- ~1102: Tamsin's read: the same as the Mart, a last stand: all they have left is the silos and the Lamps, all their people in one place; move fast; a full assault. Kesh: fast, yes (leave today); a last stand here maybe, not for the Sixth (their strength is on Atoma Prime); but you can't assault what you can't find: a whole world of silos and she doesn't know which. Find the garden first, then hit it with everything. She gave him the papers (an Administratum tithe-assessors' warrant) and her one name on Gallowmere: **Tithe-Reeve Osk Varrow at the main grain port**, last report ~20 days ago.
- ~1104: Tamsin: 'Then an infiltration first.' Kesh: agreed: in as assessors, find Varrow, find the garden; the assault after. She waits for what else he needs.
- ~1106: Tamsin's request: Pell stays with Maren till the Light come back, and Joren's mother can watch over them too. **Kesh: yes (no roll: she'd already agreed day 41 that Pell and Joren's mother may live on the Rest).** Pell lives in Kesh's house with Maren; Aldwyn has the keeping of both; Oculine sits with Pell once today first (anyone that close to Maren gets seen). Joren's mother: when she's here; Joren's letter goes out in Kesh's packet today.
- ~1108: Tamsin: 'And about Maren and putting her in?' Kesh: still after the Lamps. Oculine has seen her; her chirurgeon drew Maren's blood last night; not tainted, not a witch, but there's something in the blood Kesh wants understood before she sends her to a house full of children. Until then: Tamsin's ward, sleeping in Kesh's house.
- ~1110: Tamsin: 'Consider it done already.' Out of Kesh's room; on the bead: Light ready for takeoff, leaving now, brief aboard soon, say goodbyes; Sallow, if with Maren, tell her she'll be safe soon. Kell: heard. **Sallow (d6 3): aboard, not with her.** **Hollis (d6 3): still at the residence with the girls**; told Maren. Maren asked whether Tamsin would come and say goodbye himself (Dannel and Joren with them; no beads, they didn't hear the call).
- ~1111: Tamsin went to the little room off the kitchen: Maren, Pell, Hollis, Dannel, Joren. Maren came to him; asked if he was coming back, and held up the corner of a page she'd drawn stars on.
- ~1112: Tamsin took Maren's star drawing, folded it into his pocket: 'This fight is for you to see the stars, Maren. Remember that.' She closed the last step and hugged him round the middle, quick and hard, then let go. 'I'll remember.' Dannel said goodbye to Pell (she cried; he promised to bring her something from the next world). Hollis: 'Ship, boss.'
- ~1112-1135: the walk back to berth 38 with Hollis, Dannel and Joren. Joren wrote his letter to his mam (Annet, block 4-E, ring four, Ustrennos) on the walk; at the ramp he paid a berth runner a coin of his own to carry it to the Lady's house for her packet. ~1135 aboard: the Light gathered in the hold, kitted. **Mira: the Valk's bolt-rocket pods (d6 2) have NOT come in**; the hauler bringing them is late, nobody at the Rest knows when. The Valk flies with the nose multi-laser only (untested) unless they wait.
- ~1136: Tamsin: 'We can't wait. We have to go now; Kesh's orders.' The pods stay on order at the Rest (Kesh's people to hold them). Mira: she'll test the nose gun when they drop out. Kell: 'Light, listen up.' The brief is Tamsin's.
- ~1137-1145: Tamsin's brief in the hold: Gallowmere (agri world, ~4 days' warp); the garden's silos; the Lamps ~day 55 (13 days); Gallowmere's choir silent four days; the leak was a scribe in Voke's Spire, and one ran; infiltration first under Administratum tithe-assessors' papers; find Kesh's contact at the main grain port, find the garden; then the full assault. The Light quiet after it; Tamsin has more to say.
- ~1146: Tamsin's words after the brief (tally: a briefing and a speech, CHA): a small team in first to get the information and get out; then the hit. An open world, so the fight is on the ground, not a hive: boots on the ground, Teo from above, Mira in the sky; the Light-bringers go in on the assault and leave no heretic standing, nowhere to run; their last stand in this sector, wiped out, the area kept clear. 'Not just for us... for them' (pointing to the ring): for the two girls to not have a moment's worry, and for the people on this ring not to worry about infiltration. 'We are the Light, and we will show them that we mean it.' The hold answered (Morcant first, then all of them).
- ~1148: Tamsin circled the Light for a talk: sick of what the cult does to people, forcing them into what they're not; men who joined because it was their last hope; a little girl made part of their manufacturing; all the sickness and death for their god; 'they used and tortured one of our own' (Maren); no more days for them; march on, here for vengeance. 'WHAT ARE WE?' The hold: 'THE LIGHT!' (three times, Kell leading the third).
- ~1150: (the bridge vox had only carried the shout to the berth master, not the brief.) Tamsin: 'Take us out, Mira.' ~1152 cast off from berth 38; out through the Rest's traffic lanes; ~1400 the jump: into the warp for Gallowmere (~4 days: out ~day 46 afternoon, ~9 days before the Lamps).
