## Day 50 (from ~2228 day 49)

- ~2228 day 49: Tamsin went to Brenner at his station off the bridge, the city vox murmuring low.
- ~2230: "We have a location?" Number 7, the Quay of Weights, a kilometre downriver: four storeys on the quay front, the counting-house below and the family above, a private river landing out back, a walled cart yard. "Which two are the ones I need to chat to?" Abner Coyle, the driver, and Rudd Kemp, the plate man, both in the east vault. "I remember what they look like." Tamsin went to the bunks: Corvane up and restless, healed; Nessa oiling her knives; Dag sitting up in his collar at the end.
- ~2242: In the east vault Tamsin walked up to Rudd Kemp, "This one," then to Abner Coyle, "This one," and walked out. Nessa kept them apart: Kemp in the gatehouse guardroom, Coyle in the old vox room next door, Dag and Corvane on the doors.
- ~2244: The bet. "I bet I can crack Kemp before you can Coyle." Not Thrones: "If you win, I train you on all the knife and hand-to-hand I know. But if I win, you owe me a one-on-one bare fist in the ring." Nessa: "Either way I get my hands on you. Deal."
- ~2246: Tamsin sat down across from Kemp. "Rudd Kemp, family heirloom man, ran when it got hard... ties to Sallis men. I'm not just some raider taking you in. I'm an Interrogator, and that's what I do. So make this easy for me: where is she at?" Kemp: "Home. Where she always is. You've had the house off the registry, I'd wager." And: "I ran because the cart was done. Not me." Next door, Coyle was already talking.
- ~2249: "Number 7, downriver of Silo Row. Sallis and Daughters, tall merchant house, four storeys, flat to the quay front, sound familiar? Yeah, I know. Family lives above and a private river landing out back. I know it. But what's inside is for you to tell. Work with me and I can work with you." Kemp gave him the inside: the counting-house below, two men on the street door at night and one on the landing, the widow on the third floor, the strongroom in the cellar. Then: "What's she done? I drive her money. I don't count it."
- ~2252: "Plaguing the innocent and implanting spores in people to turn them into a mindless horde or walkers... you wanna be a part of that? You can be, and suffer for it in my report, or I say in there that you had no idea and cooperated nicely. How about that? Speak and you'll get out nicely." Kemp went grey. "Prove it. Show me. Then I'll tell you every door in that house." Next door, Coyle had a coughing fit and lost his thread.
- ~2255: Tamsin laid the receipts in front of Kemp, every one stamped Sallis & Daughters, "for the Lamps bake." "Now first think about it... I'm the Inquisition: you think I come over here to throw around false accusations and destroy silos and take out bakers for no reason? Bakers? For bread? Use your brain... Want proof? Here you go... Want to fix yourself, use your brain." Kemp read them all. Then he gave every door: the barred street door and its two men, the clerks' side door off the cart yard, the river landing and its man, a coal chute into the cellar, a skylight on the roof, and Old Brannoch asleep across the widow's door.
