## Day 50 (from ~2228 day 49)

- ~2228 day 49: Tamsin went to Brenner at his station off the bridge, the city vox murmuring low.
- ~2230: "We have a location?" Number 7, the Quay of Weights, a kilometre downriver: four storeys on the quay front, the counting-house below and the family above, a private river landing out back, a walled cart yard. "Which two are the ones I need to chat to?" Abner Coyle, the driver, and Rudd Kemp, the plate man, both in the east vault. "I remember what they look like." Tamsin went to the bunks: Corvane up and restless, healed; Nessa oiling her knives; Dag sitting up in his collar at the end.
- ~2242: In the east vault Tamsin walked up to Rudd Kemp, "This one," then to Abner Coyle, "This one," and walked out. Nessa kept them apart: Kemp in the gatehouse guardroom, Coyle in the old vox room next door, Dag and Corvane on the doors.
- ~2244: The bet. "I bet I can crack Kemp before you can Coyle." Not Thrones: "If you win, I train you on all the knife and hand-to-hand I know. But if I win, you owe me a one-on-one bare fist in the ring." Nessa: "Either way I get my hands on you. Deal."
- ~2246: Tamsin sat down across from Kemp. "Rudd Kemp, family heirloom man, ran when it got hard... ties to Sallis men. I'm not just some raider taking you in. I'm an Interrogator, and that's what I do. So make this easy for me: where is she at?" Kemp: "Home. Where she always is. You've had the house off the registry, I'd wager." And: "I ran because the cart was done. Not me." Next door, Coyle was already talking.
- ~2249: "Number 7, downriver of Silo Row. Sallis and Daughters, tall merchant house, four storeys, flat to the quay front, sound familiar? Yeah, I know. Family lives above and a private river landing out back. I know it. But what's inside is for you to tell. Work with me and I can work with you." Kemp gave him the inside: the counting-house below, two men on the street door at night and one on the landing, the widow on the third floor, the strongroom in the cellar. Then: "What's she done? I drive her money. I don't count it."
- ~2252: "Plaguing the innocent and implanting spores in people to turn them into a mindless horde or walkers... you wanna be a part of that? You can be, and suffer for it in my report, or I say in there that you had no idea and cooperated nicely. How about that? Speak and you'll get out nicely." Kemp went grey. "Prove it. Show me. Then I'll tell you every door in that house." Next door, Coyle had a coughing fit and lost his thread.
- ~2255: Tamsin laid the receipts in front of Kemp, every one stamped Sallis & Daughters, "for the Lamps bake." "Now first think about it... I'm the Inquisition: you think I come over here to throw around false accusations and destroy silos and take out bakers for no reason? Bakers? For bread? Use your brain... Want proof? Here you go... Want to fix yourself, use your brain." Kemp read them all. Then he gave every door: the barred street door and its two men, the clerks' side door off the cart yard, the river landing and its man, a coal chute into the cellar, a skylight on the roof, and Old Brannoch asleep across the widow's door.
- ~2258: "Guards, locks, how many people: I know you know, you're holding it from me... Want to make a difference and go down a different path... now that's a man who won't run. But from what I'm seeing you already run from problems well enough... that right?" Kemp gave the rest: the bar and chain on the street door; the strongroom's combination, known to the widow and her steward Ammon Price; in the house the widow, her daughter Miss Ilse who keeps the books, the steward, a cook, two maids, Old Brannoch and the night men. "I'm not running now. Am I."
- ~2300: "Getting there, but you're still holding more: what they carry, what's important in there, who has what permissions, what she does and when she's there... Spit it out, tell me everything you know, and I'll make sure it goes on paper." Kemp broke. Carbines and clubs on the street door, a shotgun on the landing, Old Brannoch with a heavy pistol and a long knife. The strongroom held the true ledgers; the clean books were Miss Ilse's on the fourth floor. Price the steward had every key and the combination. The widow went to the weigh-guild's exchange at nine with Brannoch and was home by noon, and to chapel on Chapel Square every second evening at six. Tamsin won the bet: Nessa owed him a bare-fist bout in the ring.
- ~2302: "So next time I shouldn't use the tools in my hand? Gosh, I think that sounds like salt." Nessa: "Salt? I'm a smuggler's daughter. I invented salt." "Dag, Corv, take them back." Kemp and Coyle went back to the east vault; the receipts went back into Tamsin's satchel.
- ~2310: Tamsin let Kell make the plan and laughed when it came: a day of eyes on No. 7 (Isk on a roof, Nessa at the exchange, Sorel on Brannoch, Brenner with the wall-seeing auspex), then the chapel if she keeps her routine, or the house at night by the coal chute and the skylight if she doesn't. "If it was me I'd just rush it... But smarter is better no matter the situation. Sounds good to me."
- ~2312: "Uhhh, I'll think about it, Sergeant. Make sure you tell everyone silent kits, then." Kell: "Silent kits. Done." Tamsin went to Ollin, still up past eleven, logging the two hauls crate by crate.
- ~2314: The phosphex would go to Kesh, so she could see how they'd got it. "Anyway, I need a silenced gun to bring up, silenced and concealable." Ollin had four silenced stub pistols in the cage and put the cleanest on the counter, the weigh-house's, barely fired, with a shoulder rig and a crate of AP stub rounds if plates were a worry.
- ~2316: Tamsin signed for it, strapped the shoulder rig over his carapace under his coat and loaded AP. "We got any more mags we found? No way this guy had one round in the gun." Two spares had been in the same cupboard, and the Crane Yard pistols took the same pattern: three spare magazines, all loaded AP.
- ~2328: Tamsin went to Dace, still at his bench under one lamp with Sorel's hellgun stripped in front of him.
- ~2332: "A spare hellgun, of course." Sorel would carry a compact Accatran from Fourteen's haul; his own stayed on the bench. "And when you get a minute, go see Ollin; he has something for you." Dace put down his file at once.
- ~2340: For the widow, if it's the crowd: "I'll have to wear the mesh." The spare mesh from the customs house, the refractor under a coat, no helm; the silenced pistol and the kukri.
