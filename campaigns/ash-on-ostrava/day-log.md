## Day 46

- ~1005-1200: Tamsin: 'Nope, let's go ahead and go for it.' Out at Gallowmere (an ordinary drop). Mira: the lanes are empty, no barges, nothing moving; Brenner: nothing in high orbit on the auspex. Port control hailing: a human voice, Hearthgate, the main grain port, asking for ident. ~4 hours to orbit.
- ~1203: Tamsin on the bridge took the vox (OOC check: 'Inquisition' would blow the cover; the player changed it): 'Administratum tithe-assessors... what is the situation here?' Vael: eight days ago the governor (Ansel Corrow) quarantined all outbound grain for a blight in the outer tithe-districts; no barge lifts till the harvest's inspected; the choir took ill the same week; they're the first ship in a week. He'll give them a berth on Hearthgate's upper port; asked for the ship's name and the warrant's code.
- ~1205: Tamsin: 'Sounds good. The ship's name is the Gardener Going' (the player kept it as bait after the OOC warning), and read Kesh's warrant authority code. Vael logged both without a pause; assigned upper port berth 7 at Hearthgate; the tithe-reeve's office will want to see the assessors; ~4 hours to orbit.
- ~1207: Tamsin on the bead: 'Light, everyone to the bridge.' They crowded the bridge and spilled into the corridor and the briefing-room hatch, in full kit (Ferro last, from the node).
- ~1209: Tamsin to the Light on the bridge: obviously something's wrong; they go in and scout, then all go in, to end this once and for all; be ready; it ends soon. 'LIGHT' in answer. Kell: posts and kit as they are; the assault team ready to drop on the boss's word. Nessa: assessors don't wear carapace, boss.
- ~1211: Tamsin: the assessor team strips down to flak under cover coats; when they regroup for the assault, the team brings the rest of their gear. **Coats (d6 3): Ollin turned up four old Munitorum greatcoats from the stores, grey, long, a little moth-eaten: passable for clerks.** Brenner: four data-slates and a ledger for props.
- ~1213: Tamsin carries Last Word, his knife and the Kasr Frontier under the coat. (OOC: a rifle won't hide under a greatcoat; Ferro's fix offered: a tithe-assessor's grain-probe case.) - ~1215: Tamsin: 'No, it's fine. A man holding a rifle never made anyone suspicious nowadays.' The Kasr Frontier goes slung over his greatcoat. Nessa rolled her eyes; Sorel: half the men on a farm world carry one; not usually the clerks.
- ~1220: Tamsin changed: flak, a grey Munitorum greatcoat over it, Last Word under the coat, the knife, the plague rebreather on the belt beneath the coat, the Kasr Frontier slung openly. Carapace, refractor field, Vigil, Stinger and Shameholder packed in a kit bag for the assault team (Kell to carry Shame).
- ~1235: Tamsin gathered the assessor team in the briefing room: Nessa, Sorel and Tey in grey greatcoats with slates, flak under, sidearms hidden.
- ~1237: Tamsin's plan: find Tithe-Reeve Varrow first and 'talk' to him; then find the silo; once it's confirmed, double back and call in the team for the assault. 'But that's in a universe where everything goes to plan...' Nessa: this isn't that universe. Sorel: then what's the plan when it doesn't? A word for 'come now', and a place to fall back to.
- ~1239: Tamsin: he'll do the talking with Varrow; the codeword 'Harvest'? Nessa: every farmer on Gallowmere says harvest ten times a day; on an open channel it'll be heard by accident. Sorel: fall back to the ship, berth seven, upper port, unless the boss names somewhere better once they've seen the ground.
- ~1242: Two codes settled: **walking in: the garden's own challenge and answer from the Mart, 'Thorn' / 'Bloom'** (Tamsin's idea to use their words; Nessa's split). **Calling the Light down, 'come now, everything': 'MAREN'** (Tey's idea). **Fallback: the ship, upper port berth 7** (Sorel). Tamsin does the talking to Varrow. Tey grinned; Sorel: 'Tey.'
- ~1246: Tamsin: someone runs the silo, a ringleader, who'll have the answers about the jars and the reasons. Nessa: so take him alive. Sorel: a regiment's limb has an officer (the Sixth fights like a real regiment): find the man the others salute. Tey: Wolfer? (Nobody knows where Wolfer is.)

Sorel, putting it right: the Sixth's main strength is on Atoma Prime, with Inquisitor Grendyl's people fighting them (Aldwyn's word); where Wolfer himself is, nobody's said; he was freed off a prison transport once. - ~1250: Tamsin: it'll be a whole regiment's worth, their last known presence in this sector; not a fair fight but an unfair one. Nessa: then we make it unfair for them first. Sorel: Kesh called it a limb, not the regiment; how big a limb, they'll learn from the ground. Tey, quietly: he'll stay bored till it's time not to be.
- ~1300: On the bridge, ~3 hours out: the port still silent; Brenner offered to magnify Hearthgate on the way in (berth 7).
- ~1302: Tamsin asked Brenner whether he can see the silos once they're closer. Brenner: from here the big complexes are the pale dots on the fields; in low orbit (~1530) he can pick out single silos and anything moving round them, but he can't read a number off a roof; which one is 'the Ninth' needs a district chart (the port's, or Varrow's). He offered a quiet sweep of the fields with the auspex on the way in.
- ~1303: Tamsin: yes to the sweep; then the ship's scanner for a close-up; once they find it, the Valk drops them a little off to the side and they walk up close.

One silo complex ~300 km upriver of Hearthgate runs warm, like a compost heap, with a cold smear round it that won't hold a lock (like the customs house on the Mart); grain haulers on its one road, the only ground traffic on this face. Which district: no numbers readable from orbit.
- ~1530: Low orbit; the close-up on the ship's augur: about a dozen silos on a river bend round a rail spur and loading yard; tarps over half the yard; haulers in and out; a ring of fields cut to stubble round it, ~500 m wide; a few figures, no formations. Mira: that ring is a killing ground; and a Valk dropping off an assessors' ship in daylight will be seen from Hearthgate (berth 7 expected ~1600).
- ~1532: Tamsin's plan: dock as assessors and talk to Varrow; back to the ship and regroup; out at night in the Valk to scout the complex; back with the info; then squad up for the assault. - ~1600: Docked at Hearthgate upper port, berth 7 (the orbital dock at the head of the lifter threads; the ground port below). The assessor team out through the hatch: Tamsin, Nessa, Sorel, Tey. Waiting at the berth gate: Tithe-Reeve Osk Varrow himself, and with him Under-Secretary Jarel Quint of the governor's office (off-world visitors are to be accompanied during the quarantine). Quint asked about the rifle.
- ~1601: Tamsin to Quint, flat and harsh: 'Came to a world one day just doing my job... and got stabbed by a corrupt government... now I carry. So I don't give a damn if it's unusual.' Intimidation 14 + 2 CHA + 4 prof - 1 open rifle = 19: it worked. Quint went pale, said the governor has nothing to hide, and wrote something on his slate. Varrow: the car down is waiting; his office at the ground port has the ledgers.
- ~1603-1610: Down the gantry to the passenger car, Tamsin walking beside Varrow, Quint at Varrow's other side; Tamsin asked why the quarantine, and whether the grain tithe will be affected, mixing his real questions with an assessor's. Quint gave the official line: a grain-rot blight in the outer districts, found by the governor's agri-inspectors eight days ago; no barge lifts till the harvest's inspected; the governor expects it lifted by the Harvest Lamps. Varrow: the tithe will be late either way, nine days to the Lamps; if the quarantine runs past them, the whole tithe's short (that's why assessors came, he'd expect); the outer districts aren't his ledgers; he'll have the district charts out in his office. Into the passenger car down, about an hour to the ground port.
- ~1611: Tamsin: 'The Lamps, what do you mean by that?' Quint glanced up (an assessor who hasn't heard of the Lamps?); Varrow covered: Terra's men never have. The Harvest Lamps: the night the harvest is done, kept all along the grain routes; on Gallowmere every district lights lamps on its silos and roads, and the first tithe barges lift at dawn after; the biggest night of the year here. Tamsin got in the car and waved Nessa, Sorel and Tey quietly to the bench behind; Varrow and Quint sat with him in front, Nessa right behind Quint. The car dropped: about an hour to the ground port.
- ~1615: In the car, Tamsin: 'Anything else you know of that happens on the Lamps?' Varrow: feasts, every farm hands out bread at its gate; the reeves walk their silos and seal each with tithe-wax so the count can't change before the barges; the priests bless the first barge at dawn; and every soul on Gallowmere is out on the roads all night, under the lamps. Quint: this year the governor lights the first lamp himself at Hearthgate and speaks to every district on the vox.
- ~1618: Tamsin: has anyone been sent out to inspect all the silos and find the cause; the Imperium is cracking down on plague areas that do more damage and hide other things inside, so they want it dealt with fast. Quint (his stylus stopped for a moment at 'hiding other things inside'): the governor's own agri-inspectors are in the outer districts now; their reports go straight to the governor; the assessors will have a summary; there's nothing hidden on Gallowmere but grain. Varrow: the reeves were ordered out of the outer districts till it's done, his ledgers stop at the cordon; Terra wanting it fast suits him fine.
- ~1620: Tamsin looked Quint over (Perception 17 + 5 WIS + 4 prof = 26): a faint sweet smell under heavy scented oil, like the Mart's bloom; pale gold dust in the welts of his polished shoes; a thin cord round his neck under the collar seal, something small hanging out of sight under his shirt. Quint didn't notice.
- ~1622: Tamsin looked at Quint, serious: 'Thorn.' (Deception 12 + 2 CHA = 14: a near miss, a partial: first narrated as a flat fail, fixed at the player's call.) Quint's stylus stopped. A long look; then, very quietly: 'Bloom.' His eyes flicked to Varrow: 'Not here. Not in front of the reeve.' Varrow heard both words; he looked from one to the other, went pale, and edged away from Tamsin on the bench. Nessa leaned forward behind Quint. The ground port a few minutes below.
- ~1623: Tamsin to Quint: 'Just wanted to be sure... I'll let it be for now.' Quint: 'Later, then.' His hand came off the cord; the stylus went back to work. Varrow sat rigid the rest of the way.
- ~1630: The car into the ground port at Hearthgate: a vast grain terminal, the lifter heads stopped, barges idle on the river quays, warehouses shut, a few dockers and port wardens about. Quint stayed at their side ('the governor insists'); at the gate he slipped a folded note to a port runner, who ran off into the terminal. Nessa caught it and gave Tamsin the smallest nod toward the runner. Varrow walked ahead stiffly toward his office.

- ~1640: Varrow's office, up an iron stair over the terminal floor: one room, a long table stacked with tithe ledgers, a window over the idle quays, and a big district chart on the wall with every tithe-district numbered. Varrow, pale and stiff, set out the ledgers; Quint took a chair by the door.
