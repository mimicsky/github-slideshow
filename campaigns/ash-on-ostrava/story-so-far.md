# Ash on Ostrava — the story so far

Written to be read cold. Read this, then `README.md` in this folder, then
`../../DND_CODEX.md` for the rules.

**Where we stopped:** end of session 1, night, in the cult's cistern under
Tower Nine. Tamsin has just accepted Varsaine's pin and become an acolyte of the
Ordos. Next scene starts fresh.

---

## The setting

Warhammer 40,000. Eleven months after the fall of **Cadia**, the fortress world
that held for ten thousand years until its gate at **Kasr Holn** opened from the
inside. **Ostrava Secundus** is a promethium refinery world where the Cadian
survivors were scattered into scratch units.

## Who Tamsin is

**Tamsin Rook**, a Cadian corporal. On Cadia's last night, as a private, Tamsin
carried a message to the gate control room and saw a major at the controls with
two officers on the floor. The major sent Tamsin away. Forty minutes later the
gate opened. Tamsin never delivered the message and never told anyone.

## Session 1

On third watch with **Private Dace**, Tamsin saw an unmarked shuttle land and
the same major step off it, now wearing a colonel's braid and a dead
regiment's pin. Tamsin crept to the bunker grille, heard him demand the roster
of all Kasr Holn survivors, and saw a case of Cadian dog tags being sorted into
"dead" and "unconfirmed" piles, and an Inquisitorial rosette on his belt.

Summoned to the bunker, Tamsin tried to bluff, kept a hidden knife and was
caught out by both. Then Tamsin told the truth. The man was **Interrogator
Varsaine** of the Ordos. He says he did not open the gate: someone behind the
door took his hand and left him for dead. He was hunting the people in that
room, and one of them was posted to Ostrava under a false name: **Dace**, really
**Halvic Teodor**.

Tamsin talked Varsaine into letting them work Dace alone, with Sergeant **Kell**
shadowing. Playing a soldier sick of officers, Tamsin drew Dace in: the
**Open Gate** cult, its leader the **Keyholder**, a freighter the ***Calyx
Dawn*** leaving in three days, and a plan for the north line's guns to fall
silent. Over a vox, the Keyholder heard Tamsin's name and said: *bring the
corporal, gently.*

Tamsin went to the meeting under Tower Nine: forty people, a Chaos star painted
inside an open gate, and handprints of blood. The Keyholder knew a detail only
someone in that control room could know. He gave Tamsin a brass data-key to
silence the Hydra battery, and then a ritual knife to give blood to the gate.

Tamsin shouted Varsaine's signal phrase, **"the gate held,"** disguised as the
knife getting into their head. The Keyholder didn't buy it, but Kell's team
heard it and came down the stair. Tamsin drove the ritual knife into the
Keyholder, tore his laspistol away and shot **Sergeant Brask** dead. Then
Tamsin made Dace surrender, chased the Keyholder into the culverts and shot the
signal box out of his hand before he could summon "friends beyond the wall."

The Keyholder is **Captain Oren Malcade**, 122nd Cadian, officially dead. He
said three people opened Cadia: himself, Dace at the door, and an officer who
gave the order to stand the gate guns down. That order carried the seal of the
**Lord Castellan's Strategium**: the command of Cadia itself.

Varsaine made Tamsin an **acolyte** of the Ordos. Tamsin's answer: *"Ready for
the next one, sir."*
