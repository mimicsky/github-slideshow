# Ash on Ostrava: fight card

Ten fight rules on one screen, copied short from `DND_CODEX.md` Parts 6 and 8, `wh40k_gear/bestiary/roles.md` and the campaign sheet (`ash-on-ostrava-campaign-sheet.md`); every line ends with where the full text lives.
**The codex, roles.md and the sheet win.** When this card and they disagree, they are right: change them first, then this card. Nothing goes on this card that is not already written there, and no rulings' history: that stays in the codex.
It is steps 6-9 of the campaign README's Fight checklist in short; steps 1-5 (where we are, the enemy, each person's kit and traits, each weapon's own entry) still get read first.
## 1. The beat
- One **main thing** + a **short move** (a few metres, or to the next cover): a shot string, a throw or one launcher shot, a melee chain, or a patch, reload, vent, jam clear, item, lift-pack hop or sprint (codex, A beat).
- **Everyone acts every beat**, allies and enemies, each with their own open roll; a Commander's order and a duelist's shot-and-swing are free extras (codex, How the player likes to play; A beat).
- **Surprise:** a group that hasn't seen the attackers when the first shot goes can't act or defend that beat, and every attack on it gets **+2**; next beat it fights normally (codex, A beat).
- **Run and gun:** sprint and still fire a string at **-2**. **Leftover shots** go into another enemy on their own unless Tamsin said hold (swap -1). **Tamsin:** Action Surge once a fight = two main things (codex, A beat).
## 2. To hit
- Hidden target number: **10** easy (close, still), **15** normal, **20** hard (far, moving, small); people in melee use the exchange instead (codex, Shots and hits).
- Cover adds to it: light **+1**, half **+2**, heavy **+4**, full = can't be shot (except through-cover guns, grenades round it, flamers); only from the front or side it blocks; shooting from cover is free, moving out of it loses it that beat (codex, Cover).
- **Solid hit** = the total beats the number by **3+**. Taking someone alive with a limb shot: **+2** to the number (codex, Shots and hits; Rulings).
## 3. Roll checklist (every roll; write each bonus by name)
1. **Base:** d20 + stat (+1 per full 10 levels); melee STR, or the better of STR/DEX with a finesse weapon. **Tamsin adds proficiency +4** (attacks, trained skills; Remarkable Athlete +2 on untrained STR/DEX/CON); **the Light add no proficiency** (codex, Roll checklist 1; sheet, How the Light rolls).
2. **Specialty:** **+2** (or its drilled level), only on its kind of roll (codex, Roll checklist 2; sheet, Specialty table).
3. **Role and orders:** the roller's role rules; an ordered ally **+2 and acts first**; Kell's **+1** to the whole Light when he is present (codex, Roll checklist 3; section 11 below).
4. **Surprise:** **+2** on a surprised group (codex, Roll checklist 4).
5. **Gear:** every attachment by slot and every eye (optic and HUD eye stack), blessings, the gun's entry; crits on a **natural 20; Tamsin 19-20; Vigil 17-20** (codex, Roll checklist 5; Shots and hits).
6. **The string:** chain, swap, recoil, drum (-1 quick-draw), string limit, dual pistols: section 4 (codex, Roll checklist 6).
7. **Movement:** airborne (own shots -2, the Light -1; shots at them -3), run and gun -2, from cover free (codex, Roll checklist 7).
8. **Standing bonuses:** traits, finished books, armour and fields (codex, Roll checklist 8; sheet, Tamsin's and the Light's standing modifiers).
9. **Conditions:** Hurt **-1**, Badly hurt **-2**; food, fatigue, sickened, blinded; cover on the target; darkness or smoke (a low-light optic or lumen cancels it); a Dirty or Neglected gun's jam range (codex, Roll checklist 9).
10. **Saves:** purity seals, rebreathers, Indomitable; an enemy's CON save = d20 + STR bonus (codex, Roll checklist 10).
11. **Will checks:** +1 per finished manual; hard methods +5% and their risk roll: section 12 (codex, Roll checklist 11).
- **Stacking:** the same effect from two places counts **once**; different sources add up (codex, Roll checklist, Stacking).
## 4. Strings (shots)
- **Chain:** the first shot **hits: +2** to the next; a later shot **hits: +1**; any **miss: -1**; only the last shot's result counts, never stacked (codex, HR6 Shot chains).
- **Length:** **3** shots by default, or the gun's own limit; **Tamsin +1** on every string (Extra Attack) (codex, A beat; sheet, Tamsin's standing modifiers).
- **Recoil**, every shot after the first: **0** las weapons and stub/auto pistols; **1** autoguns on burst, SMGs and machine pistols, shotguns, revolvers, hand cannons, a braced heavy stubber; **2** bolt weapons, a heavy stubber on the move, anti-materiel rifles (codex, Gear rules: Swapping, recoil and attachment slots).
- **Swap:** a shot at a different target than the last one in the string **-1** (codex, Gear rules: Swapping, recoil and attachment slots).
- **Dual pistols:** both guns' limits added together, **-3 on every shot**; or fire one at full value and the other is a free reload (codex, HR5 Dual-wielding).
## 5. Melee: the exchange
| Defender's total vs the attacker's | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Lower or equal | **Lands** |
| 1-2 higher | **Block:** no damage; the attacker keeps initiative, the chain carries on |
| 3+ higher | **Parry:** no damage; the chain ends, **initiative flips**, the defender swings next beat |
- A **raw d20 of 17+** can't be blocked, parried or dodged. Each swing is one exchange; **a defender answers 2 attackers a beat**, the rest get through; a doorway cuts how many can reach (codex, HR5 The exchange).
- **Chains a beat:** light **3** (knives, daggers, machetes, hatchets), medium **2** (one-handed swords, chainswords, axes, batons, mauls), heavy **2** (two-handed). Tamsin +1 swing (Shameholder 3). Two melee weapons: +1 swing, +1 on attacks, nothing on defence; back to guns costs a beat (codex, HR5 Melee chains; Dual-wielding; sheet, Tamsin's standing modifiers).
- **Stat:** finesse (knives, daggers, bayonets in hand, short blades) = the better of STR or DEX; chainswords, axes, mauls, hammers, power fists, long blades = STR; grappling STR, slipping DEX; both sides, attacking and defending (codex, HR5 Which stat).
- **Knife defence is footwork:** a block is a slip, a parry an evade-and-counter, same math. Anyone may **dodge** instead of using the weapon: **d20 + DEX**, proficiency only if trained in Acrobatics (**Tamsin: Remarkable Athlete +2**) (codex, HR5 Knife defence and dodging).
## 6. Health, Down and breaking
- **Fine → Hurt (-1) → Badly hurt (-2, no sprint) → Down (out).** A hit = one step, unless armour shrugs it; a **crit = one extra step** (vs armour that would have shrugged it: one step, no extra). Extra stages before Hurt: Zealot and Skitarius +1, Brute +2, Ogryn +3 (codex, HR4 Health as a state; Shots and hits; Armour).
- **Wear-down:** every **3** shrugged hits on one person in a fight = one step; **power armour 5**. Traits move it: Glass jaw every 2 (Varga), Thick-skinned's first doesn't count (codex, Armour; HR9 Traits; sheet, traits table).
- **Going Down drops** what's in the hands (a slung long gun stays; a drop-prone gun rolls) (codex, Gear rules: Going Down drops your weapons).
- **Enemies Down:** d6 each after the fight, **1-3 dead, 4-6 alive** (+2 on the d6 if every step came from limb shots or unarmed blows) (codex, Breaking and Down; Rulings).
- **The Light never die from Down:** out of the fight, and dead only if finished off or hit once more while Down. A medic's patch (Down → Hurt) puts them back in at Hurt (codex, Breaking and Down).
- **Break check** when the **leader falls or half are Down:** open d20, **1-10 surrender, 11-16 fight on, 17-20 run**; cultists surrender only on **1-5** (6-16 fight on) unless their leader just fell; a group that fights on is checked again when another quarter go Down; an enforcer stops breaks while it stands (codex, Breaking and Down).
## 7. Armour (what a hit does)
| Armour | Shrugs off | Still gets through |
| --- | --- | --- |
| None, coat | nothing | everything |
| Flak | glancing hits; stub and autogun at range | las, hellguns, shotguns up close, chain, frags |
| Reinforced flak | stub, autogun and glancing las, fully | clean las hits, hellguns, shotguns up close, chain, frags |
| Carapace | lasguns, autoguns, stub, shotguns at range, most blades, frags (unless the throw was a solid hit) | hellguns, hot-shot, bolters, heavy stubbers, chain, power weapons, plasma, krak |
| Heavy carapace | the above + hellguns and heavy stubbers at range | hellguns and heavy stubbers up close (inside ~15 m), bolters, chain on a solid hit, power weapons, plasma, melta, krak |
| Ceramite-forged | the above + hellguns and heavy stubbers even up close | bolters, chain on a solid hit, power weapons, plasma, melta, krak |
| Power armour | almost everything a soldier carries | bolters, plasma on a solid hit, melta, power weapons, krak |
- **Part-armour** counts only from the side it covers: **Tamsin is heavy carapace from the front**, carapace from the side and back. **Crits always get through** (one step vs armour that would have shrugged it). Armour takes damage; the DM says when it fails (codex, Armour; sheet, Full kit, Tamsin).
## 8. Heat, packs and fields
- **Packs:** hot-shot (hellguns, hellpistols, hot-shot conversions) **30 shots**; regular las (lasguns, lascarbines, laspistols) **45**; a pack swap mid-fight is the beat's move (codex, Gear rules: Pack sizes).
- **Vigil:** crits **17-20**, **each crit +1 heat**; **vent a beat at 3**; firing anyway at 3: d20, **1-5 cracks the coil**; heat clears after venting or at the fight's end (sheet, Full kit, Tamsin; inventory.md, Tamsin's weapons in detail).
- **Plasma:** **1 heat per 2 shots** in a fight; at **3** it must vent a beat; firing anyway: d20, **1-5 overheats** (bearer Hurt, gun out). **Hot-shot conversion:** one step harder, ammo twice as fast, **+1 heat per chain** on a gun that builds heat (codex, Gear rules: Plasma heat; Hot-shot conversion).
- Any other gun's heat, string limit and quirks: its own catalogue entry (codex, Gear rules intro).
- **Refractor field:** light hits (what carapace shrugs) stopped automatically, at most **3 a beat** (a 4th gets through and the field is down 1 beat); heavy hits: d6, **5-6** stopped (codex, Gear rules: Refractor field).
- **Airborne:** own shots **-2 (the Light -1)**, shots at them **-3**; anyone who shoots at a jumper this beat is open to surprise (+2) from the ground (codex, Gear rules: Airborne).
- **Doses:** d6, **1-3 lasts 3 beats, 4-6 lasts 4**; a beat after the last dose ends, **crash to Badly hurt** (3+ doses in a row: to Down) (codex, Gear rules: Doses).
## 9. Gun condition
- **Clean** (cleaned after its last fight or within ~3 days): never jams, a natural 1 is just a miss. **Dirty** (~30 shots or ~3 days of use since cleaning): **a natural 1 jams**, a beat to clear. **Neglected** (a week uncleaned, or mud, grit, vacuum, a gale): **jams on 1-3, a natural 1 seizes it** until a smith fixes it (codex, Gear rules: Gun condition and jams).
- Las guns count one step cleaner; never-jam guns ignore it. **Enemies:** cultists and raiders Dirty or Neglected (even chance), pirates Dirty, traitors and traitor elites Clean (codex, Gear rules: Gun condition and jams).
- **The Light:** all Clean unless the Guns line under the Full kit says otherwise; Stinger's bolt jams only past 25 straight rounds; a silencer fouls a gun faster than ~30 shots: its own entry (sheet, Full kit, Guns and Tamsin's row; catalogue kit.md, Muzzles).
## 10. Knife bouts (the mat)
- Blunted knives, chalked edges: **d20 + DEX each, no specialties; Tamsin +11** (7 DEX + 4 proficiency). A landed strike on hand, arm or leg is **minor (1 hit)**; on head, neck, torso or back **vital (2 hits)**, and only on a **raw 17+ or a win by 5+**, otherwise minor (codex, Matches, bouts and knife bouts; sheet, Tamsin's standing modifiers).
- **First to 2 hits takes the round, first to 5 rounds the bout.** Play blocks and parries straight through, stop when a touch lands, hand back to the player. Sword bouts on the mat: blunt practice swords, **d20 + STR** (codex, Matches, bouts and knife bouts).
## 11. The Light's roles (the Light count as traitor elites: each role's elite side note applies)
- **Tamsin, Commander:** **1 order a beat, 2 if both go to specialists**; the ordered ally gets **+2 and acts first**; it doesn't cost his own action; only in scenes run in beats (fights, chases, breaches, disasters) (roles.md, Commander; sheet, Tamsin's standing modifiers).
- **Kell, Leader:** **+1** to every Light-bringer and Tamsin whenever he is present and giving orders (roles.md, Leader; sheet, Roles).
- **Sallow, Medic:** one patch a beat, one step back, once per person (only patches that work count); **d20 + WIS + 4**, no penalties; Down → Hurt costs a stimm; anyone else patches Hurt → Fine only, at d20 + WIS (roles.md, Medic; codex, Rulings).
- **Varga, Jumper:** airborne her shots **-1**, shots at her **-3**; a hop and a string in one beat (roles.md, Jumper; codex, Gear rules: Airborne).
- **Tey, Gunslinger:** **+3** with one-handed guns only (pistols, machine pistols) (roles.md, Gunslinger; sheet, Roles).
- **Corvane, Duelist:** a shot **and** a swing in the same beat; **+1** if he does only one of them; **+2 DEX** moving between cover (roles.md, Duelist).
- **Morcant, Heavy gunner:** whoever he fires at takes **-2** on their shots (suppression), once; his specialty is braced fire +2, not a second -2 (roles.md, Heavy gunner; sheet, Specialty table).
- **Isk, Sniper:** **+1** on his shooting lines (roles.md, Sniper).
- **Hollis, Grenadier:** a grenade or launcher shot that misses by 1-2 still does **half effect** (roles.md, Grenadier; sheet, Roles).
- **Dace, Bodyguard:** once a beat, takes a hit aimed at the one he guards (roles.md, Bodyguard).
- **Sorel, Tracker:** **+2** to find hidden enemies, tracks and traps; an enemy can't break contact while he's on them (roles.md, Tracker).
- **Nessa, Infiltrator:** starts the fight hidden, behind or beside the enemy, quiet guns only; ambushes from the rear, fades if spotted (roles.md, Infiltrator).
## 12. Will checks
- The DM calls them and rolls openly: total **9 or less: 0-2%** Will lost, **10-14: 5-10%**, **15-19: 10-15%**, **20+: 15-20%**; a natural 20 doubles it, a natural 1 nothing. **Tamsin +1** (the provost's manual). **Hard methods:** **+5%** on each check, but a d20 each session, **1-3** the subject goes Down (and may die), and pain answers may be lies; **3 sessions a day at most** (codex, HR8 Will; sheet, The character).
