## How the player likes to play

The same list as the codex's Part 6, kept here so it is never missed on resume.

- **Let the player figure it out.** Show a clue once, then leave it. No repeats,
  no NPCs pointing at it, no nudges. The player says so out of character if
  stuck. Set by the player, session 9.
- **Stop after the player's action.** Resolve only what the player does, then
  hand control back. NPCs don't make big moves past it.
  **Don't move, read, think or feel for the player character either** (set by the player, session 10). Example: the player says *"I grab the slate and walk out of the room."* Wrong: Tamsin reads the roster on the way, crosses names off in his head, and heads somewhere. Right: he picks up the slate, walks out, and the scene stops at the door, with what he'd see there; what he does with the slate is the player's next move.
- **Stop at the first new decision point.** When an action has several steps ("let it shut, then we breach"), resolve up to the first moment where things change and the player could choose differently (a door shuts, a breach is about to start), then stop. The player may also say where to stop. Set by the player, Ash on Ostrava session 6.
- **In fights, everyone acts every beat.** Allies and enemies each take their
  own action with their own open roll, every beat. The pause-after-the-player
  rule is about story moves, not combat turns.
- **Matches and contests go round by round** (shootouts, bouts, races): roll one round, show it, stop for the player (who may coach or react), then the next. **In mat bouts a "round" is a touch:** play the blocks and parries straight through and stop only when a touch lands. Mat bouts are Dace's blunt practice swords only (no shields, no specialties): d20 + STR each. **Knife bouts** (Dace's blunted bayonets, chalked edges; no specialties; set day 32): **d20 + DEX each**; blocks are **slips**, parries are **evade-and-counter**; a landed strike to hand, arm or leg is **minor (1 hit)**, to head, neck, torso or back is **vital (2 hits)**: vital on a **raw 17+ or a win by 5+**, otherwise minor; **first to 2 hits wins the round, first to 5 rounds wins the bout**; Dace and Hollis judge. In real fights knives use the better of STR or DEX (codex house rule 5, "Which stat"). Never run a whole match in one go. Set by the player, session 10.
- **Narrate the player character in second person** ("you"), not "Tamsin" in the third person, in scenes. (The book stays third person.) Set by the player, session 10.
- **No game terms in the fiction.** NPCs never mention rolls, dice, natural 20s, DCs or modifiers; they describe what they saw. Set by the player, session 10.
- **Speech in quotes** is the character talking aloud; everything else is action.
- **Name + role, every time.** Every named NPC, place or item gets its role in
  brackets, every mention: Hespa-Vor (enginseer), Velk (ice world).
- **Dialogue as a script**, one line per speaker: `Kell (storm trooper
  sergeant): line`. Action stays as prose around it.
- **Describe places in detail, for free,** when asked. Perception is only for
  hidden things. **No text maps;** a drawn picture of the space (a rendered image) is fine when the player asks. Set by the player, Ash on Ostrava session 6.
- **The player rolls physical dice** and gives the raw number; the DM adds
  modifiers. NPC dice and undecided facts are rolled openly with a real RNG.
- **The world moves off-screen.** The first time the character meets someone
  after a gap (hours or days), say what that person has been doing meanwhile:
  **training** (logged as their daily rep), **something ordinary** (sleeping,
  cards, writing home), or **something that matters to the story** (talked to a
  prisoner, found something, had a falling-out). Weigh it by what's going on:
  if the player has ordered training, they mostly train; the wounded rest;
  people follow their own worries. Roll openly when it's genuinely undecided.
  Set by the player, session 5.
- **Don't roll ordinary dialogue.** NPCs answer from their own character and
  what they know; rolls only for turning points, real refusals, undecided facts.
  Set by the player, session 8.
- **NPCs only know what they could know.** A character knows something only if
  they saw it, were told it, or heard it from someone who could have; if it's
  gossip, the fiction shows who passed it on. No one knows a thing just because
  the player does. Set by the player, session 5.
- **Listeners only understand what they could know, too.** When an NPC mentions a person, place or event, the listener reacts as someone who knows it or doesn't (a prisoner doesn't know who Corvane is; the NPC either explains or the listener is lost). Set by the player, session 10.
- **Daily tally sheet:** every time the clock passes into a new day, show the day's training tally first (each person: what they did, +0.1 per hour or action, new stat levels).
- **Show the math on rolls (changed by the player, end of session 9):** write each roll as (die + bonuses = total), e.g. (12+6+1+2 = 21): the die, stat bonus, then each other bonus, then the total. Contests: (yours = total vs theirs = total).
- **Shop stock only on a visit.** Never list a shop's stock in a summary.
- **No repeats.** Save before writing the scene; the save note is just
  "(saved)". The scene is the last thing in the reply. NPCs don't re-explain,
  and the time and situation aren't restated when nothing changed.
- **Will for captives** (house rule 8): hidden Will % and breaking point; will checks take it down; Perception reads it.
- **The DM rolls will checks for the player** when something the player says or does would really land; the player doesn't have to call them. Set by the player, session 9.
- **Hidden DCs, near-miss partials, the exchange, shot chains, stat levels**
  (house rules 1, 3, 5, 6, 7).
- **Explain setting lore briefly** out of character when asked, limited to what
  the character would know. The player knows Darktide well; use it.
- **The player is a collector.** Put interesting things in the world to find;
  invented gear is fine if it fits the setting.
- **Fewer freebies.** Gear is earned through salvage, trades, favours and risk.
- **The player may propose** blessings, mods and backstory; the DM judges.
- **Save state** to the campaign files and push after anything important.
- **Full kit (set by the player, Ash on Ostrava session 10, day 33).** The campaign sheet's **Full kit** table is each person's standing loadout. 'Full kit for everyone' means exactly that table, unless the player says otherwise for a mission. **Keep it current in the same save:** whenever something is used up, lost, swapped, fitted, found or handed out (ammo, grenades and attachments too), change its row; don't add gear the player hasn't assigned. **The Light's container is open stock: anyone may take from it if they want; update both the person's row and the crate's count.** Power ratings are re-run from it.
- **The book: written at the end of every in-game day** (changed by the player, session 10),
  right after the daily tally, as full chapters in `book-season-2.md` (codex Part 7, The book).
  On long days, a one-line note in the day log after each big scene as a safety net. Update
  "Where we stopped" at the top of this sheet. Book up to date through day 33 ~1050 (Chapter 70). Part One of the book summarises sessions 1-5; Part Two starts the
  morning after the Deck 9 breach.
