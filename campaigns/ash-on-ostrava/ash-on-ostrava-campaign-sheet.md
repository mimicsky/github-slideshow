## The Light: companions

**The Light's creed** (Tamsin, session 7): care about the fight; know your weapon, make it yours and paint it; train hard; family looks after family; the container belongs to all of them; carry loot for the boss without complaint. **Uniform:** grey plate with white trim (edges, helmet stripe, belt), grey Navy coveralls under it; weapons personal. Everyone is a **Light-bringer**; tags are in the Full kit.

**Grunts (set by the player, session 9).** Not every recruit has a role or a specialty. A **grunt** starts with neither. When they find their thing in play (a moment, a drill, a talent that shows), they choose a focus and train it like a specialty from **+0** (+1 per 10 drill days, to +5), and take a role when they've earned it in the fiction.

**Specialties (set session 8, at the player's request).** Each member of the Light has a role specialty: **+2 on rolls of that kind**. Drilling the specialty as the day's training adds progress: **every 10 drill days, +1, to a max of +5**.

**Training plan (Tamsin, session 9; from warp day 15).** Reps by codex house rule 7 (revised end of session 9): **+0.1 per hour of training, +0.1 per action in play** (kills, successful rolls, hits stayed up through, orders, clever plays). A normal ship day on the plan = 6 hours of its drill (+0.6). The DM tallies at day's end.
