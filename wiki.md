## Part 1: Owned characters

### Black Legion (Chaos)
**Haarken** · base Epic · yours: Legendary 12,795
- Melee: Piercing ×4 · Move 4 · Traits: Let the Galaxy Burn (bonus hits), **Flying**, Terrifying (less melee dmg taken)
- **Active – Herald of the Apocalypse:** hits target + the enemy behind it, makes them take more damage, Battle Fatigue on nearby enemies.
- **Passive – Head-Claimer:** every kill = more damage; every *character* kill = +1 hit.
- **Take:** 🔥 Snowballs. Let him get the first kill every fight. He's why Chaos is your main team.

**Angrax** · base Common · yours: Rare 2,412
- Melee: Power ×4 · Move 2 · Traits: Let the Galaxy Burn, **Deep Strike**, Terminator Armour
- **Active – Bringer of Despair:** power hit on target + a random adjacent enemy, Battle Fatigue ×2.
- **Passive – Hateful Assault:** free power attack on an enemy that is **adjacent to him and moves away** (like a parting shot). Walking past him doesn't trigger it.
- **Take:** 🔥 Tank + pin. Get him adjacent to ranged/squishy enemies: they either stay in melee with him or eat a hit leaving. Correct #1 orb priority.

**Archimatos** · base Uncommon · yours: Rare 1,714
- Melee: Psychic ×2 · Ranged: Psychic ×1, range 2 · Move 3 · Trait: Psyker (splash to an adjacent enemy)
- **Active – Daemonic Incursion:** summons Bloodletters next to enemies that keep attacking.
- **Passive – Possession:** melee kill → Bloodletter; ranged kill → Blue Horror.
- **Take:** Extra bodies every fight. Psychic ignores armour. Great in Common-cap tournament.

**Forgefiend** · Machine of War · base Common
- **Primary – Hades Autocannons:** flame damage to summons + units within 2 hexes (costs munitions).
- **Secondary – Daemonic Ordnance:** sets hexes on fire; plasma to targets already burning.
- **Take:** Anti-summon. Worse than Biovore for you right now.
