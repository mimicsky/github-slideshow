# Tacticus Wiki – Alex's Roster

## Part 1: Owned characters

### Black Legion (Chaos)
**Haarken** · base Epic · yours: Legendary 12,795
- Melee: Piercing ×4 · Move 4 · Traits: Let the Galaxy Burn (bonus hits), **Flying**, Terrifying (less melee dmg taken)
- **Active – Herald of the Apocalypse:** hits target + the enemy behind it, makes them take more damage, Battle Fatigue on nearby enemies.
- **Passive – Head-Claimer:** every kill = more damage; every *character* kill = +1 hit.
- **Take:** 🔥 Snowballs. Let him get the first kill every fight. He's why Chaos is your main team.

**Angrax** · base Common · yours: Rare 2,412
- Melee: Power ×4 · Move 2 · Traits: Let the Galaxy Burn, **Deep Strike**, Terminator Armour
- **Active – Bringer of Despair:** power hit on target + a random adjacent enemy, Battle Fatigue ×2.
- **Passive – Hateful Assault:** free power attack on any enemy that walks away from him.
- **Take:** 🔥 Tank + zone lock. Deep Strike him next to the enemy backline. Correct #1 orb priority.

**Archimatos** · base Uncommon · yours: Rare 1,714
- Melee: Psychic ×2 · Ranged: Psychic ×1, range 2 · Move 3 · Trait: Psyker (splash to an adjacent enemy)
- **Active – Daemonic Incursion:** summons Bloodletters next to enemies that keep attacking.
- **Passive – Possession:** melee kill → Bloodletter; ranged kill → Blue Horror.
- **Take:** Extra bodies every fight. Psychic ignores armour. Great in Common-cap tournament.

**Forgefiend** · Machine of War · base Common
- **Primary – Hades Autocannons:** flame damage to summons + units within 2 hexes (costs munitions).
- **Secondary – Daemonic Ordnance:** sets hexes on fire; plasma to targets already burning.
- **Take:** Anti-summon. Worse than Biovore for you right now.

### Death Guard (Chaos)
**Maladus** · base Uncommon · yours: Epic 1,119
- Melee: Power ×3 · Move 2 · Traits: Contagions (enemies lose armour in aura), **Resilient** (survives at 1 HP), Terminator Armour
- **Active – Flail Swing:** power hit; a kill spreads corruption to adjacent enemies.
- **Passive – Haze of Corruption:** kills chain toxic damage to nearby enemies.
- **Take:** Slow but very hard to kill. Solid 4th slot.

**Corrodius** · base Rare · yours: Rare 1,173
- Melee: Plasma ×1 · Move 2 · Traits: Contagions, Resilient
- **Active – Poxwalkers:** summons plague zombies that attack right away.
- **Passive – Cursed Plague Bell:** allied Chaos units move farther; enemy psykers hurt themselves using abilities.
- **Take:** The movement buff is real value for slow Terminators. Good bench. Strong vs psyker-heavy enemies.

**Rotbone (Nauseous Rotbone)** · base Epic · yours: Epic 1,259
- Melee: Power ×4 · Move 2 · Traits: Contagions, Resilient, **Healer**
- **Active – Revitalising Malignancy:** heals self + adjacent allies, extra for Chaos. **Imperials get nothing.**
- **Passive – Tainted Narthecium:** chance to revive adjacent fallen Chaos allies.
- **Take:** 🔥 Your only Chaos healer AND a reviver. Keep him adjacent to Haarken/Angrax.

### Thousand Sons (Chaos)
**Toth** · base Uncommon · yours: Uncommon 556
- Melee: Power ×4 · Ranged: Flame ×5, range 2 · Move 2 · Traits: Weaver of Fates, Suppressive Fire, Terminator Armour
- **Active – Hellfyre Missile Rack:** big hit + bonus to everyone already hit by psychic damage.
- **Passive – Time Flux:** regen each turn; revives once at a "time ghost" spot.
- **Take:** Needs psychic teammates. Low power. Bench.

**Yazaghor** · base Common · yours: Uncommon 481
- Melee: Physical ×1 · Ranged: Heavy Round ×1, range 2 · Move 4 · Trait: Flying
- **Active – Sorcerous Facade:** boosts a friendly psyker's damage and swaps places with them.
- **Passive – Reality Unbound:** more damage per enemy hit by psychic effects.
- **Take:** Pure Thousand Sons support. Useless without a psyker team. Skip.

### World Eaters (Chaos)
**Macer** · base Uncommon · yours: Rare 829
- Melee: Chain ×3 · Move 3 · Traits: Blessings of Khorne (stronger per melee kill), Resilient
- **Active – Jakhal Stimms:** hits several adjacent enemies, costs his own HP.
- **Passive – Skullsmasher:** more damage the lower his HP; stuns targets after one kill.
- **Take:** Glass-cannon berserker. Fun, but too low power now.

**Wrask** · base Uncommon · yours: Uncommon 814
- Melee: Flame ×4 · Move 2 · Traits: Khorne, **Deep Strike**, Rapid Assault, Terminator Armour, Unstoppable
- **Active – Bloody Fury:** damage based on how often he's been hit; overflow spreads.
- **Passive – Wrathful Devotion:** on landing or a melee kill, gives **Shields to himself and adjacent Chaos allies**.
- **Take:** 🔥 Shields for your whole Chaos front line. That's why he's in the orb queue.

### Blood Angels (Imperial)
**Lucien** · base Uncommon · yours: Rare 6,420
- Melee: Physical ×4 · Ranged: Blast ×3, range 2 · Move 3 · Traits: Rapid Assault, Resilient, Unstoppable
- **Active – Black Rage:** drops to 30–50% HP, charges and hits hard, **then he's AI-controlled for the rest of the fight.**
- **Passive – Visions of Heresy:** free bolter shot after every attack; stronger at low HP.
- **Take:** 🔥 Your strongest Imperial. Save Black Rage for a finishing charge, because you lose control of him.

**Cezare** · base Uncommon · yours: Uncommon 636
- Melee: Power ×4 · Move 2 · Traits: Deep Strike, Terminator Armour, Rapid Assault
- **Active – Savage Echoes:** hits harder with more open hexes around him; self-heal at low HP.
- **Passive – Red Rampage:** after moving, hits ALL adjacent enemies.
- **Take:** Good kit, low power. Bench.

### Dark Angels (Imperial)
**Azrael** · base Epic · yours: Legendary 4,596
- Melee: Power ×3 · Ranged: Plasma ×1, range 2 · Move 3 · Traits: Final Vengeance, Overwatch
- **Active – Dark Talon Strike:** air strike on a target, suppresses enemies in a line.
- **Passive – Lion Helm:** nearby allies block more; Dark Angels get defense + counterattack damage.
- **Take:** Team-wide defense buff. Correct #1 Imperial upgrade. Gets better if you unlock Sarquael/Asmodai.

**Storm Speeder** · Machine of War · Dark Angels
- **Primary – Death on the Wind:** line strike; targets take more damage from Dark Angels that turn.
- **Secondary – Hailstrike:** boosts an ally's ranged/overwatch attacks and joins in.
- **Take:** Only good with a Dark Angels team. Biovore stays your MoW.

### Ultramarines (Imperial)
**Tigurius** · base Uncommon · yours: Rare 4,533
- Melee: Physical ×1 · Ranged: Psychic ×1, range 2 · Move 3 · Trait: Psyker
- **Active – Storm of Wrath:** psychic damage to target + up to 5 enemies within 2 hexes.
- **Passive – Psychic Fortress:** nearby allies block more and take less psychic damage.
- **Take:** Best AoE on your Imperial team. Psychic ignores armour.

**Certus** · base Common · yours: Epic 2,360
- Ranged: Bolter ×1, range 3 · Move 2 · Traits: Overwatch, Heavy Weapon (bonus if stationary), Infiltrate
- **Active – Mortis Round:** big sniper shot, range 3, knocks back. Double Heavy Weapon bonus.
- **Passive – Camo Cloak:** less damage from ranged attacks.
- **Take:** Sniper. **Don't move him before shooting.** Range 3 is his edge.

**Bellator** · base Common · yours: Rare 2,166
- Melee: Bolter ×6 · Move 4 · Traits: Flying, Mk X Gravis (armour counts twice)
- **Active – Death From Above:** summons Inceptors (more the later in the fight).
- **Passive – Shock Assault:** adjacent Inceptors join his attacks.
- **Take:** 🔥 Use his Active **late** (turn 3+) for more Inceptors.

**Incisus** · base Common · yours: Rare 1,779
- Melee: Piercing ×1 · Move 3 · Trait: Healer
- **Active – Combat Restoratives:** heals self + adjacent allies, extra for Imperials.
- **Passive – Narthecium:** revives one adjacent fallen ally per turn (not machines).
- **Take:** Imperial version of Rotbone. Swap him in if Imperial fights get rough.

**Galatian** · Machine of War · Ultramarines
- **Primary – Duty Eternal:** drops Galatian as a big stationary unit on the field.
- **Secondary – Macro Plasma Incinerator:** marks hexes; plasma to enemies ending there.
- **Take:** Area denial. Low priority.

### Black Templars (Imperial)

- **Passive – Champion of the Feast:** after moving, hits an adjacent enemy and **shreds its armour permanently**.

### Space Wolves (Imperial)

### Salamanders (Imperial)

### Sisterhood (Imperial)

**Exorcist** · Machine of War · Sisterhood

### Necrons (Xenos)

**Makhotep** · base Common · yours: Rare 1,968

**Aleph-Null** · base Uncommon · yours: Rare 1,368
